# Post-Exoneration Support Organizations

## After Innocence

### Basic Information
- **Name**: After Innocence
- **Type**: Post-Exoneration Support Services
- **Website**: [after-innocence.org](https://www.after-innocence.org/)
- **Founded**: 2016
- **Geographic Scope**: National (United States)

### Mission and Focus Areas
After Innocence provides free post-release assistance to people who have been released from prison after wrongful incarceration. Their mission is to bring reliable assistance to every person in America who has been wrongfully incarcerated, whether they've been out of prison for days or decades, and support them as they rebuild their lives.

### Services Offered
- **First Steps After Wrongful Imprisonment**: Help with obtaining photo ID, social security card, cell phone, stimulus payments, and building credit
- **Healthcare Navigation**: One-on-one "concierge" help from expert healthcare advocates to ensure clients see doctors or specialists, sort out coverage problems, and get the most out of available healthcare
- **Free Dental Services Project**: Coordination of free dental services for exonerees
- **Local Resource Connection**: Case managers identify local organizations offering job training, housing, adult education and help clients make the most of these programs

### Key Achievements
- Provided assistance to more than 800 exonerees nationwide since 2016
- Coordinated more than $1 million in donated dental care
- Secured $2.2 million in tax refunds and cancelled debt for clients
- 96% of clients surveyed say they're "highly likely" to recommend After Innocence to another exoneree

### Additional Information
- The vast majority of America's exonerees are released with little more than the clothes on their backs and left to fend for themselves
- Most exonerees do not receive consistent re-entry help or meaningful compensation for what they went through
- After Innocence provides trauma-informed case management to help exonerees navigate post-release challenges
- According to their statistics, 3,600+ people have been exonerated since 1989, representing 32,000+ years lost to wrongful imprisonment
- Every 57 hours, another innocent person is exonerated in the United States
