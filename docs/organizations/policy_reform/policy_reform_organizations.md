# Policy Reform Organizations

## Equal Justice Initiative (EJI)

### Basic Information
- **Name**: Equal Justice Initiative (EJI)
- **Type**: Policy Reform and Legal Advocacy Organization
- **Website**: [eji.org](https://eji.org/)
- **Geographic Scope**: National (United States)

### Mission and Focus Areas
The Equal Justice Initiative works to end mass incarceration and excessive punishment, challenge racial and economic injustice, and protect basic human rights for the most vulnerable people in American society. They have a significant focus on wrongful convictions as part of their broader criminal justice reform work.

### Key Statistics on Wrongful Convictions
- 3,175+ exonerations since 1989 according to the National Registry of Exonerations
- 375 people exonerated through DNA evidence since 1989
- 27,200+ years exonerated people spent in prison for crimes they did not commit
- Only 44% of the 95 conviction integrity units in the U.S. have recorded exonerations

### Causes of Wrongful Convictions Highlighted by EJI
- False testimony (more than half of wrongful convictions can be traced to witnesses who lied in court)
- Misconduct by government officials
- Mistaken eyewitness identifications
- False or misleading forensic science ("junk science" techniques)
- Jailhouse informants
- Inadequate legal representation
- Racial bias (African Americans make up 47% of exonerations despite being only 13% of the population)

### Notable Cases
- Anthony Ray Hinton: Spent 30 years on Alabama's death row after being wrongfully convicted based on faulty bullet match evidence
- Beniah Dandridge: Spent 20 years in prison after being wrongfully convicted based on an erroneous fingerprint match
- Diane Tucker: A woman with intellectual disability who was wrongfully convicted of murdering an infant that evidence later proved never existed

### Source
- [Equal Justice Initiative - Wrongful Convictions](https://eji.org/issues/wrongful-convictions/)

## Innocence Project (Policy Reform Work)

### Basic Information
- **Name**: Innocence Project (Policy Reform Division)
- **Type**: Policy Reform Organization
- **Website**: [innocenceproject.org/transforming-systems](https://innocenceproject.org/transforming-systems/)

### Mission and Focus Areas
In addition to their direct legal work, the Innocence Project has a significant policy reform division that works to transform the criminal justice system by tackling systemic flaws that lead to wrongful convictions.

### Policy Reform Initiatives
- Advocating for legislation to prevent wrongful convictions
- Working to eliminate failings in the criminal justice system
- Addressing issues that disproportionately impact marginalized communities

### Source
- [Innocence Project - Transforming Systems](https://innocenceproject.org/transforming-systems/)
