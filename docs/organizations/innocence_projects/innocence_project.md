# Innocence Project

## Organization Profile

### Basic Information
- **Name**: Innocence Project
- **Founded**: 1992
- **Founders**: Peter Neufeld and Barry Scheck
- **Website**: [innocenceproject.org](https://innocenceproject.org/)
- **Type**: Legal Advocacy Organization

### Mission and Focus Areas
The Innocence Project works to free the innocent, prevent wrongful convictions, and create fair, compassionate, and equitable systems of justice for everyone. Their mission encompasses three main areas:
1. Exonerating wrongfully convicted individuals
2. Preventing future wrongful convictions through policy reform
3. Creating fair and equitable justice systems

### Geographic Scope
National organization based in the United States, with cases across the country.

### Services Offered
- Legal representation for wrongfully convicted individuals
- DNA testing to prove innocence
- Policy advocacy for criminal justice reform
- Public education about wrongful convictions
- Support for exonerees post-release

### Key Achievements
- Helped free or exonerate more than 250 innocent people from prison
- Clients collectively spent more than 3,700 years wrongfully incarcerated
- Passed transformative state laws and federal reforms
- Advanced the innocence movement nationally and internationally

### Organizational Structure
The Innocence Project is a community of empathetic, open-minded, and creative individuals who are passionate about driving necessary change in the criminal justice system.

### Notable Cases
The organization has worked on hundreds of cases, including:
- Ian Schweitzer

- Termaine Hicks

### Additional Notes
- The Innocence Project uses DNA and other scientific advancements to prove wrongful conviction
