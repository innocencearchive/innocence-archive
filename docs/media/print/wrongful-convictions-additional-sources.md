# Additional Academic Sources on Wrongful Convictions

This document contains a list of additional academic sources on wrongful convictions, organized by type.

## Books

1. **The Court of Last Resort** - Erle Stanley Gardner  
   *(1952)*

2. **Not Guilty** - Barbara and Jerome Frank  
   *(1957)*

3. **The Innocents** - Edward Radin  
   *(1964)*

4. **The Death Penalty in America** - Hugo Bedau  
   *Chapter: Murder, Errors of Justice, and Capital Punishment (1964) at 434*

5. **Convicting the Innocent** - Brandon Garrett  
   *(2011)*

6. **Predicting Erroneous Convictions: A Social Science Approach to Miscarriages of Justice** - Jon B. Gould, Julia Carrano, Richard A. Leo, and Joseph Young  
   *(2013) Available at: https://www.ncjrs.gov/pdffiles1/nij/grants/241389.pdf*

7. **The Innocence Commission: Preventing Wrongful Convictions and Restoring the Criminal Justice System** - Jon B. Gould  
   *(2007)*

## Academic Journal Articles

1. **Miscarriages of Justice in Potentially Capital Cases** - Hugo Bedau & Michael Radelet  
   *Stanford Law Review, Vol. 40, pp. 21 (1987)*

2. **An Integrated Justice Model of Wrongful Convictions** - Marvin Zalman  
   *Albany Law Review, Vol. 74, pp. 1465 (2010/2011)*

3. **Exonerations in the United States, 1989 Through 2003** - Samuel Gross et al.  
   *Journal of Criminal Law & Criminology, Vol. 95, pp. 523 (2005) at 551*

4. **Professional and Conviction Integrity Programs: Why We Need Them, Why They Will Work, and Models for Creating Them** - Barry Scheck  
   *Cardozo Law Review, Vol. 31, pp. 2215 (2010)*

5. **The Role of Innocence Commissions: Error Discovery, Systemic Reform or Both?** - Kent Roach  
   *Chicago-Kent Law Review, Vol. 85, pp. 89 (2010) at 119*

6. **Innocence Commissions and the Future of Post-Conviction Review** - David Wolitz  
   *Arizona Law Review, Vol. 52, pp. 1027, 1033 (2010)*

7. **Judging Innocence** - Brandon L. Garrett  
   *Columbia Law Review, Vol. 108, pp. 55 (2008)*

8. **Frequency and Predictors of False Convictions: Why We Know So Little, and New Data on Capital Cases** - Samuel R. Gross & Barbara O'Brien  
   *Journal of Empirical Legal Studies, Vol. 5, pp. 927 (2008)*

9. **One Hundred Years Later: Wrongful Convictions After a Century of Research** - Jon B. Gould & Richard A. Leo  
   *Journal of Criminal Law & Criminology, Vol. 100, pp. 825, 832-33 (2010)*

10. **Preventing Wrongful Convictions** - Jon B. Gould, Julia Carrano, Richard A. Leo, and Katherine Hail-Jares  
    *Iowa Law Review, Vol. 99, pp. 471 (2014)*

11. **Predictors of Miscarriages of Justice in Capital Cases** - Talia Roitberg Harmon  
    *Justice Quarterly, Vol. 18, pp. 949 (2001)*

12. **Too Late for Luck: A Comparison of Post-Furman Exonerations and Executions of the Innocent** - Talia Roitberg Harmon and William S. Loftquist  
    *Crime & Delinquency, Vol. 51, pp. 498 (2005)*

13. **Finality in Criminal Law and Federal Habeas Corpus for State Prisoners** - Paul M. Bator  
    *Harvard Law Review, Vol. 76, pp. 441 (1963)*

14. **Crime, Punishment, and Politics: An Analysis of Political Cycles in Criminal Sentencing** - Carlos Berdejó and Noam Yuchtman  
    *Review of Economics & Statistics, Vol. 95, pp. 741 (2013)*
