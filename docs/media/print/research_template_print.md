#   Print Media Research: Wrongful Conviction Media Sources

##   Research Template for Each Article

For each magazine or newspaper article on wrongful convictions, we'll collect the following information:

###   Basic Article Information

-   **Title**: Full title of the article
-   **Author(s)**: All authors
-   **Publication**: Name of the magazine or newspaper
-   **Date of Publication**: Full date (e.g., January 15, 2024)
-   **Section/Page Numbers (if applicable)**: Section of the publication and page numbers where the article appears
-   **Article Type**:
    -   News Article
    -   Feature Article
    -   Investigative Report
    -   Opinion Piece
-   **Format**: Print, Online
-   **Focus/Theme**: Main topics covered (e.g., eyewitness misidentification, legal reform)
-   **Key Statistics/Information**: Notable statistics or key information presented

###   Wrongfully Convicted Individuals Featured

For each individual mentioned or featured:

-   **Name**: Full name of the wrongfully convicted person
-   **Case Location**: City, state, country
-   **Conviction Year**: When they were convicted
-   **Exoneration Year**: When they were exonerated (if applicable)
-   **Crime Convicted Of**: What they were wrongfully convicted of
-   **Sentence**: Original sentence received
-   **Time Served**: How long they were incarcerated
-   **Contributing Factors**: Factors that led to wrongful conviction (false confession, eyewitness misidentification, etc.)
-   **Legal Status**: Current legal status (exonerated, released, pardoned, etc.)
-   **Organizations Involved**: Any organizations that assisted with their case
-   **Cross-Reference**: Whether they appear in the exoneree database

###   Article Research Notes

-   **Key Quotes/Passages**: Important quotes or significant passages in the article
-   **Systemic Issues Identified**: Broader issues in the justice system discussed or highlighted
-   **Policy Recommendations/Calls to Action**: Any suggested reforms, policy changes, or calls to action presented
-   **Additional Resources**: Other sources mentioned that could be valuable

##   Research Strategy

1.  Begin with articles that focus on multiple cases to maximize data collection
2.  Prioritize recent publications for current information and perspectives
3.  Cross-reference individuals with the exoneree database
4.  Document specific details (page numbers, URLs) for easy verification

##   Integration Plan

-   Create a structured CSV file for each article with all individuals mentioned/featured
-   Develop a master cross-reference spreadsheet linking exonerees to article mentions
-   Prepare database schema updates if needed to accommodate article references
