#   Academic Journal Research: Wrongful Conviction Sources

##   Research Template for Each Article

For each academic journal article on wrongful convictions, we'll collect the following information:

###   Basic Article Information

-   **Title**: Full title of the article
-   **Author(s)**: All authors
-   **Journal Title**: Full name of the academic journal
-   **Publication Date**: Full date or season/year (e.g., January 15, 2024, or Fall 2023)
-   **Volume and Issue Number**: Journal volume and issue number
-   **Page Numbers**: Page range where the article appears
-   **DOI (if available)**: Digital Object Identifier
-   **Article Type**:
    -   Research Article
    -   Review Article
    -   Case Study
    -   Legal Analysis
    -   Commentary
-   **Format**: Print, Online
-   **Focus/Theme**: Main topics covered (e.g., cognitive psychology of eyewitness identification, legal reforms, sociological factors in wrongful convictions)
-   **Key Statistics/Information**: Notable statistics or key information presented

###   Wrongfully Convicted Individuals Featured (if applicable)

For each individual mentioned or featured:

-   **Name**: Full name of the wrongfully convicted person
-   **Case Location**: City, state, country
-   **Conviction Year**: When they were convicted
-   **Exoneration Year**: When they were exonerated (if applicable)
-   **Crime Convicted Of**: What they were wrongfully convicted of
-   **Sentence**: Original sentence received
-   **Time Served**: How long they were incarcerated
-   **Contributing Factors**: Factors that led to wrongful conviction (false confession, eyewitness misidentification, etc.)
-   **Legal Status**: Current legal status (exonerated, released, pardoned, etc.)
-   **Organizations Involved**: Any organizations that assisted with their case
-   **Cross-Reference**: Whether they appear in the exoneree database

###   Article Research Notes

-   **Key Findings/Arguments**: Important findings, arguments, or conclusions presented in the article
-   **Methodology (if applicable)**: Research methods used in the study
-   **Systemic Issues Analyzed**: Broader issues in the justice system discussed or analyzed
-   **Policy Implications/Recommendations**: Any implications for policy or recommendations for reform discussed
-   **Limitations of the Study (if noted)**: Any limitations of the research acknowledged by the authors
-   **Additional Resources/References**: Other sources cited that could be valuable

##   Research Strategy

1.  Prioritize articles in reputable academic journals
2.  Focus on recent publications for current research and perspectives
3.  Use keywords to search within academic databases (e.g., JSTOR, HeinOnline, Google Scholar)
4.  Cross-reference individuals with the exoneree database
5.  Document complete citation information for accurate referencing
