#   Podcast Research: Broken Justice

##   Basic Podcast Information

-   **Title**: Broken Justice
-   **Host(s)**: Produced by PBS NewsHour team including Amna Nawaz, Frank Carlson, Vika Aronson, Emily Carpeaux, and Erica R. Hendry
-   **Release Year(s)**: 2019
-   **Production Company(ies)/Network**: PBS NewsHour
-   **Series Type**:
    -   Limited Series (5 episodes + special episode)
-   **Format**: Podcast
-   **Focus/Theme**: Examines the crisis in Missouri's public defender system through the case of Ricky Kidd, who was wrongfully convicted of a double homicide and sentenced to life without parole
-   **Key Statistics/Information**: 
    - Focuses on the systemic issues in the U.S. public defender system
    - Highlights how overworked, underfunded public defenders can lead to wrongful convictions
    - Ricky Kidd spent 23 years in prison before being exonerated

##   Podcast Research Notes

-   **Key Quotes/Moments**: "In 1997, Ricky Kidd was sentenced to life without parole for a double homicide he says he didn't commit. And, he argues, his court-appointed lawyer is the reason."

-   **Systemic Issues Identified**: 
    - Overworked and underfunded public defenders
    - Crisis in the public defender system, particularly in Missouri
    - Inadequate legal representation for those who cannot afford private attorneys
    - Flaws in the U.S. justice system that can lead to wrongful convictions

-   **Policy Recommendations/Calls to Action**: 
    - The podcast examines a St. Louis County prosecutor's campaign to uproot the process that fuels wrongful convictions
    - Highlights the need for reform in the public defender system
    - Advocates for better funding and resources for public defenders
