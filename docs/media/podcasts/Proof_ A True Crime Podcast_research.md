#   Podcast Research: Proof: A True Crime Podcast

##   Basic Podcast Information

-   **Title**: Proof: A True Crime Podcast
-   **Host(s)**: Susan Simpson and Jacinda Davis
-   **Release Year(s)**: 2022-present
-   **Production Company(ies)/Network**: Produced by Susan Simpson of Undisclosed and the producers of Evil Lives Here
-   **Series Type**:
    -   Serialized investigation (case-per-season format)
-   **Format**: Investigative podcast examining potential wrongful convictions
-   **Focus/Theme**: In-depth investigation of cases of potential wrongful conviction
-   **Key Statistics/Information**: 
    - Season 1 directly contributed to the exoneration of Lee Clark and Joshua Cain Storey in December 2022
    - The podcast's investigation uncovered new evidence that led to their convictions being overturned
    - Currently in Season 2, titled "Murder at the Warehouse"

##   Episode List

### Season 1: Russian Roulette
- **Description**: Investigates the case of Lee Clark and Joshua Cain Storey, who were wrongfully convicted of murdering 15-year-old Brian Bowling in 1996

### Season 1 Update: Truth Will Reveal Itself
- **Description**: Follow-up episode after Lee Clark and Joshua Storey were exonerated on December 8, 2022, discussing their adjustment to freedom 15 months after release

### Season 2: Murder at the Warehouse
- **Description**: Current season investigating another potential wrongful conviction case

##   Wrongfully Convicted Individuals Featured

### Lee Clark and Joshua Cain Storey
-   **Name**: Lee Clark and Joshua Cain Storey
-   **Age at Time of Conviction**: 17 years old
-   **Conviction Year**: 1997
-   **Crime Convicted Of**: Murder of Brian Bowling
-   **Sentence**: 25+ years in prison
-   **Years Served Before Exoneration**: 25 years
-   **Exoneration Year**: 2022
-   **Contributing Factors**: 
    - Official misconduct
    - Newly discovered evidence uncovered by the podcast
-   **Additional Information**: 
    - The podcast investigation found evidence that called the State's case into question
    - A Floyd County Prosecutor and Judge agreed their convictions should be overturned
    - Both men were released on December 8, 2022

##   Podcast Research Notes

-   **Key Quotes/Moments**: "On December 8, 2022, after serving 25 years in prison for a crime they did not commit, Lee Clark and Cain Joshua Storey were exonerated. Newly discovered evidence found in season one of Proof called the State's case into question, and a Floyd County Prosecutor and Judge agreed their convictions should be overturned."

-   **Systemic Issues Identified**: 
    - Official misconduct (noted as a contributing factor in over 50% of wrongful conviction cases)
    - Flawed investigation procedures
    - Issues with witness testimony

-   **Policy Recommendations/Calls to Action**: 
    - The podcast implicitly advocates for the review of potentially wrongful convictions
    - Highlights the importance of re-examining evidence in old cases
