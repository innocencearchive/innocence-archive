#   Podcast Research: Serial (Season 1)

##   Basic Podcast Information

-   **Title**: Serial (Season 1)
-   **Host(s)**: Sarah Koenig
-   **Release Year(s)**: 2014
-   **Production Company(ies)/Network**: Serial Productions (spinoff of "This American Life")
-   **Series Type**:
    -   Limited Series (12 episodes plus updates)
-   **Format**: Investigative podcast
-   **Focus/Theme**: Reinvestigation of a 1999 murder case in Baltimore, examining whether Adnan Syed was wrongfully convicted of killing his ex-girlfriend Hae Min Lee
-   **Key Statistics/Information**: 
    - Launched in 2014 as a spinoff of "This American Life"
    - Became a cultural phenomenon and set a new standard for investigative audio reporting
    - Won numerous awards including the first-ever Peabody Award given to a podcast
    - Adnan Syed was freed from prison eight years after the podcast aired

##   Wrongfully Convicted Individuals Featured

### Adnan Syed
-   **Name**: Adnan Syed
-   **Age at Time of Conviction**: 17
-   **Conviction Year**: 2000
-   **Crime Convicted Of**: First-degree murder of Hae Min Lee
-   **Sentence**: Life in prison
-   **Years Served Before Exoneration**: 23 years
-   **Exoneration Year**: 2022
-   **Contributing Factors**: 
    - Case largely based on one witness's testimony (Jay)
    - Cell phone evidence later questioned by the expert who originally testified
    - Lack of physical evidence connecting Syed to the crime
    - Potential alibi witness not called to testify
    - Brady violations (evidence not disclosed to defense)
-   **Legal Status**: Conviction vacated in 2022, charges dropped
-   **Additional Information**: 
    - In 2019, Maryland's highest court reversed a decision to give Adnan a new trial
    - In 2022, prosecutors said he had been wrongfully convicted and new DNA evidence showed he was not involved in the death

##   Podcast Research Notes

-   **Key Quotes/Moments**: "The case against him was largely based on the story of one witness, Adnan's friend Jay, who testified that he helped Adnan bury Hae's body. But Adnan has always maintained he had nothing to do with Hae's death."

-   **Systemic Issues Identified**: 
    - Reliance on single witness testimony without corroborating evidence
    - Failure to investigate potential alibi witnesses
    - Questionable cell phone location evidence
    - Potential racial and religious bias (Adnan is Muslim of Pakistani descent)
    - Brady violations (withholding evidence from defense)

-   **Policy Recommendations/Calls to Action**: 
    - The podcast itself doesn't explicitly make policy recommendations but raises questions about the criminal justice system
    - Highlights the importance of thorough investigation of all potential witnesses
    - Questions the reliability of cell phone location data as evidence

-   **Additional Resources**: 
    - Updates on the case posted on the Serial website
    - HBO documentary series "The Case Against Adnan Syed" (2019)
    - Court documents and legal filings referenced throughout the podcast
