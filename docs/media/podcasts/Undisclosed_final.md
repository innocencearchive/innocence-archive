#   Podcast Research: Undisclosed

##   Basic Podcast Information

-   **Title**: Undisclosed
-   **Host(s)**: Rabia Chaudry, Susan Simpson, Colin Miller
-   **Release Year(s)**: 2015-2022 (Original run), 2025 (Relaunch)
-   **Production Company(ies)/Network**: Undisclosed, LLC / Audioboom
-   **Series Type**:
    -   Ongoing Series with 24 seasons and approximately 450 episodes
-   **Format**: Podcast
-   **Focus/Theme**: Investigates wrongful convictions and the U.S. criminal justice system by examining the perpetration of crimes, investigations, trials, and verdicts, with a focus on finding new evidence that never made it to court.
-   **Key Statistics/Information**: 
    - 24 seasons covering different wrongful conviction cases
    - Approximately 450 episodes in total
    - 4.2/5 rating on Apple Podcasts (9.6K ratings)
    - Released final episode of original run in March 2022
    - Relaunched in February 2025

### Wrongfully Convicted Individuals Featured

#### Adnan Syed

-   **Name**: Adnan Syed
-   **Case Location**: Baltimore, Maryland, USA
-   **Conviction Year**: 2000
-   **Exoneration Year**: 2022
-   **Crime Convicted Of**: First-degree murder of Hae Min Lee (his ex-girlfriend)
-   **Sentence**: Life plus 30 years
-   **Time Served**: Approximately 23 years
-   **Contributing Factors**: 
    - Unreliable witness testimony
    - Cell phone location data that was presented without proper context
    - Ineffective assistance of counsel
    - Brady violations (evidence not disclosed to defense)
-   **Legal Status**: Exonerated; conviction vacated in September 2022
-   **Organizations Involved**: 
    - Innocence Project
    - University of Baltimore Innocence Project Clinic
-   **Cross-Reference**: Yes, appears in exoneree database

##   Podcast Research Notes

-   **Key Quotes/Moments**: The podcast's tagline is "The Undisclosed podcast investigates wrongful convictions, and the U.S. criminal justice system, by taking a closer look at the perpetration of a crime, its investigation, the trial, and ultimate verdict... and finding new evidence that never made it to court."

-   **Systemic Issues Identified**: 
    - Problems with reliability of cell phone location data
    - Issues with witness testimony and police interview techniques
    - Prosecutorial misconduct
    - Inadequate legal representation
    - Flawed forensic evidence
    - Eyewitness misidentification
    - False confessions

-   **Policy Recommendations/Calls to Action**: 
    - The podcast often discusses reforms needed in the criminal justice system
    - Has been involved in advocacy for the exoneration of the individuals featured in their episodes
    - For Adnan Syed specifically, the podcast includes links to support his reentry into society:
      - www.patreon.com/freeadnan
      - www.causeteam.com/causepages/detail/we-freed-adnan
      - https://www.launchgood.com/campaign/help_adnan_syed_restart_his_life_2
    - Uses hashtag #wefreedadnan to promote awareness

-   **Additional Resources**: 
    - The podcast has a website: undisclosed-podcast.com
    - Related podcasts by the hosts include "The Mystery Hour with Rabia Chaudry" and "Proof: A True Crime Podcast" by Susan Simpson
    - The podcast "Serial" (Season 1) which initially brought attention to Adnan Syed's case
    - HBO documentary "The Case Against Adnan Syed"
