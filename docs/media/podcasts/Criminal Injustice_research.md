#   Podcast Research: Criminal Injustice

##   Basic Podcast Information

-   **Title**: Criminal Injustice
-   **Host(s)**: Professor David Harris
-   **Release Year(s)**: 2015-2022
-   **Production Company(ies)/Network**: Initially produced with NPR station WESA in Pittsburgh, later became independent
-   **Series Type**:
    -   Weekly series (150+ episodes over 6 years)
-   **Format**: Interview-based podcast
-   **Focus/Theme**: Explores issues in the U.S. criminal justice system, including wrongful convictions, police reform, racial bias, and mass incarceration
-   **Key Statistics/Information**: 
    - Aired its final episode in April 2022 after six years
    - Conducted over 150 interviews with experts, advocates, and authorities
    - Began in response to the 2014 killing of Michael Brown in Ferguson, Missouri

##   Episode List

### Episode 151: The DNA Earthquake
- **Description**: Examines how DNA evidence has transformed the criminal justice system by uncovering wrongful convictions

### Episode 146: Public Defense and Criminal Justice Reform
- **Description**: Discusses the role of public defense systems in criminal justice reform and preventing wrongful convictions

### Episode 145: To Blame or to Learn? Sentinel Event Reviews of Police Action
- **Description**: Explores how focusing on blame versus learning affects the ability to prevent future errors in the criminal justice system

### Episode 140: Flawed Forensics
- **Description**: Examines the reliability of forensic science in courtrooms and its potential contribution to wrongful convictions

### Episode 134: Re-entry After Exoneration
- **Description**: Discusses the challenges faced by exonerees when re-entering society after wrongful conviction

##   Wrongfully Convicted Individuals Featured

The podcast does not focus on specific wrongfully convicted individuals in the same way as other podcasts dedicated solely to wrongful convictions. Instead, it takes a broader approach to criminal justice issues, with wrongful convictions being one of many topics covered.

##   Podcast Research Notes

-   **Key Quotes/Moments**: "On Criminal Injustice, we've examined a host of changes and reforms that have altered the criminal justice landscape. But nothing – nothing – can match the change brought to every aspect of the system by the use of DNA to uncover wrongful convictions." (Episode 151 description)

-   **Systemic Issues Identified**: 
    - Flawed forensic science contributing to wrongful convictions
    - Inadequate public defense systems
    - Racial bias in the criminal justice system
    - Mass incarceration
    - Police misconduct and use of force
    - Challenges of re-entry after incarceration or exoneration

-   **Policy Recommendations/Calls to Action**: 
    - Reform of public defense systems
    - Improved forensic science standards
    - Alternatives to aggressive policing
    - Sentinel event reviews to learn from mistakes rather than just assigning blame
    - Support systems for those re-entering society after incarceration or exoneration

-   **Additional Resources**: 
    - Full catalog of episodes available on the podcast website
    - Host David Harris is a law professor at the University of Pittsburgh
    - The podcast was inspired by the renewed sense of urgency surrounding criminal justice reform following the 2014 killing of Michael Brown
