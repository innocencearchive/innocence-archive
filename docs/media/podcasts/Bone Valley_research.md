#   Podcast Research: Bone Valley

##   Basic Podcast Information

-   **Title**: Bone Valley
-   **Host(s)**: Gilbert King
-   **Release Year(s)**: 2022
-   **Production Company(ies)/Network**: Lava for Good Podcasts in association with Signal Co. No1
-   **Series Type**:
    -   Limited Series/Miniseries
-   **Format**: Podcast
-   **Focus/Theme**: Investigative journalism examining the wrongful conviction of Leo Schofield for the murder of his wife Michelle, with evidence pointing to another suspect who has confessed to the crime
-   **Key Statistics/Information**: 
    - Created by Pulitzer Prize-winning author Gilbert King
    - Three-year investigation into Leo Schofield's case
    - Reveals new evidence about the actual perpetrator, Jeremy Scott
    - Leo Schofield remains in prison despite another man's confession

##   Wrongfully Convicted Individual Featured

### Leo Schofield

-   **Name**: Leo Schofield
-   **Case Location**: Lakeland, Florida
-   **Conviction Year**: 1989 (Two years after the murder)
-   **Exoneration Year**: Not yet exonerated (still in prison as of 2024)
-   **Crime Convicted Of**: Murder of his 18-year-old wife Michelle Schofield
-   **Sentence**: Life in prison
-   **Time Served**: Over 35 years (as of 2024)
-   **Contributing Factors**: 
    - Circumstantial evidence
    - Lack of forensic evidence connecting him to the crime
    - Unidentified fingerprints not properly investigated at the time
    - Tunnel vision by investigators focusing solely on Leo
-   **Legal Status**: Still incarcerated despite new evidence
-   **Organizations Involved**: 
    - Lava for Good (advocacy through podcast)
-   **Cross-Reference**: Not yet exonerated

##   Podcast Research Notes

-   **Key Quotes/Moments**: "In 1987, 21-year-old guitarist Leo Schofield was pursuing his rockstar dreams when his 18-year-old wife Michelle was found dead in a phosphate mining pit in Lakeland, Florida. Two years later, Leo was convicted and sentenced to life in prison for the murder—a crime he has always denied."

-   **Systemic Issues Identified**: 
    - Fingerprint evidence not properly investigated
    - Tunnel vision by investigators
    - Difficulty in overturning convictions despite new evidence
    - Reluctance of the system to admit mistakes
    - Fifteen years into his sentence, Leo learned that previously unidentified fingerprints from Michelle's car pointed to a new suspect: Jeremy Scott

-   **Policy Recommendations/Calls to Action**: 
    - The podcast exposes "the fundamental and catastrophic flaws in the American criminal justice system"
    - Advocates for the power of investigative journalism in correcting wrongful convictions
    - Highlights the need for continued review of evidence in cases where new suspects emerge

-   **Additional Resources**: 
    - Gilbert King's Pulitzer Prize-winning book "Devil in the Grove" which led to exonerations in another Florida case
    - Updates on Leo Schofield's parole hearings and legal status
