# Podcast Research: The Innocence Podcast

## Basic Podcast Information

- **Title**: The Innocence Podcast
- **Host(s)**: Kylie Pentelow (Presenter/Researcher)
- **Release Year(s)**: 2022-present
- **Production Company(ies)/Network**: The Manchester Innocence Project
- **Series Type**: Episodic, focusing on different cases of wrongful conviction
- **Format**: Interview-based podcast featuring conversations with exonerees, lawyers, and experts
- **Focus/Theme**: Explores cases of wrongful conviction, the work of innocence projects, and systemic issues in the criminal justice system
- **Key Statistics/Information**: 
  - Executive Producer: Claire McGourlay
  - Producer: Lucy McDaid
  - Editor: Kris McConnachie
  - Features criminal appeal lawyer Mark Newby who works on cases of potentially wrongfully convicted individuals

## Episode List

### 24: The Falsely Accused Football Star
- **Description**: Features Brian Banks, a high school football star falsely accused of rape
- **Key Information**: Banks spent over five years in prison and served five years on parole until his accuser was secretly recorded admitting the truth; his story was later turned into a Hollywood movie

### 23: The Godfather of Innocence
- **Description**: Features Barry Scheck, co-founder of The Innocence Project
- **Key Information**: Discusses how Scheck changed the way DNA is used to prosecute and prove innocence; he was part of OJ Simpson's winning defense team

### 22: Art Not Evidence
- **Description**: Explores how creative expression is used as evidence in criminal trials
- **Key Information**: Features Kier Monteith KC and Elli Brazill discussing the alarming increase in rap music being used to convict people

### 21: The Smells of Freedom
- **Description**: Features Dean Gillispie who served 20 years in prison for rape and kidnap - crimes he didn't commit
- **Key Information**: Mark Godsey at the Ohio Innocence Project helped free him; Gillispie was recently awarded $45 million in compensation

### 20: Joint Enterprise
- **Description**: Features Dr. Felicity Gerry discussing joint enterprise law
- **Key Information**: Explores how people can be wrongly convicted for crimes committed by others; Dr. Gerry persuaded the UK Supreme Court to change the law, but thousands remain in prison for murders they were not involved in

### 19: Another Level of Ridiculousness
- **Description**: Features Kimberley Long, a nurse wrongfully convicted of murdering her boyfriend
- **Key Information**: The Innocence Project's Justin Brooks proved her innocence by showing there was no blood splatter on her clothing and the victim's time of death didn't match up to where Kim was

- **Age at Time of Conviction**: 27

## Podcast Research Notes

- **Key Quotes/Moments**: "Every week Mark Newby gets letters from people desperate for his help. Those people are behind bars, they say they are innocent and have no way of proving it."

- **Systemic Issues Identified**: 
  - Joint enterprise laws that can convict people for crimes committed by others
  - Use of creative expression (like rap music) as evidence in criminal trials
  - Lack of support for exonerees after release
  - Minimal compensation for the wrongfully convicted
  - Difficulty in returning to previous careers after exoneration

- **Policy Recommendations/Calls to Action**: 
  - Reform of joint enterprise laws
  - Stopping the use of creative and artistic expression as evidence in criminal trials
  - Better support systems for exonerees
  - Improved compensation for the wrongfully convicted
