#   Podcast Research: Unjust & Unsolved

##   Basic Podcast Information

-   **Title**: Unjust & Unsolved
-   **Host(s)**: Maggie Freleng
-   **Release Year(s)**: 2020-2021
-   **Production Company(ies)/Network**: Daisy/Obsessed Network
-   **Series Type**:
    -   Limited Series (22 episodes)
-   **Format**: Investigative podcast
-   **Focus/Theme**: Examines cases of potentially wrongfully convicted individuals whose cases remain unsolved
-   **Key Statistics/Information**: 
    - The Innocence Project estimates that there are currently over 20,000 innocent people locked away in US prisons
    - Each week, Investigative Journalist Maggie Freleng tells the story of one of those people and takes a deep dive into the crime they were convicted of

##   Episode List

### Episode 22: Cheri Hayden
- **Description**: Explores the case of Cheri Hayden, a 45-year-old woman convicted of a botched robbery that left 68-year-old Patricia Landry dead, despite witnesses describing the perpetrator as a blonde woman in her 20s

### Episode 21: Brandon Spencer
- **Description**: Examines the case of 19-year-old Brandon Spencer who was sentenced to 40 years to life for a shooting at USC despite having a strong alibi and the star witness later recanting

### Episode 20: Sara Bennett
- **Description**: Interview with former appellate attorney and author Sara Bennett who captures portraits of women serving life sentences in prison

### Episode 19: Andre Brown
- **Description**: Investigates the case of college student Andre Brown who was convicted in the Bronx, New York during the 1990s amid a drug turf war

### Episode 18: Ralph Trent Stokes
- **Description**: Covers the case of 19-year-old Ralph Trent Stokes who was convicted of three counts of first-degree murder during a restaurant robbery in Philadelphia in 1982 and sentenced to death

### Episode 17: The Prosecutors
- **Description**: Interview with Brett and Alice, real-life prosecutors and co-hosts of "The Prosecutors" podcast, discussing Darrell Ewing's case from Episode 8

##   Wrongfully Convicted Individuals Featured

### Cheri Hayden
-   **Name**: Cheri Hayden
-   **Crime Convicted Of**: Murder of Patricia Landry during a botched robbery
-   **Contributing Factors**: 
    - Eyewitness misidentification
    - Inadequate investigation
-   **Additional Information**: 
    - Witnesses described the perpetrator as a blonde woman in her 20s, while Hayden was 45 with deep facial wrinkles

### Brandon Spencer
-   **Name**: Brandon Spencer
-   **Age at Time of Conviction**: 19
-   **Crime Convicted Of**: Four counts of attempted murder
-   **Sentence**: 40 years to life
-   **Contributing Factors**: 
    - Star witness later recanted
    - No physical evidence linking him to the crime
-   **Additional Information**: 
    - Was preparing to enter an EMT program before his conviction
    - Had family, friends, police, and even the former mayor of Inglewood vouching for him

### Ralph Trent Stokes
-   **Name**: Ralph Trent Stokes
-   **Age at Time of Conviction**: 19
-   **Conviction Year**: 1982
-   **Crime Convicted Of**: Three counts of first-degree murder
-   **Sentence**: Death penalty
-   **Contributing Factors**: 
    - Prosecutorial misconduct
    - Fabricated physical evidence
    - Unreliable witness testimony
-   **Additional Information**: 
    - Prosecutor Roger King held the record for most death sentences achieved in Pennsylvania
    - King was infamous for prosecutorial misconduct and having multiple overturned convictions

### Andre Brown
-   **Name**: Andre Brown
-   **Crime Convicted Of**: Not specified in available information
-   **Contributing Factors**: 
    - Convicted during the most violent decade in New York City's history
    - Case involved a corrupt mob lawyer for the Bonanno crime family

##   Podcast Research Notes

-   **Key Quotes/Moments**: "The Innocence Project estimates that there are currently over 20,000 innocent people locked away in US prisons. These cases remain unsolved. Each week, Investigative Journalist Maggie Freleng tells the story of one of those people and takes a deep dive into the crime they were convicted of."

-   **Systemic Issues Identified**: 
    - Eyewitness misidentification
    - Prosecutorial misconduct
    - Inadequate investigations
    - Unreliable witness testimony
    - Racial bias in the criminal justice system
    - Unique challenges faced by women in prison

-   **Policy Recommendations/Calls to Action**: 
    - The podcast implicitly advocates for the exoneration of the featured individuals
    - Highlights the need for better investigation procedures
    - Emphasizes the importance of scrutinizing witness testimony
    - Discusses the need for accountability for prosecutorial misconduct
