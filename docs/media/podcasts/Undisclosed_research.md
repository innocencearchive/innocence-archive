#   Podcast Research: Undisclosed

##   Basic Podcast Information

-   **Title**: Undisclosed
-   **Host(s)**: Rabia Chaudry, Susan Simpson, Colin Miller
-   **Release Year(s)**: 2015-2022 (Original run), 2025 (Relaunch)
-   **Production Company(ies)/Network**: Undisclosed, LLC / Audioboom
-   **Series Type**:
    -   Ongoing Series with 24 seasons and approximately 450 episodes
-   **Format**: Podcast
-   **Focus/Theme**: Investigates wrongful convictions and the U.S. criminal justice system by examining the perpetration of crimes, investigations, trials, and verdicts, with a focus on finding new evidence that never made it to court.
-   **Key Statistics/Information**: 
    - 24 seasons covering different wrongful conviction cases
    - Approximately 450 episodes in total
    - 4.2/5 rating on Apple Podcasts (9.6K ratings)
    - Released final episode of original run in March 2022
    - Relaunched in February 2025

##   Episode List

The podcast has approximately 450 episodes across its 24 seasons. Each season typically includes main episodes focusing on the case, along with addendum episodes, updates, and occasionally bonus content.

### Notable Episodes:

#### Season 1: The State v. Adnan Syed
- This season focused on the case that was previously covered in the first season of the podcast Serial
- Examined the conviction of Adnan Syed for the killing of Hae Min Lee
- Adnan Syed was eventually released and his conviction vacated

#### Season 2: The State v. Joey Watkins
- Focused on the Georgia conviction of Joey Watkins for the murder of Isaac Dawkins
- According to the podcast, Watkins' cell phone records proved he could not have committed the murder

##   Podcast Research Notes

-   **Key Quotes/Moments**: The podcast's tagline is "The Undisclosed podcast investigates wrongful convictions, and the U.S. criminal justice system, by taking a closer look at the perpetration of a crime, its investigation, the trial, and ultimate verdict... and finding new evidence that never made it to court."

-   **Systemic Issues Identified**: The podcast examines various systemic issues in the criminal justice system that contribute to wrongful convictions, including:
    - Flawed forensic evidence
    - Eyewitness misidentification
    - False confessions
    - Prosecutorial misconduct
    - Inadequate legal representation

-   **Policy Recommendations/Calls to Action**: The podcast often discusses reforms needed in the criminal justice system and has been involved in advocacy for the exoneration of the individuals featured in their episodes.

-   **Additional Resources**: 
    - The podcast has a website: undisclosed-podcast.com
    - Related podcasts by the hosts include "The Mystery Hour with Rabia Chaudry" and "Proof: A True Crime Podcast" by Susan Simpson
