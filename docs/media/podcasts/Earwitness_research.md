#   Podcast Research: Earwitness

##   Basic Podcast Information

-   **Title**: Earwitness
-   **Host(s)**: Beth Shelburne (investigative journalist)
-   **Release Year(s)**: 2023
-   **Production Company(ies)/Network**: Lava for Good Podcasts
-   **Series Type**:
    -   Limited Series (8 episodes)
-   **Format**: Podcast/Docuseries
-   **Focus/Theme**: Investigates the wrongful conviction of Toforest Johnson, who has been on Alabama's death row for over 25 years despite significant evidence pointing to his innocence
-   **Key Statistics/Information**: 
    - Created by the team behind the award-winning podcast Bone Valley
    - Features unprecedented access to key players including the lead detective, lead prosecutor, witnesses, jurors, and the "earwitness" herself
    - Examines how Toforest Johnson was convicted and sentenced to death despite having multiple witnesses placing him elsewhere at the time of the murder

##   Wrongfully Convicted Individual Featured

### Toforest Johnson

-   **Name**: Toforest Johnson
-   **Case Location**: Birmingham, Alabama
-   **Conviction Year**: 1998
-   **Exoneration Year**: Not yet exonerated (still on death row as of 2024)
-   **Crime Convicted Of**: Murder of Deputy Sheriff William G. Hardy
-   **Sentence**: Death penalty
-   **Time Served**: Over 25 years on death row (as of 2024)
-   **Contributing Factors**: 
    - No eyewitnesses or physical evidence tied Toforest to the murder
    - The state tried to convict a different man for the same crime
    - Conviction relied on an "earwitness" - a woman who claimed to have eavesdropped on an incriminating phone call
    - Prosecutors paid the witness for her testimony in secret, which was not disclosed to the jury, Toforest, or his lawyers until after he had been on death row for 17 years
    - At least ten people saw Toforest four miles away at a popular nightclub called Tee's Place at the time of the murder
-   **Legal Status**: Still on death row in a 5' by 8' cell in Alabama
-   **Organizations Involved**: 
    - Lava for Good (advocacy through podcast)
-   **Cross-Reference**: Not yet exonerated

##   Podcast Research Notes

-   **Key Quotes/Moments**: "How did an innocent man end up on death row — and why is the state still trying to execute him over the objection of the prosecutor who put him there?"

-   **Systemic Issues Identified**: 
    - Use of paid testimony without disclosure to the jury
    - Lack of physical evidence or eyewitnesses in capital cases
    - Prosecutorial misconduct
    - Failure to consider alibis and alternative suspects
    - Continued pursuit of execution despite evidence of innocence
    - Racial bias in the criminal justice system

-   **Policy Recommendations/Calls to Action**: 
    - The podcast questions the use of the death penalty in cases with troubling evidentiary flaws
    - Highlights the need for transparency in witness compensation
    - Advocates for thorough investigation of alibis and alternative suspects
    - Questions why the state continues to pursue execution despite the objection of the prosecutor who originally tried the case
