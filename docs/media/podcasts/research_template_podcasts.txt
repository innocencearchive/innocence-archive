#   Podcast Research: Wrongful Conviction Media Sources

##   Research Template for Each Podcast

For each podcast on wrongful convictions, we'll collect the following information:

###   Basic Podcast Information

-   **Title**: Full title of the podcast
-   **Episode # & Title (if applicable)**: Episode number and title within a series
-   **Host(s)**: All hosts
-   **Release Year(s)**: Year(s) of release (e.g., 2020 or 2020-2022)
-   **Production Company(ies)/Network**: Companies or networks involved in production
-   **Series Type**:
    -   Ongoing Series
    -   Limited Series (specify number of episodes: e.g., 6 episodes)
    -   Episodic Series (each episode is generally a standalone story)
    -   Seasonal Series (specify if a particular season has a focus: e.g., Season 2 - Focus on Eyewitness Misidentification)
-   **Format**: Podcast
-   **Focus/Theme**: Main topics covered (e.g., investigative journalism, legal analysis)
-   **Key Statistics/Information**: Notable statistics or key information presented

###   Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: Executive Summary of the podcast episode as a whole. 1 paragraph, at most 8 sentences.

###   Wrongfully Convicted Individuals Featured

For each individual mentioned or featured:

-   **Name**: Full name of the wrongfully convicted person
-   **Case Location**: City, state, country
-   **Conviction Year**: When they were convicted
-   **Exoneration Year**: When they were exonerated (if applicable)
-   **Crime Convicted Of**: What they were wrongfully convicted of
-   **Sentence**: Original sentence received
-   **Time Served**: How long they were incarcerated
-   **Contributing Factors**: Factors that led to wrongful conviction (false confession, eyewitness misidentification, etc.)
-   **Legal Status**: Current legal status (exonerated, released, pardoned, etc.)
-   **Organizations Involved**: Any organizations that assisted with their case
-   **Innocence Lawyer Details: if given and available, a summary of facts about the individual(s) who assisted with their case.
-   **Cross-Reference**: Whether they appear in the exoneree database
-   **Narrative of Original Crime: a summary description of the crime for which the person was convicted.
-   **Key Evidence at Trial (Prosecution): a description of the main evidence used to convict the individual.
-   **Trial Defender Notes: a summary description of attempts or lack thereof to prove innocence by the original trial defense lawyer
-   **Key Evidence for Exoneration/Change in Legal Status: details of the specific evidence that led to exoneration or, if not fully exonerated, the path towards it.
-   **Actual Perpetrator Details: if given and available, a summary of the facts about the actual perpetrator of the crime. 



###   Podcast Research Notes

-   **Key Quotes/Moments**: Important quotes or pivotal moments in the podcast
-   **Systemic Issues Identified**: Broader issues in the justice system discussed or depicted
-   **Policy Recommendations/Calls to Action**: Any suggested reforms, policy changes, or calls to action presented
-   **Personal Narrative/Impact: A summary of the personal experience or impact described by the exoneree. A focus from Wrongful Conviction might be to summarize the final thoughts of the individual.
-   **Additional Resources**: Other sources mentioned or shown that could be valuable

##   Research Strategy

1.  Begin with podcasts that focus on multiple cases to maximize data collection
2.  Prioritize recent releases for current information and perspectives
3.  Cross-reference individuals with the exoneree database
4.  Document timestamps or relevant segments for key information to enable verification

##   Integration Plan

-   Create a structured CSV file for each podcast (or series episode) with all individuals mentioned/featured
-   Develop a master cross-reference spreadsheet linking exonerees to podcast mentions
-   Prepare database schema updates if needed to accommodate podcast references
