# Episode Research: Undisclosed - Season 1 (Adnan Syed Case)

## Episode Information

### Episode 1: Adnan's Day
- **Release Date**: Original release in 2015; Revisited version released September 21, 2022
- **Duration**: 59:34
- **Description**: Replay of Season 1, The State v. Adnan Syed. This episode focuses on Adnan Syed's day on January 13, 1999, the day Hae Min Lee disappeared.
- **Link**: https://audioboom.com/posts/8161258-undisclosed-revisited-adnan-s-day

### Episode 2: Hae's Day
- **Release Date**: Revisited version available in the podcast feed
- **Description**: Focuses on Hae Min Lee's movements and timeline on the day of her disappearance

### Episode 3: Jay's Day
- **Release Date**: Revisited version available in the podcast feed
- **Description**: Examines the testimony and timeline of Jay Wilds, a key witness in the case against Adnan Syed

### Episode 4: 28 Days
- **Description**: Continues the investigation into the case timeline

### Episode 5: Autopsies
- **Description**: Examines the forensic evidence in the case

## Wrongfully Convicted Individual Featured

### Adnan Syed

- **Name**: Adnan Syed
- **Case Location**: Baltimore, Maryland, USA
- **Conviction Year**: 2000
- **Exoneration Year**: 2022
- **Crime Convicted Of**: First-degree murder of Hae Min Lee (his ex-girlfriend)
- **Sentence**: Life plus 30 years
- **Time Served**: Approximately 23 years
- **Contributing Factors**: 
  - Unreliable witness testimony
  - Cell phone location data that was presented without proper context
  - Ineffective assistance of counsel
  - Brady violations (evidence not disclosed to defense)
- **Legal Status**: Exonerated; conviction vacated in September 2022
- **Organizations Involved**: 
  - Innocence Project
  - University of Baltimore Innocence Project Clinic
- **Cross-Reference**: Yes, appears in exoneree database

## Podcast Research Notes

- **Key Quotes/Moments**: The podcast description states "The Undisclosed podcast investigates wrongful convictions, and the U.S. criminal justice system, by taking a closer look at the perpetration of a crime, its investigation, the trial, and ultimate verdict... and finding new evidence that never made it to court."

- **Systemic Issues Identified**: 
  - Problems with reliability of cell phone location data
  - Issues with witness testimony and police interview techniques
  - Prosecutorial misconduct
  - Inadequate legal representation
