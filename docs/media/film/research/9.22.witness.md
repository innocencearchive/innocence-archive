###   Basic Film/Documentary Information

-   **Title**: The Witness
-   **Episode # & Title (if applicable)**: N/A
-   **Director(s)**: James Solomon
-   **Release Year**: 2015 (Festival), 2017 (PBS Premiere)
-   **Production Company(ies)**: The Witnesses LLC
-   **Format**: Documentary
-   **Focus/Theme**: Follows Bill Genovese, brother of murder victim Kitty Genovese, as he investigates her 1964 murder decades later. Critically re-examines the famous "38 witnesses watched and did nothing" narrative (the bystander effect) popularized by The New York Times. Explores the lasting trauma on the victim's family and the search for truth behind a crime myth.
-   **Key Statistics/Information**: Debunks or heavily nuances the widely reported story that 38 witnesses passively watched Kitty Genovese's murder. Bill Genovese lost his legs serving in Vietnam, partly motivated by his sister's death and the bystander narrative.
