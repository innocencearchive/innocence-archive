## Wrongfully Convicted Individuals Featured

-   **Name**: Film follows six inmates: George Crawford, Ashanti Witherspoon, Bishop Eugene Tanniehill, John Brown, Vincent Simmons, and Logan McGuinness.
-   **Note on Wrongful Conviction Focus**: The documentary provides intimate portraits of these men's lives in prison. While some, notably Vincent Simmons, maintained their innocence, the film's primary focus is not an investigative deep-dive into proving/disproving these claims but rather a portrayal of life under extreme sentences at Angola.
