## Film/Documentary Research Notes

-   **Notes from `film_list.txt`**: Follows stories of seven DNA exonerees adjusting to life after prison; Explores reintegration struggles. Directed by Jessica Sanders. Available via Amazon, DVD.
-   **Research Findings**: This documentary focuses on the often-overlooked difficulties faced by individuals *after* exoneration. It follows seven (list notes 7, Wikipedia lists 8) men exonerated by DNA, exploring their emotional journeys, struggles to find work, reconnect with family, access support, and obtain compensation for the years lost to wrongful imprisonment. It contrasts the public perception of exoneration as a happy ending with the often harsh realities faced by exonerees.
