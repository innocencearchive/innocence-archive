### Film/Documentary Research Notes

-   **Key Quotes/Moments**: Likely features interviews with Ryan and Bill Ferguson, footage from the trials and appeals, examination of the recanted testimonies, and the emotional toll on the family. Highlights the extraordinary efforts of a father fighting for his son's freedom.
-   **Systemic Issues Identified**: Wrongful convictions based solely on unreliable testimony, consequences of perjury, potential prosecutorial misconduct (withholding evidence), difficulty in overturning convictions even with recantations, the power of determined advocacy. The film suggests this type of wrongful conviction can happen to anyone, not just those perceived as vulnerable due to race or socioeconomic status.
-   **Policy Recommendations/Calls to Action**: Implicitly supports mechanisms for reviewing convictions based on recanted testimony and highlights the need for prosecutorial accountability (Brady compliance).
-   **Additional Resources**: News archives covering the Ryan Ferguson case, 48 Hours episodes on the case.
