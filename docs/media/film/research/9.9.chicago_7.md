## Wrongfully Convicted Individuals Featured

-   **Name**: The Chicago 7 (Abbie Hoffman, Jerry Rubin, David Dellinger, Tom Hayden, Rennie Davis, John Froines, Lee Weiner) & Bobby Seale (initially).
-   **Note on Wrongful Conviction Focus**: This film portrays a famous political trial where activists were charged with conspiracy and inciting riots related to protests at the 1968 DNC. While they were convicted on some charges (after a notoriously biased trial under Judge Julius Hoffman), the focus is on the political nature of the prosecution and the courtroom chaos, rather than factual innocence of an underlying common crime. Most of the convictions were later reversed on appeal due to judicial errors and bias. Therefore, it's generally considered a case of unjust prosecution rather than a standard wrongful conviction case. Searches did not yield specific information framing it within the typical wrongful conviction discourse.

It highlights issues of government overreach, judicial prejudice (particularly from Judge Hoffman), and the clash between counterculture movements and the establishment during the Vietnam War era. While convictions occurred, they were largely seen as politically motivated and were mostly overturned on appeal, making it distinct from cases where factual innocence is the primary issue.
