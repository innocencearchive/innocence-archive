## Film/Documentary Research Notes

-   **Key Quotes/Moments**: Morton emphasizing "accountability works" over revenge; Archival footage of Anderson mocking Morton's tears during the original trial.
-   **Systemic Issues Identified**: The devastating impact of prosecutorial misconduct, the failure to disclose evidence, the difficulty in holding prosecutors accountable, the long battle often required for DNA testing.
-   **Policy Recommendations/Calls to Action**: Implied need for greater prosecutorial accountability and stricter adherence to evidence disclosure rules (Brady rule).
