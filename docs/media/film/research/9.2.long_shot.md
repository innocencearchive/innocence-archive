## Film/Documentary Research Notes

-   **Notes from `film_list.txt`**: Tells remarkable story of how footage from HBO's "Curb Your Enthusiasm" helped exonerate Juan Catalan; Explores role of coincidence and persistence. Available via Netflix.
-   **Research Findings**: This short documentary focuses on the unique way Juan Catalan's lawyer proved his client's alibi. Catalan claimed he was at a Dodgers game during the time of the murder he was accused of. Standard checks (tickets, receipts) were insufficient, but his lawyer discovered "Curb Your Enthusiasm" was filming at the stadium that day and meticulously reviewed outtakes, finding footage of Catalan that corroborated his alibi and led to the charges being dropped.
