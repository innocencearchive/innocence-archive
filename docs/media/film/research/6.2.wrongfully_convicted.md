## Film/Documentary Research Notes

-   **Notes from `film_list.txt`**: Profiles case of three men spending combined 100+ years based on false testimony of 12-year-old boy who later recanted. Available via ABC/Hulu.
-   **Research Findings**: While searches didn't confirm a specific Nightline episode with this title, the details match the high-profile exoneration of Ricky Jackson, Wiley Bridgeman, and Kwame Ajamu in Cleveland. Their convictions rested almost entirely on the testimony of a young boy, Eddie Vernon, who recanted decades later, stating police coerced him. Jackson's 39 years served was the longest known US wrongful imprisonment at the time of his release. Case details above rely on list info and public knowledge consistent with the list description.
