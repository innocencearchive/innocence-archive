## Film/Documentary Research Notes

-   **Notes from `film_list.txt`**: Chronicles filmmaker Ray Klonsky's efforts to help free David McCallum, wrongfully convicted of murder at age 16. Directed by Ray Klonsky & Marc Lamy. Available via Amazon Prime, DVD, Festivals.
-   **Research Findings**: This documentary follows the personal journey of Ray Klonsky, who began corresponding with David McCallum as a teenager. Their friendship evolved into an effort, documented by Klonsky and co-director Marc Lamy, to investigate McCallum's decades-old conviction, highlighting coerced confessions and weak evidence. The film captures the long fight involving lawyers and advocates like Rubin Carter, culminating in McCallum's exoneration in 2014.
