## Film/Documentary Research Notes

-   **Notes from `film_list.txt`**: Documents Darryl Hunt's struggle for justice after 1984 wrongful conviction for rape/murder. Directed by Ricki Stern & Annie Sundberg. Available via HBO Max, Amazon Prime, DVD.
-   **Research Findings**: This documentary provides an in-depth chronicle of Darryl Hunt's case, from his initial arrest and trials marred by racial bias and questionable evidence, through the decade-long fight by his attorney Mark Rabil to get DNA testing, the initial DNA exclusion that didn't free him, and the eventual DNA match that identified the true perpetrator and led to Hunt's exoneration after 19 years. It highlights systemic failures and the immense personal toll of wrongful conviction.
