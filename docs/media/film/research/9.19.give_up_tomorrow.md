###   Wrongfully Convicted Individuals Featured

-   **Name**: Francisco Juan "Paco" Larrañaga
-   **Case Location**: Cebu City, Philippines
-   **Conviction Year**: 1999 (Crime occurred 1997)
-   **Exoneration Year**: Not exonerated.
-   **Crime Convicted Of**: Kidnapping and Serious Illegal Detention with Homicide and Rape (Chiong sisters case)
-   **Sentence**: Death penalty (imposed by Supreme Court 2004, affirmed 2005), later commuted to life imprisonment when the Philippines abolished capital punishment in 2006.
-   **Time Served**: 1997-Present (Transferred to serve sentence in Spain in 2009 under a treaty).
-   **Contributing Factors**: Unreliable/questionable witness testimony (key witness Rusia admitted to prior convictions after denying them), limited ability for defense to cross-examine key witness, alleged judicial corruption, ignoring strong alibi evidence.
-   **Legal Status**: Convicted; serving sentence in Spain. Co-defendants Josman Aznar, Rowen Adlawan (alias Wesley Balansag), Alberto Caño, and Ariel Balansag were released in 2019 under a good conduct law (GCTA), but their release was controversial and they later surrendered. James Anthony Uy was also convicted but received a lesser sentence as a minor.

-   **Cross-Reference**: Unlikely to be in typical US-based exoneree databases as the case is in the Philippines and he has not been exonerated.
