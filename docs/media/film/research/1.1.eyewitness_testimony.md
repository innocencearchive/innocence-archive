### Film/Documentary Research Notes

* **Key Quotes/Moments**:
    * Jennifer Thompson describing the "suffocating, debilitating shame" after learning of her mistake [cite: 1.1].
    * Jennifer Thompson: "A wrongful conviction doesn't hurt like a person... I think it's hundreds of people, for every single wrongful conviction case, that are hurt" [cite: 1.1].
    * The segment highlights Cotton's exoneration via DNA after 10.5-11 years in prison [cite: 1.1, 2.1, 2.2].
    * Features Thompson and Cotton speaking together about their experience and advocating for reform [cite: 1.1].
* **Systemic Issues Identified**:
    * Fundamental unreliability of eyewitness memory and identification [cite: 2, 1.1, 1.2].
    * Problems with police lineup procedures (photo and physical) [cite: 1.1, 2.1, 2.2].
    * The justice system's failure to consider or admit exculpatory evidence [cite: 2.1, 2.2].
    * The widespread impact ("shrapnel") of a wrongful conviction beyond just the innocent person [cite: 1.1].
    * Lack of accountability for systemic failures leading to wrongful convictions [cite: 1.1].
* **Policy Recommendations/Calls to Action**: The segment implicitly calls for reforms in eyewitness identification procedures and greater system accountability, highlighted by Thompson's advocacy work [cite: 1.1].
