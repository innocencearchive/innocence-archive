## Film/Documentary Research Notes

-   **Notes from `film_list.txt`**: Directed by Errol Morris, focuses on Randall Dale Adams case, led to Adams' release. Available on various platforms.
-   **Research Findings**: Errol Morris's highly influential documentary used stylized reenactments and interviews to meticulously deconstruct the case against Randall Dale Adams. Adams was convicted largely on the testimony of David Ray Harris, a teenager Adams had met hitchhiking, who was later revealed to be the actual killer [Based on common knowledge]. The film exposed contradictions, unreliable witnesses, and strongly suggested Harris's culpability, ultimately leading to Adams's conviction being overturned and his release. It's a landmark film both for its style and its real-world impact on a wrongful conviction case.
