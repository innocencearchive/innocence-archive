# Research: 2.1. The Plea (FRONTLINE)

## Basic Film/Documentary Information

-   **Title**: FRONTLINE
-   **Episode # & Title (if applicable)**: "The Plea"
-   **Director(s)**: Ofra Bikel
-   **Release Year**: 2004
-   **Production Company(ies)**: FRONTLINE, WGBH, Ofra Bikel Productions
-   **Format**: Documentary (Series Episode)
-   **Focus/Theme**: Plea bargaining system, innocent defendants pleading guilty, prosecutorial pressure, inadequate defense counsel, impact of poverty on justice outcomes.
-   **Key Statistics/Information**: Highlights how the vast majority of cases are resolved through pleas, often pressuring innocent individuals.

## Film/Documentary Research Notes

-   **Key Quotes/Moments**: Stewart describing the pressure to take the plea to care for her children; Kelly discussing the inadequate time/advice from court-appointed counsel; Discussion of the informant's unreliability coming out later.
-   **Systemic Issues Identified**: The overwhelming pressure of the plea bargain system, particularly on poor defendants; inadequate resources and potential conflicts of interest for court-appointed attorneys; the devastating collateral consequences of a felony conviction (even via plea); the potential for unreliable informants to cause widespread injustice; racial disparities in drug enforcement.
-   **Policy Recommendations/Calls to Action**: Implied need for reform in the plea bargaining process, better funding and oversight of indigent defense, scrutiny of informant use, addressing collateral consequences.
