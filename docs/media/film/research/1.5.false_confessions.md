# Research: 1.5. False Confessions (60 Minutes)

## Basic Film/Documentary Information

-   **Title**: 60 Minutes
-   **Episode # & Title (if applicable)**: Segment "The False Confession Capital" (Reported by Byron Pitts)
-   **Director(s)**: Information Unavailable
-   **Release Year**: December 9, 2012
-   **Production Company(ies)**: CBS News
-   **Format**: TV news magazine segment
-   **Focus/Theme**: False confessions, police interrogation techniques, juvenile suspects, wrongful convictions in Chicago.
-   **Key Statistics/Information**: Chicago identified as having a high number of documented false confession cases ("False Confession Capital").

## Film/Documentary Research Notes

-   **Key Quotes/Moments**: Interviews with exonerated individuals describing interrogation experiences; Psychologist Saul Kassin explaining the psychology of false confessions; Cook County State's Attorney Anita Alvarez defending police actions at the time.
-   **Systemic Issues Identified**: Psychological vulnerability of suspects (especially juveniles) during intense interrogation, police tactics leading to false confessions, reliance on confessions over contradictory evidence (like DNA), difficulty in overturning convictions based on confessions.
-   **Policy Recommendations/Calls to Action**: Implied need for reforms in police interrogation practices (e.g., recording interrogations, time limits, special protections for juveniles), better evaluation of confession evidence by prosecutors and courts.
