## Film/Documentary Research Notes

-   **Notes from `film_list.txt`**: Follows Bloodsworth's journey from death row to exoneration to becoming an advocate for criminal justice reform. Available via CNN/Max.
-   **Research Findings**: Kirk Bloodsworth's case is landmark as the first US death row DNA exoneration. He was convicted based on eyewitness accounts despite lack of physical evidence, had his first conviction overturned due to prosecutorial misconduct (withheld evidence), was reconvicted, and finally freed by DNA testing in 1993. While a specific CNN Special Report titled "Sole Survivor" wasn't confirmed via search, his story has been widely covered, including in the 2015 documentary "Bloodsworth: An Innocent Man".
