## The Wrongful Convictions Reader

### Basic Book Information
- **Title**: The Wrongful Convictions Reader
- **Author(s)**: Russell D. Covey and Valena E. Beety (Editors)
- **Publication Year**: 2018 (1st Edition), 2023 (2nd Edition)
- **Publisher**: Carolina Academic Press
- **Format**: Paperback
- **Focus/Theme**: Core contributing factors to wrongful convictions including false confessions, witness misidentifications, cognitive bias, junk science, police and prosecutorial misconduct
- **Key Statistics**: Over 45% of documented wrongful capital convictions have been traced to false informant testimony

### Research Notes
This book takes an interdisciplinary approach to the study of wrongful convictions, combining legal scholarship with social science research. It appears to be designed as a comprehensive textbook for courses on wrongful convictions, with interactive elements and multimedia resources.

The book is structured around the contributing factors to wrongful convictions rather than focusing on specific cases, though case studies are likely used throughout to illustrate these factors. The second edition (2023) has been expanded to 728 pages from the original 666 pages, suggesting updated or additional content.

According to a review by Professor Stephanie Hartung of Northeastern Law School, what makes this book unique is "its interdisciplinary approach to the study of wrongful convictions" and its inclusion of interactive exercises and multimedia resources.
