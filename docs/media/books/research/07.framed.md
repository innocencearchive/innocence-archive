# Book Research: Wrongful Conviction Media Sources

## Book 7: Framed: Astonishing True Stories of Wrongful Convictions

### Basic Book Information
- **Title**: Framed: Astonishing True Stories of Wrongful Convictions
- **Author(s)**: John Grisham and Jim McCloskey
- **Publication Year**: 2024 (October 15, 2024)
- **Publisher**: Penguin Random House (Doubleday)
- **Format**: Hardcover, also available as paperback and e-book
- **Focus/Theme**: Collection of case studies examining wrongful convictions, their causes, and the fight for exoneration
- **Key Statistics**: The book mentions there have been 3,600 exonerations in the U.S. since 1989, with Centurion Ministries (founded by co-author Jim McCloskey) responsible for 70 exonerations

### Wrongfully Convicted Individuals Featured
The book features ten true stories of wrongful convictions. Based on the research, the following individuals are mentioned:

1. **Name**: Clarence Brandley
   - **Case Location**: Conroe, Texas
   - **Conviction Year**: Early 1980s
   - **Exoneration Year**: After nearly 10 years in prison
   - **Crime Convicted Of**: Rape and murder of a 16-year-old White girl
   - **Sentence**: Death row
   - **Time Served**: Nearly 10 years
   - **Contributing Factors**: Racial bias (Brandley was Black), tunnel vision by investigators, circumstantial evidence (he found the body)
   - **Legal Status**: Exonerated, died in 2018 of pneumonia
   - **Organizations Involved**: Centurion Ministries
   - **Cross-Reference**: Not specified

2. **Name**: Ron Williamson (mentioned as a previous case that inspired Grisham's earlier book "The Innocent Man")
   - **Case Location**: Oklahoma
   - **Conviction Year**: Not specified in the research
   - **Exoneration Year**: Not specified, but was exonerated through DNA evidence
   - **Crime Convicted Of**: Murder of a local waitress
   - **Sentence**: Death row
   - **Time Served**: Came within five days of execution
   - **Contributing Factors**: Lack of DNA testing at time of conviction
   - **Legal Status**: Exonerated
   - **Organizations Involved**: Not specified
   - **Cross-Reference**: Not specified

Note: The research did not reveal detailed information about the other eight cases featured in the book.

### Book Research Notes
- **Key Quotes**: 
  - "From a moral perspective, it is imperative for a society to face and correct injustice... We decided to write about and focus on the ten most astonishing cases, and publish them as Framed. There are hundreds of others." — John Grisham
  - "The flaws and lethal errors that condemn innocent people in the American legal system have caused too many for too long to needlessly suffer." — Bryan Stevenson (quoted in book review)
  - "There have been 3,600 exonerations in the U.S. since 1989. In my understanding, there has not been one public inquiry about any single conviction." — Jim McCloskey

- **Systemic Issues Identified**: 
  - Law enforcement tunnel vision: "They're under enormous pressure to solve the crime, to clear the case, to get the conviction, and that bleeds over to the prosecution... Once they zero in on a suspect, they ignore all contrary evidence." — John Grisham
  - Racial bias in the criminal justice system
  - Flawed forensic science (mentioned in relation to shaken baby syndrome case)
  - Prosecutorial misconduct
  - Biased judges
  - Coached witnesses
  - Compromised jailhouse snitches
  - Cultural bias

- **Policy Recommendations**: 
  - Grisham calls for an end to the death penalty in the U.S.
  - The book advocates for reform to address issues like wrongful convictions
  - Suggests the need for public inquiries into wrongful convictions

- **Additional Resources**: 
  - Centurion Ministries, founded by co-author Jim McCloskey, which works to free the wrongly convicted
  - Grisham's previous book "The Innocent Man" (2006), which was later developed into a Netflix series

## Research Strategy Notes
This book is particularly valuable for our project as it:
1. Features multiple cases to maximize data collection
2. Is a very recent publication (October 2024) with current information
3. Is co-authored by Jim McCloskey, founder of Centurion Ministries, a leading organization in exonerating the wrongfully convicted
4. Provides analysis of systemic issues contributing to wrongful convictions

The book appears to focus on the dramatic stories of exoneration battles while also examining the underlying causes of wrongful convictions, making it a rich source for both individual cases and systemic analysis.
