### Additional Notes
The book appears to be a comprehensive examination of the challenges facing forensic science, including issues that can lead to wrongful convictions. While it does not focus exclusively on wrongful convictions, it does address how problems in forensic science can contribute to them, particularly in Chapter 11 which discusses DNA's role in exonerating the innocent.

The book is structured to examine various aspects of forensic science, including the challenges faced by forensic laboratories, issues with expert testimony, and problems with the medico-legal death investigation system. It includes interviews with leading experts in the field and explores possible solutions to the identified problems.

Based on the available information, the book seems to be more focused on the systemic issues in forensic science rather than detailed case studies of specific wrongfully convicted individuals. However, it does discuss how these systemic issues can and have led to wrongful convictions, particularly through the lens of DNA exonerations.
