### Additional Notes
Jim McCloskey founded Centurion Ministries in 1983, the first organization in America devoted to overturning wrongful convictions. His journey began when he was a chaplain at Trenton State Prison in 1980, where he met Jorge de los Santos, who claimed innocence. After investigating the case, McCloskey became convinced of de los Santos's innocence and worked to free him.

The book chronicles McCloskey's journey from a former management consultant who enrolled at Princeton Theological Seminary at the age of 37 to becoming one of the foremost advocates for the wrongfully convicted. John Grisham, who wrote the foreword to the book, calls McCloskey "the dean of all innocence advocates."

The memoir details not only the cases McCloskey worked on but also the personal and spiritual journey that led him to dedicate his life to freeing the innocent. The book has been praised for its compassionate portrayal of the wrongfully convicted and its illumination of flaws in the criminal justice system.
