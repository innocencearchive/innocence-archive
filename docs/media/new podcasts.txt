
## Podcasts
- **"Dead Man Talking"** - Explores whether Angel Resendiz aka "The Railroad Killer," who was executed by the State of Texas in 2006, was responsible for other murders to which he purportedly confessed.
- **"Exoneration Nation"** - Christopher Scott was convicted of murder in 1997 and exonerated in 2009. In this podcast, Scott interviews fellow exonerees, providing them the opportunity to share their unique stories.
- **"If Emmett Was Alive Today Podcast"** - Features interviews with Texas death row exonerees Christopher Scott and Anthony Graves detailing their experience as innocent men on death row.
- **"Murderville"** - In Season Two, investigates the case of Charles Raby, who has been on death row since 1994 and maintains his innocence of the crime to which he seemingly confessed.
