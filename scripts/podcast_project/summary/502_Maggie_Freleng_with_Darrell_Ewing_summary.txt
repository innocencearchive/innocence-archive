### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode details the wrongful conviction of Dorelle Ewing for the 2009 murder of J.B. Watson in Detroit. Convicted primarily on the eyewitness testimony of Raymond Love and a gang rivalry theory, Ewing maintained his innocence, supported by a strong alibi confirmed by multiple witnesses. Post-conviction, significant juror misconduct involving outside research and social media viewing surfaced, alongside a confession from the actual perpetrator, Tyree Washington, corroborated by an FBI informant. After years of appeals highlighting these issues and a final confrontation over due process violations regarding evidence disclosure, a judge dismissed the charges against Ewing in March 2024. He served over 14 years and is now pursuing a law degree while advocating for justice system reform, though the District Attorney is appealing the dismissal.
