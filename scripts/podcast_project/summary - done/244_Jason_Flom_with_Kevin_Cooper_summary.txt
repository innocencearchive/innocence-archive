- **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Kevin Cooper, who has been imprisoned since 1985 for the murders of the Ryan family and Christopher Hughes, crimes he insists he did not commit.  The podcast details the horrific crime and the subsequent investigation, highlighting numerous instances of alleged police misconduct, evidence tampering, and a rush to judgment.  Despite initial witness descriptions of three white or Hispanic perpetrators, suspicion quickly fell on Cooper, a Black man who had recently escaped prison nearby.  The episode presents a compelling case for Cooper's innocence, citing mishandled evidence, planted evidence, ignored alternate suspects, and a flawed trial.  Kevin Cooper and his attorney, Norman Heil, discuss the case's complexities, the ongoing fight for exoneration, and the recent innocence investigation ordered by Governor Newsom, offering a stark example of potential systemic failures within the criminal justice system.
