### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom revisits the case of James King, who remains imprisoned despite compelling evidence of his innocence in a sexual assault case.  King was initially convicted based on the testimony of a 13-year-old girl, Jamie, whose story was riddled with inconsistencies and demonstrably false claims, including fabricated physical evidence and refuted medical testimony.  Despite a federal judge overturning his first conviction due to ineffective counsel for failing to challenge the flawed medical expert testimony, King was retried and again wrongfully convicted.  His attorney, Ian Graham, discusses the egregious errors in the case, the devastating impact on King and his family, and the ongoing fight for his exoneration, now focused on clemency from the governor after the California Supreme Court denied his habeas petition. The episode highlights systemic failures in the justice system, the devastating consequences of false accusations, and the urgent need for reform.
