### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode details the case of Brandon Spencer, convicted of four counts of attempted murder following a shooting at a party near USC in 2012 and sentenced to 40 years to life. The prosecution heavily relied on alleged gang motivations, interpreting photos, tattoos, and social media posts as evidence, despite Brandon's denial of gang affiliation and lack of prior record. Key physical evidence like gunshot residue testing and fingerprints did not link Brandon to the crime, and the DNA evidence on the gun was weak (1 in 40). The primary eyewitness later recanted, alleging coercion by police and prosecutors. Now represented by the Innocence Center, Brandon's legal team is exploring avenues for relief, including the recantation and California's Racial Justice Act, while Brandon focuses on personal growth and maintaining hope after over 12 years incarcerated.
