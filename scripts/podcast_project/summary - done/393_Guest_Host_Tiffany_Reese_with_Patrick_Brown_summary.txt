### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features the story of Patrick Brown, wrongfully convicted of aggravated rape in 1994 based on a child's statement to a doctor, despite his innocence and lack of corroborating evidence.  The host, Tiffany Reese, interviews Patrick and his attorney, Kelly Oryans, detailing the flawed trial, the victim Sarah's decades-long struggle to correct the injustice, and Patrick's eventual exoneration in 2023 after serving nearly 30 years. The episode highlights systemic issues including leading questions in child interviews, ineffective defense, prosecutorial misconduct under DA Harry Connick Sr.'s office, and the devastating impact of wrongful convictions on both the wrongfully accused and the original victim. It emphasizes the importance of listening to survivors and reforming the justice system. Both Patrick and Kelly provide closing arguments advocating for truth, systemic change, and a more compassionate approach to justice.

-   **Systemic Issues Identified**:
    -   Flawed child sexual assault investigations: Leading questions, lack of parental presence during questioning, misinterpretation of child statements, reliance on doctor's interpretations over direct child testimony.
    -   Ineffective indigent defense system: Lack of resources for defense attorneys, contributing to inadequate representation.
    -   Prosecutorial misconduct: Withholding of potentially exculpatory evidence, as exemplified by the DA's office under Harry Connick Sr.
    -   Re-victimization of survivors: Sarah's decades-long struggle to be heard and correct the wrongful conviction highlights the system's failure to listen to and support victims.
    -   Over-reliance on incarceration as a solution to crime: Questioning the effectiveness and justice of long prison sentences and advocating for alternative approaches to justice and community safety.
-   **Policy Recommendations/Calls to Action**:
    -   Reimagine justice and safety in communities beyond imprisonment.
    -   Reform the justice system to prioritize truth, survivor voices, and prevent wrongful convictions.
    -   Improve investigation techniques in child sexual assault cases, ensuring accurate and sensitive questioning and proper consideration of child testimony.
    -   Strengthen indigent defense systems to provide adequate legal representation.
    -   Increase transparency and accountability within prosecutor's offices.
