### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction, guest-hosted by exoneree Patrick Persley, tells the story of Carl Williams, who was wrongfully convicted of murder and carjacking in Chicago at age 17.  Despite no physical evidence and co-defendants initially stating his innocence, Williams was convicted based on a coerced false confession after hours of intense police interrogation. The episode details the systemic failures within the criminal justice system, including ineffective public defenders and lengthy post-conviction processes.  Williams spent 26 years in prison before being released due to a Miller v. Alabama related sentencing adjustment, not full exoneration.  Despite this, Carl Williams has dedicated his life post-release to community work, teaching carpentry to at-risk youth and running his own furniture business, Royal Men Solutions, embodying a powerful story of resilience and redemption. The episode concludes with calls to action for listeners to vote for reform-minded prosecutors and legislators to address wrongful convictions and hold accountable those responsible for misconduct.
