### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction, False Confessions" introduces the case of Tommy Ward and Carl Fontenot, two men wrongfully convicted in Ada, Oklahoma, for the 1984 disappearance of Denise Haraway.  The hosts, Lauren Nyreiter and Steve Drizen, along with author John Grisham, detail how police coerced a false confession from Tommy Ward based on a dream he described during interrogation.  This dream, influenced by details the police themselves suggested, became the cornerstone of the prosecution's case. Carl Fontenot, who was intellectually disabled and traumatized by his mother's recent death, also confessed under pressure. Despite inconsistencies and lack of physical evidence, including Denise Haraway's body, both men were convicted and sentenced to death, highlighting the dangers of false confessions and flawed evidence in the justice system. The episode sets up a two-part story, promising further developments and analysis in the next episode.
