### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom delves into the case of Lamont McIntyre, wrongfully convicted at 17 for a double homicide in 1994 and imprisoned for 23 years. The episode features interviews with McIntyre's attorney, Cheryl Pilate, retired FBI agent Al Generich, and McIntyre himself.  The investigation was described as grossly deficient, lacking physical evidence and ignoring exculpatory information.  Key evidence against McIntyre relied on eyewitness testimony, later revealed to be unreliable and potentially coerced.  The podcast highlights the alleged corruption of detective Roger Golubsky, who is suspected of framing McIntyre due to personal vendetta and was involved in widespread corruption within the Kansas City, Kansas Police Department.  The victims' families believed in McIntyre's innocence throughout.  McIntyre was eventually exonerated in 2017 after a long legal battle, exposing profound systemic failures and police misconduct.
