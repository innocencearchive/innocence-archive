### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features the case of John Moss, who has been imprisoned for nearly 40 years for a triple homicide he did not commit in West Virginia.  The episode includes interviews with John Moss, Professor Mark Howard, and Georgetown pre-law student Jessica Scorato, who are working on his case.  The podcast details how despite the actual perpetrator confessing to the gruesome murders of a mother and her two young children, John Moss, a young black man, was falsely accused and convicted based on a coerced confession and falsified blood evidence by notorious serologist Fred Zane.  The discussion highlights the systemic failures, potential racial bias, and ineffective legal representation that led to this egregious miscarriage of justice.  The episode calls for public awareness and action to pressure West Virginia authorities to rectify this wrongful conviction and free John Moss.  Listeners are directed to justiceforjohnmoss.com to learn more and get involved.
