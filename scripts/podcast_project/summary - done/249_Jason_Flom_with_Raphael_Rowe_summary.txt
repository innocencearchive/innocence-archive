### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Raphael Rowe, who shares his harrowing story of being wrongfully convicted for a series of violent robberies and a murder in 1988 in Southeast England, known as the "M25 crimes". Despite consistent witness descriptions of two white and one black assailant and strong alibis, Raphael and two other black men were convicted based on flawed witness testimonies, incentivized informants, and police misconduct.  Raphael details the racial bias, fabricated evidence, and pressure on victims to change their statements. He spent 12 years in brutal prison conditions, during which he educated himself in law and journalism.  Through persistent efforts and media attention, his case reached the European Court of Human Rights, leading to his exoneration in 2000.  Raphael now works as a journalist and advocate for criminal justice reform, highlighting systemic issues and the devastating impact of wrongful convictions.
