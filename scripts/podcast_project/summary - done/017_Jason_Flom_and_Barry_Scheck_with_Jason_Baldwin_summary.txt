### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom features Jason Baldwin of the West Memphis 3 and Barry Scheck of the Innocence Project. Baldwin recounts his wrongful conviction at age 16 for the murder of three boys, highlighting the lack of evidence and the role of community panic and "satanic ritual" accusations in the trial. The discussion delves into the coerced false confession of Jesse Miss Kelly due to his low IQ and police interrogation tactics.  Scheck emphasizes systemic issues like inadequate defense funding and the power of false confessions in court. Baldwin describes the horrific conditions in maximum security prison and the Sophie's Choice of taking an Alford plea to free himself and Damien Echols, despite maintaining innocence.  The episode concludes with a call to action to support innocence organizations and advocate for criminal justice reform, emphasizing the importance of education to combat the "they all say that" dismissal of innocence claims.
