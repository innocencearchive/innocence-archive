### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features guest host Patrick Persley interviewing Eric Blackbin about his wrongful conviction for murder in Chicago.  Blackbin recounts his arrest, despite having an alibi of attending a barbecue with dozens of witnesses.  He details the coercive interrogation tactics used by police, his inadequate legal representation at trial, and his subsequent 60-year sentence.  Blackbin describes the horrific conditions of Cook County Jail and his journey to fight his conviction from within prison as a "jailhouse lawyer." With the help of pro bono attorneys from the Center on Wrongful Convictions and Karen Daniel, his conviction was eventually overturned after he served years in prison for a crime he did not commit. Blackbin now works to reform the criminal justice system and advocates for others facing wrongful convictions and excessive sentencing. He emphasizes the importance of fighting injustice and supporting organizations working on criminal justice reform.
