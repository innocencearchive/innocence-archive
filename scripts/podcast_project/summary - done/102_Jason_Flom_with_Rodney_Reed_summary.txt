### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom" focuses on the case of Rodney Reed, who was sentenced to death for the 1996 murder of Stacey Stites. The episode reveals that Reed's conviction was based on flawed forensic testimony regarding sperm survival and a false timeline of events.  Key evidence presented includes recanted testimonies from state experts, expert forensic analysis from Dr. Michael Baden indicating a different time of death, and the emergence of Jimmy Fennell, the victim's fiancé, as a highly suspicious alternative suspect. The podcast features interviews with Rodney Reed from death row, his attorney Bryce Benjet, his brother Roderick Reed, and Dr. Phil, all advocating for Reed's innocence and highlighting systemic issues within the justice system that led to his wrongful conviction. The episode emphasizes the urgent need to reopen Reed's case and prevent his execution, highlighting the potential for a grave miscarriage of justice.  Listeners are urged to take action by contacting Governor Abbott and supporting the Innocence Project.
