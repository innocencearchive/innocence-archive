### Basic Podcast Information

- **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling" tells the story of Nikki Zinger, who was wrongfully convicted of the first-degree murder of her mother, Linda Holly, in 1991.  Nikki and her boyfriend, Daniel Risher, became primary suspects shortly after Linda's body was discovered. The prosecution's case heavily relied on circumstantial evidence, including life insurance policies and flawed luminol testing suggesting blood on Daniel's jacket was Linda's.  Despite maintaining her innocence and the lack of concrete evidence, Nikki was convicted and sentenced to life in prison. The podcast highlights the inadequate investigation, the prosecution's focus on a financial motive, and the impact of "Satanic Panic" era biases on the case.  After 31 years of incarceration, new DNA evidence and the work of Proclaim Justice offer a glimmer of hope for Nikki's exoneration, though she remains imprisoned and longs for freedom and justice.

    - Life insurance policies on Linda Holly, with Nikki as the primary beneficiary.

- **Trial Defender Notes**: Defense attempted to introduce alternate suspects, including a police officer Linda was dating and other individuals seen around her property. They also tried to highlight the similarity to another murder case nearby with different suspects.  The defense also pointed out the lack of concrete evidence and questioned the motive given Linda's terminal cancer.
- **Key Evidence for Exoneration/Change in Legal Status**:
    - DNA testing on the blood found on Daniel's jacket indicated it was not definitively human blood, supporting Daniel's claim it was deer blood from hunting.
    - Lack of a clear motive, especially considering Linda's declining health due to cancer.
    - Parole board unanimously recommended parole in 2016 based on new DNA findings, but it was denied by the governor.
    - Continued legal efforts are underway, focusing on further DNA testing of other evidence.
