### Basic Podcast Information

- **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction focuses on the case of Robert Robertson, a Texas man on death row for the alleged Shaken Baby Syndrome (SBS) death of his two-year-old daughter Nikki in 2002. The podcast highlights the now-debunked SBS diagnosis as junk science and presents evidence suggesting Nikki's death was likely due to undiagnosed medical issues, including viral pneumonia and the harmful prescription drug Finergan, coupled with a possible fall from bed.  Despite new scientific understanding and exculpatory evidence like missing CAT scans showing only one impact site on Nikki's head, lower courts have denied Robert a new trial. His appellate attorney Gretchen Swen and host Jason Flom argue that Robert's original trial was a farce, tainted by false accusations of sexual abuse, and that systemic issues and outdated medical beliefs led to his wrongful conviction and death sentence.  They call for public awareness and action to support Robert and reform the justice system to prevent similar tragedies.
