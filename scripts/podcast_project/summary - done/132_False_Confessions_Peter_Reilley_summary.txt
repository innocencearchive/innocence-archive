### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction: False Confessions" tells the story of Peter Riley, an 18-year-old wrongfully convicted in 1973 for the murder of his mother based on a false confession.  After discovering his mother's brutalized body, police interrogation tactics led Peter to believe he might have committed the crime and repressed the memory.  Despite community support and celebrity advocacy spearheaded by Arthur Miller, Peter was initially convicted.  However, expert testimony and the discovery of withheld alibi evidence led to his exoneration in 1976. Peter's case became a landmark example of false confessions, highlighting systemic issues in the justice system and the importance of recording interrogations, influencing legal reforms in Connecticut and beyond and inspiring the hosts' work in wrongful conviction advocacy.
