### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling" tells the story of Annette Cahill, wrongfully convicted of the 1992 murder of Corey Winicki.  Annette, a 61-year-old woman with a family, was arrested decades after the crime based on a recovered childhood memory and circumstantial evidence. The podcast details Annette's life, her complicated relationship with Corey, and the investigation that led to her conviction despite two hung juries.  Key evidence against her included a 25-year-old memory of a child claiming to have heard Annette confessing and later testimony about burning bloody clothes.  The episode highlights the lack of forensic evidence linking Annette to the crime and introduces new evidence discovered by the Iowa State Public Defender's Office, including inconsistencies in the eyewitness testimony and potential alternative suspects. Annette maintains her innocence and is fighting for exoneration, raising serious questions about the reliability of memory and the justice system.
