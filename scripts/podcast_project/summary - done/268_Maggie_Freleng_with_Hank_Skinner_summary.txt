### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling" focuses on the case of Hank Skinner, who has been on death row in Texas for nearly 30 years for the 1993 murders of his girlfriend and her two sons. Despite evidence suggesting his incapacitation due to extreme intoxication on the night of the murders, and the presence of another potential suspect (the victim's uncle), Skinner was convicted and sentenced to death. The podcast details Skinner's life, the events leading up to the murders, the flawed trial, and the subsequent appeals process. Key issues highlighted include potential police bias, inadequate defense, reliance on questionable witness testimony, and the challenges of overturning a wrongful conviction even with DNA evidence.  Skinner maintains his innocence and continues to fight for exoneration with the help of his wife and legal team.
