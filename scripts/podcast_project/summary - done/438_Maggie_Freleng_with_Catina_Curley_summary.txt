### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling" tells the story of Katina Curly, wrongfully convicted of second-degree murder for the death of her abusive husband, Reynaldo.  Despite a long history of documented domestic violence, Katina was convicted in 2007 and sentenced to life in prison.  Her initial defense attorney failed to present evidence of battered women's syndrome. After serving 11 and a half years, her conviction was overturned due to ineffective counsel.  In a 2019 retrial, with expert testimony on battered women's syndrome, Katina was acquitted.  Her story highlights the systemic issues in the justice system regarding domestic violence survivors and the importance of expert testimony in such cases. Katina now advocates for other women facing similar situations.
