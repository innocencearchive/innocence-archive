### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features the story of Jerome Loach, wrongfully convicted in Philadelphia for a home invasion he did not commit.  Despite having a strong alibi of performing in a church play with over 100 witnesses, and the recantation of the co-defendant who initially implicated him under police pressure, Loach was convicted based on fabricated phone records presented by the prosecution.  His defense attorneys failed to present his alibi effectively and expose the fraudulent evidence.  After serving a decade in prison, Loach fought for his exoneration by uncovering the truth about the fabricated phone records himself, ultimately leading to his release and the dismissal of charges due to ineffective counsel and prosecutorial misconduct.  He is now seeking compensation for the injustice he suffered. The episode highlights the dangers of police coercion, prosecutorial misconduct, and ineffective legal counsel within the criminal justice system.
