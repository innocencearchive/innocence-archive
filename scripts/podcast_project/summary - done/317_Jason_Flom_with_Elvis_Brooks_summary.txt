### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction details the story of Elvis Brooks, wrongfully convicted in 1977 for a robbery and murder in New Orleans.  The episode highlights the role of cross-racial misidentification and prosecutorial misconduct in his conviction. Despite fingerprint evidence at the crime scene not matching Brooks and thirteen alibi witnesses, he was convicted based on eyewitness testimony from traumatized, intoxicated white witnesses.  The prosecution withheld the exculpatory fingerprint evidence from the defense. Brooks spent over 42 years in Angola prison before being released after a plea deal and later fully exonerated. The podcast underscores systemic racism and the failures within the criminal justice system, particularly in Orleans Parish during the Harry Connick Sr. era.
