### Executive Summary

This episode of Wrongful Conviction features host Jason Flom in conversation with author John Grisham, discussing Grisham's non-fiction book and the Netflix series "The Innocent Man," which details the wrongful convictions of Ron Williamson and Dennis Fritz in Ada, Oklahoma. Grisham recounts the unbelievable factual basis of the case, highlighting the incompetence and tunnel vision of the police investigation, which led to the conviction of two innocent men for a murder committed by Glenn Gore. The discussion expands to broader issues within the criminal justice system, including junk science, unreliable jailhouse snitches, and the problem of false confessions, particularly in cases involving vulnerable individuals. Grisham and Flom advocate for juror education and skepticism towards prosecution evidence, especially non-physical evidence, and discuss the death penalty, with Grisham expressing strong opposition due to the risk of executing innocent people. The episode also touches upon the wrongful convictions of Tommy Ward and Carl Fontenot in a separate case in Ada, emphasizing systemic failures and the devastating impact on the wrongfully accused and their communities.
