### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Lucinda Heights-Clayba, a former bilingual teacher who was wrongfully convicted of child sexual abuse. Lucinda describes how a therapeutic tapping technique she used on a disruptive third-grade student was misinterpreted as sexual assault, leading to her arrest and conviction. Despite a lack of evidence and the school counselor's initial assessment finding no cause for concern, the District Attorney aggressively pursued the case. Lucinda was sentenced to 75 months in prison, enduring a harrowing experience as a wrongly convicted sex offender.  Her conviction was eventually overturned on appeal, highlighting systemic issues such as prosecutorial misconduct, inadequate investigation, and the devastating impact of false accusations. Lucinda emphasizes the urgent need for justice system reform and public awareness to prevent similar tragedies.
