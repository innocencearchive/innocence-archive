### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction focuses on the case of Dwayne Dunn, wrongfully convicted of murdering his neighbor Angel Torres in Elkhart, Indiana, in 2008. Despite evidence pointing to an accidental fall down a rickety staircase and initial police detectives closing the case, prosecutor Vicki Becker pursued charges.  Dwayne was convicted based on questionable blood spatter analysis and the testimony of a second medical examiner who contradicted the initial autopsy.  Ineffective trial counsel further hampered his defense.  After years of self-advocacy and the work of post-conviction attorneys including John Chenoweth, Michael Osprook, and Alex Dolan, Dwayne's conviction was overturned in federal court in 2022, highlighting systemic failures within the Elkhart County justice system, including reliance on junk science and prosecutorial overreach. The episode underscores the long fight for exoneration and the ongoing challenges exonerees face upon release.
