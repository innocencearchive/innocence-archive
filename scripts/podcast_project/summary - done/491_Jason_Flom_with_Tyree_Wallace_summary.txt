### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction details the harrowing story of Tyree Wallace, wrongfully convicted of second-degree felony murder in Philadelphia in 1999. Despite the lack of physical evidence and inconsistencies in witness testimonies, including recantations and coerced statements, Tyree was sentenced to life without parole for a crime he did not commit. The podcast exposes systemic issues such as aggressive policing, prosecutorial misconduct, and the weaponization of the felony murder rule.  After serving over two decades, Tyree's case gained renewed attention through the Pennsylvania Justice Project and pro bono legal counsel, leading to his release after resentencing in 2024.  However, the fight for his full exoneration and to clear his name continues, highlighting the ongoing struggle for justice for the wrongfully convicted and the need for systemic reform. Tyree's story underscores the devastating impact of wrongful convictions and his resilience and commitment to fighting for justice for himself and others.
