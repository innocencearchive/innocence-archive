### Basic Podcast Information

- **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction, False Confessions, tells the harrowing story of Michael Hash and Eric Weekly, childhood friends wrongly accused of the 1996 murder of Thelma Scroggins in Culpepper County, Virginia.  Police coerced a false confession from Eric, who implicated Michael and Jason Klobe. Despite Jason's acquittal due to an alibi, Michael was convicted based on Eric's false testimony and fabricated evidence from a jailhouse informant. Michael's mother, Pam Hash, tirelessly fought for his freedom, uncovering the prosecutor's deceit regarding the informant's deal and persuading Eric to recant his false confession. After nearly 12 years of wrongful imprisonment, Michael was exonerated.  The episode underscores the devastating consequences of false confessions, prosecutorial misconduct, and the long road to justice for the wrongfully convicted, while also highlighting the enduring power of a mother's love and friendship. Eric Weekly, despite his crucial recantation, remains convicted of second-degree murder and seeks a pardon.
