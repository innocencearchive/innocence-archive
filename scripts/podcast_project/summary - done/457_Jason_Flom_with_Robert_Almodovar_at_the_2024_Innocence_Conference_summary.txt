### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features the story of Robert Almatovar, wrongfully convicted in 1984 for a drive-by shooting in Chicago.  Robert and his co-defendant William de Guan were convicted based on manipulated eyewitness identifications by the notoriously corrupt Chicago police detective Ronaldo Guevara, despite a strong alibi. The podcast details Robert's upbringing, the circumstances of his arrest, the trial, and his eventual exoneration after serving over two decades.  It highlights the systemic issues within the Chicago Police Department, particularly Guevara's pattern of misconduct and framing innocent individuals. The episode emphasizes the crucial role of community support, especially from women in Robert's family, and the tireless legal efforts of Jennifer Bonjean in securing his freedom.  It underscores the devastating impact of wrongful convictions and the long fight for justice.
