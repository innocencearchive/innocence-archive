### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features the story of Michelle Morrison, who was wrongfully convicted of felony murder in Georgia in 2009 and sentenced to life plus five years, despite not being directly involved in the crime.  The podcast details the events leading to her conviction, highlighting her presence at the scene but lack of participation in the shooting.  It emphasizes the tireless fight of her mother, Cynthia Holland, for Michelle's release and the systemic injustices within the legal system that led to Michelle's wrongful imprisonment.  Michelle and Cynthia recount their experiences with the legal system, the emotional toll of incarceration, and their journey towards exoneration through the efforts of a conviction integrity unit. The episode underscores the importance of conviction integrity units and advocates for justice system reform, while also highlighting Michelle's personal growth and advocacy for others still incarcerated. Ultimately, Michelle was released in August 2022 after serving nearly 13 years, becoming the first woman released under Fulton County's new conviction integrity unit.
