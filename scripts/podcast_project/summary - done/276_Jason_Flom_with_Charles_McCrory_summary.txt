### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction details the case of Charles McCurry, wrongfully convicted in 1985 for the murder of his estranged wife, Julie Macquarie.  The conviction hinged on bite mark analysis from Dr. Dick Suveron, later recanted as junk science. Despite a lack of other physical evidence linking McCurry to the crime, dubious eyewitness testimony, and exculpatory evidence like hairs at the crime scene not belonging to him, he was convicted and sentenced to life.  The episode highlights the devastating impact of junk science in court, the flaws in the justice system, and McCurry's ongoing fight for exoneration with the help of the Innocence Project and the Southern Center for Human Rights.  The podcast emphasizes systemic issues and calls for public awareness and action to support McCurry's case and broader wrongful conviction reform. Despite recanted testimony and undermined evidence, McCurry remains incarcerated, showcasing the immense challenges in overturning wrongful convictions even with compelling evidence of innocence.
