### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction, False Confessions, tells the tragic story of Thomas Cogdel, a 12-year-old boy from Camden, Arkansas, wrongfully convicted of murdering his 11-year-old sister, Kaylee.  Police interrogated Thomas for hours without a lawyer or parent present, using manipulative tactics including lies and false promises, ultimately leading to a coerced confession after a lengthy off-camera period. Despite no corroborating evidence and inconsistencies in his confession, Thomas was convicted based solely on this false confession.  His conviction was eventually overturned by the Arkansas Supreme Court due to a Miranda rights waiver issue, highlighting the vulnerability of juveniles during police interrogations and the dangers of false confessions, especially from children.  The episode underscores the need for legal reforms to protect juveniles during interrogations and prevent similar injustices. Thomas was eventually exonerated and released, but the true perpetrator of Kaylee's murder remains unknown, and justice for Kaylee is still outstanding.
