### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction tells the harrowing story of Clarence Harrison, who was wrongfully convicted of rape and sentenced to life plus 40 years in Georgia in 1987.  The episode details the flawed investigation, including fabricated evidence by a police officer with a past grudge, and the suggestive identification by the victim. Clarence spent almost 18 years in prison, enduring immense hardship and loss of hope.  His exoneration came through DNA testing facilitated by the Georgia Innocence Project.  The podcast highlights the devastating impact of wrongful convictions on individuals and the systemic issues within the justice system. Clarence's story is one of resilience and eventual triumph, underscored by his marriage to Yvonne Sellers, who played a crucial role in rekindling his hope and connecting him with legal assistance.  The episode concludes with a call to action to support exonerees and advocate for systemic changes.
