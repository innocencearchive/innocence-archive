### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features the harrowing story of Keith Washington, a decorated Army Lieutenant Colonel, police officer, and Homeland Security official, who was wrongfully convicted of involuntary manslaughter and sentenced to 45 years in prison for defending himself, his wife, and daughter during a home invasion in 2007.  Despite overwhelming evidence supporting self-defense and contradicting the account of Robert White, a 12-time convicted felon involved in the incident, Washington was prosecuted and convicted due to prosecutorial misconduct and ambition.  The prosecution team allegedly suppressed evidence, lied to the jury, and ignored White's extensive criminal history and inconsistent statements to secure a conviction. After serving 13 years, Washington was released thanks to the efforts of his legal team and Georgetown University's Making an Exoneree program, but continues to fight for full exoneration. The episode highlights systemic issues within the criminal justice system, including prosecutorial overreach, media influence, and the devastating impact of wrongful convictions on individuals and their families, emphasizing the urgent need for reform and support for exonerees.
