### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction: False Confessions tells the tragic story of Taina Porra, a 16-year-old Maori boy wrongfully convicted of the rape and murder of Susan Burdette in New Zealand in 1994.  Despite DNA evidence pointing to serial rapist Malcolm Raywa, Porra was convicted based on a coerced false confession after a four-day interrogation.  He was retried and convicted again even after Raywa was identified and convicted for the same crime and others.  Private investigator Tim McKinnon took up Porra's case, uncovering the flawed interrogation, unreliable witnesses, and Porra's Fetal Alcohol Spectrum Disorder (FASD), which made him highly suggestible. After 22 years in prison, Porra was finally exonerated in 2015 thanks to the Privy Council and the tireless efforts of McKinnon and his team.  His case highlights systemic issues with interrogation techniques and the devastating impact of wrongful convictions, even prompting the creation of the CCRC in New Zealand.
