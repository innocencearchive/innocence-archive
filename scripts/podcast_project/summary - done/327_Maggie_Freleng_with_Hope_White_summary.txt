### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling" tells the story of Hope White, wrongfully convicted of the 2008 murder of Julie Burchett in Monticello, Kentucky. Despite flimsy witness testimonies from individuals with pending charges and a lack of forensic evidence linking her to the crime, Hope was convicted twice and spent over 13 years in prison. The podcast highlights the inconsistencies in the prosecution's case, including contradictory witness statements about a party where the murder allegedly occurred and the failure to investigate other potential suspects.  The Kentucky Innocence Project is now working on Hope's case, focusing on untested hair evidence and potential police corruption, aiming to prove her innocence and help her achieve her dream of becoming an attorney to assist others wrongfully convicted.
