### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Tim Tyler, who received a double life sentence in 1992 for a non-violent LSD offense.  The episode details the extreme severity of his sentence, the flawed legal advice he received, and the devastating impact on his life and family, including his father who also received a 10-year sentence related to Tim's case.  Tim recounts his harrowing experiences in maximum-security prisons, the unexpected kindness of some fellow inmates, and the profound deprivation and isolation he endured.  The podcast highlights the systemic issues of mandatory minimum sentencing and the criminalization of drug use, advocating for drug policy reform and clemency.  Ultimately, Tim received clemency from President Obama after serving 26 years, and his story serves as a powerful example of sentencing injustice and the urgent need for criminal justice reform.  The episode concludes with a call to action to support organizations like FAMM and CanDo Clemency and to advocate for clemency for others unjustly incarcerated.
