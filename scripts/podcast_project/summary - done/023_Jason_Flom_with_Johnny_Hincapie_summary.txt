### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom features Johnny In Capier, who recounts his wrongful conviction in the notorious 1990 Brian Watkins murder case in New York City.  In Capier details how, at 18 years old, he was coerced into a false confession by abusive detectives, leading to a 25-year prison sentence despite no evidence linking him to the crime. The podcast explores the systemic issues that contributed to his wrongful conviction, including police misconduct, pressure to solve high-profile cases, and the impact of false confessions.  In Capier emphasizes the devastating consequences of his wrongful imprisonment, the lack of support for exonerees post-release, and his advocacy for justice reform, particularly the implementation of mandatory video recording of interrogations to prevent future false confessions. He highlights the crucial role of investigators Bill Hughes and Robert Denison in uncovering the evidence that led to his exoneration after 25 years. The episode underscores the urgent need for continued reform within the justice system and emphasizes the resilience and advocacy of wrongfully convicted individuals like Johnny In Capier.
