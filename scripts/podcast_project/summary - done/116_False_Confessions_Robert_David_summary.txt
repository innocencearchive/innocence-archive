### Basic Podcast Information

- **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction, False Confessions" details the case of Robert Davis, a young man falsely accused of murder and arson in 2003. Hosts Lauren Eyreider and Steve Drizen use Davis's case to illustrate the dangers of coercive interrogation techniques and how they can lead to false confessions. The episode recounts how police manipulated Davis through lies and psychological pressure into confessing to a crime he didn't commit, despite initial confessions from the actual perpetrators, Rocky and Jessica Fugit.  Davis took an Alford plea to avoid a potentially harsher sentence, serving 12 years before being pardoned based on the recantations of the Fugits and expert analysis of his interrogation. The episode underscores systemic issues within the justice system regarding interrogation methods and the devastating impact of wrongful convictions.
