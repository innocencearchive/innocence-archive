### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features the story of Terrence Rice, who was wrongfully convicted in 1991 for attempted murder of police officers and weapons possession, crimes that evidence suggests never occurred.  Rice spent 31 years in prison based on fabricated police testimony and despite a lack of physical evidence. The podcast details the events of his arrest, the flawed trial where exculpatory witnesses were not allowed to testify, and the subsequent decades of incarceration.  It highlights systemic issues within the justice system, including police misconduct, ineffective legal representation, and the challenges faced by the wrongfully convicted even when released on parole.  The episode culminates with Rice's release due to a sentence reduction, though he remains on parole and seeks full exoneration, emphasizing the ongoing personal and legal battles despite his freedom from prison walls. The host, Jason Flomond, expresses outrage at the injustice and urges listeners to support Terrence Rice's petition for full exoneration.
