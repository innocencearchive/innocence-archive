### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction" features Jafama Coleman, who was wrongfully convicted of first-degree murder in 2003 and served 19 years in prison. The episode details the faulty eyewitness testimony and lack of physical evidence that led to his conviction for a drive-by shooting he did not commit.  Despite maintaining his innocence and having an alibi, Coleman was found guilty.  While incarcerated, he studied law and fought his case.  Eventually, Jessica Jacobs, a true crime enthusiast, and attorney Ellen Eggers joined his fight for freedom. Through their combined efforts, including uncovering crucial evidence and a witness recantation, Coleman was exonerated in 2024. The podcast explores Coleman's life before, during, and after his wrongful conviction, highlighting his resilience, his relationship with his daughter as a source of strength, and his friendship with Jessica Jacobs.
