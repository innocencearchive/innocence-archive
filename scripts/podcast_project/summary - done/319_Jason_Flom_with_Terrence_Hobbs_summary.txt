### Basic Podcast Information

- **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction details the harrowing story of Terrence Hobbs, who suffered a double wrongful conviction for bank robbery and a double homicide he did not commit.  Hobbs was initially targeted for the murders of Davana Byers and Leon Porter despite a lack of physical evidence and a more obvious suspect, drug kingpin Nathaniel Skeet Richardson.  False testimony from incentivized witnesses, including Eric Cook in the bank robbery case and five individuals in the homicide case, played a crucial role in both convictions.  Despite alibis, lack of motive, and contradictory witness statements, Hobbs was sentenced to a combined 48 years plus two life sentences.  The episode highlights systemic issues of police and prosecutorial misconduct, ineffective counsel, and the devastating impact of wrongful convictions, emphasizing Hobbs's ongoing fight for exoneration with the help of Lenny Singleton and his family's unwavering support.
