### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom details the harrowing case of James King, who was wrongfully convicted of sexual assault and spent 25 years in prison. The episode unfolds the series of lies and inconsistencies in the testimony of the alleged victim, a 13-year-old girl named Jamie, and the questionable medical testimony used to corroborate her story. Despite clear evidence of falsehoods, including contradictory statements about the location and type of carpet where the alleged assault occurred and the lack of semen evidence, King was convicted twice. The podcast highlights the systemic failures within the justice system, including ineffective legal counsel and prosecutorial decisions that led to this tragic miscarriage of justice. The episode features interviews with King himself and his attorney, Ian Graham, underscoring the devastating impact of wrongful convictions on individuals and their families and calling for public support to help exonerate James King.
