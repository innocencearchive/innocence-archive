### Basic Podcast Information

- **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom" is part of a special miniseries during COVID-19, featuring Damien Echols, who was wrongfully convicted and spent 18 years on death row.  Echols discusses how his experiences in prison, particularly extreme isolation, have equipped him to cope with the pandemic quarantine. He shares strategies for maintaining sanity and finding purpose in confinement, such as focusing on the present, creating a structured daily routine with activities like cleaning, exercise, and reading, and cultivating an internal world through spiritual practices and art.  Echols emphasizes the importance of shifting perspective, finding joy and humor even in dark times, and practicing gratitude.  He connects his past experiences to the current global situation, offering advice on resilience and mental well-being during isolation. The episode concludes with a message of thanks to those practicing self-isolation and highlights the importance of supporting organizations like the Innocence Project.
