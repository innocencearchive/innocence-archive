### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling" tells the story of Ken Nixon, who was wrongfully convicted of arson and murder in Detroit in 2005 and spent 16 years in prison.  Ken was arrested after a Molotov cocktail attack killed two children, based on the unreliable testimony of a 13-year-old witness who claimed to see him at the scene.  Despite alibis from his girlfriend and her family, and questionable evidence including a jailhouse informant, Ken was convicted.  The Western Michigan University Cooley Innocence Project took on his case, uncovering withheld memos questioning the witness's reliability and highlighting misconduct from involved officials.  Ken's conviction was finally vacated in 2021, and he is now an advocate for other exonerees through the Organization of Exonarees.
