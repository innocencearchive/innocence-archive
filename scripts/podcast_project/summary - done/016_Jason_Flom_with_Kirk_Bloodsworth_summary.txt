### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom" features an interview with Kirk Bloodsworth, the first DNA exoneree from death row in the United States. Bloodsworth recounts his wrongful conviction in 1984 for the murder and sexual assault of a nine-year-old girl, a crime he did not commit. He details the flawed investigation, including eyewitness misidentification and ineffective defense counsel, which led to his initial death sentence and subsequent double life sentence after a retrial.  After nearly nine years in prison, DNA testing, unavailable at the time of his trial, exonerated him in 1993.  Bloodsworth discusses the horrors of prison, the fight for his freedom, and his eventual exoneration thanks to DNA evidence.  Since his release, he has become a prominent activist against wrongful convictions and the death penalty, and instrumental in establishing the Kirk Bloodsworth Post-Conviction DNA Testing Program.
