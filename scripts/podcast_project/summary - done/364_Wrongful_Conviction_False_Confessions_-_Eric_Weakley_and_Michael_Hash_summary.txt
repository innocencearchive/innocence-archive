### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction: False Confessions details the wrongful convictions of Eric Weekly and Michael Hash for the murder of Thelma Scroggins in Culpepper County, Virginia in 1996.  Fueled by a coerced false confession from Eric Weekly and the manufactured testimony of a jailhouse informant, Michael Hash was wrongly convicted and sentenced to life.  The episode highlights the relentless efforts of Michael's mother, Pam Hash, who uncovered crucial evidence of prosecutorial misconduct and the jailhouse informant's deal.  Eric Weekly eventually recanted his false confession, and with the help of the Mid-Atlantic Innocence Project, Michael Hash was exonerated after serving nearly 12 years.  The podcast emphasizes the devastating impact of false confessions, the importance of family advocacy, and the long fight for justice in wrongful conviction cases, culminating in Eric Weekly's pardon in 2022.
