### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Nora Jackson, who recounts her harrowing experience of being wrongfully convicted for the murder of her mother.  Despite facing immense trauma from the unsolved murder of her father and then her mother, Nora was targeted by law enforcement and prosecutors who engaged in misconduct, including withholding key exculpatory evidence like DNA analysis that excluded her.  The Tennessee Supreme Court eventually overturned her conviction after she served 11 years, citing egregious prosecutorial misconduct.  However, instead of full exoneration, Nora was pressured into taking an Alford plea to voluntary manslaughter to secure her release, highlighting the immense pressure and flawed choices faced by wrongfully convicted individuals even after legal victories. Nora discusses the devastating impact of the wrongful conviction on her life and her eventual journey towards healing and advocacy for other exonerees through her organization "Meet Your Mentor". The episode underscores systemic issues within the justice system and the profound personal cost of wrongful convictions.
