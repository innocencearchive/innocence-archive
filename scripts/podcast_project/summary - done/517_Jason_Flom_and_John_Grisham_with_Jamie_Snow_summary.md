### Executive Summary

This episode details the wrongful conviction of Jamie Snow for the 1991 fatal shooting of gas station attendant Bill Little in Bloomington, Illinois. Despite a lack of physical evidence connecting him to the crime and passing a polygraph early in the investigation, Snow was convicted in 2001 based on flawed eyewitness accounts and the testimony of numerous incentivized jailhouse informants. Key witness Danny Martinez initially failed to identify Snow and his account was contradicted by police officers present at the scene, facts withheld from the defense along with informant deals and failed polygraphs. Many informants have since recanted, citing coercion or deals for leniency. Snow's sister-in-law, accused as a getaway driver, refused a deal, maintained her innocence, and was acquitted. Represented by the Exoneration Project, Snow continues to fight his life sentence, seeking modern forensic testing which the state opposes, after nearly 25 years of incarceration.
