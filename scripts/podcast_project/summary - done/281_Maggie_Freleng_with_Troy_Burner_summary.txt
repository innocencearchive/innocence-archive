### Basic Podcast Information

- **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling" details the wrongful conviction of Troy Burner for the 1990 murder of Michael Wilson.  Despite having an alibi and no direct evidence linking him to the crime, Troy was implicated by informants seeking leniency in their own cases.  The prosecution relied on contradictory testimonies from these informants, leading to Troy's conviction and a 30-years-to-life sentence.  After nearly 25 years of incarceration, new evidence emerged, including a recantation from a key informant and alibi witnesses.  The Mid-Atlantic Innocence Project took on his case, and Troy was eventually released on time served in 2018 and fully exonerated in 2020.  His story highlights the dangers of unreliable informant testimony and the long fight for justice after wrongful conviction.  Since his release, Troy has become an advocate for criminal justice reform and found personal fulfillment.
