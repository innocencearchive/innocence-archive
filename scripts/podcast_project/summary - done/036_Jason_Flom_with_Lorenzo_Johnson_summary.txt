### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Lorenzo Johnson, who spent 22 years in prison for a murder he maintains he did not commit in Harrisburg, Pennsylvania in 1995.  Despite being in New York at the time of the crime, Johnson was convicted based on unreliable eyewitness testimony and prosecutorial misconduct.  Key exculpatory evidence, including statements discrediting the main witness and police reports identifying her as a suspect, was withheld from the defense.  Johnson was initially released in 2012 due to insufficient evidence, but the Supreme Court controversially reinstated his conviction.  After years of legal battles and facing the prospect of further appeals, Johnson accepted a plea deal to a lesser charge to secure his release in July 2017, although he maintains his innocence and expresses bitterness about the plea and the systemic injustices he endured.  He now advocates for criminal justice reform and supports other wrongfully convicted individuals.
