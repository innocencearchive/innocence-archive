### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Leroy Harris, who shares his harrowing 29-year ordeal of wrongful imprisonment. Arrested initially on a warrant he knew nothing about, his case spiraled into a nightmare of legal missteps, including being told to leave the courthouse and then charged with escape.  Wrongfully convicted of robbery and sexual assault based on suggestive identification and ineffective counsel, Harris details the systemic failures and vindictive prosecution that led to his decades-long incarceration.  Despite DNA evidence and witness recantations eventually proving his innocence, the justice system offered him a release only through an Alford plea to a fictional kidnapping charge, allowing them to avoid fully admitting their error and preventing him from suing.  Harris highlights the personal toll of wrongful conviction and his dedication to helping others through his organization, the Help the Needy Foundation.  His story underscores the potential for injustice within the system and the importance of organizations like the Innocence Project.
