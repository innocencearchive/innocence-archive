### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction delves into the tragic case of Willie and Ashanti Smith, two cousins wrongfully convicted of murder in Cleveland in 1995.  The brothers were teenagers when they were accused of killing Reggie Lewis, a neighborhood terror.  Their cousin, William Marshall, provided coerced testimony, influenced by police threats and family pressure, falsely implicating them. Despite alibi evidence and later recantation by Marshall, the Smith brothers spent nearly three decades in prison. The podcast highlights systemic issues within the justice system, including ineffective defense, witness tampering, and the devastating impact of wrongful convictions on individuals and families.  The episode culminates with the hope of a new hearing based on Marshall's recantation and a call for community support as Willie and Ashanti continue their fight for freedom and justice.
