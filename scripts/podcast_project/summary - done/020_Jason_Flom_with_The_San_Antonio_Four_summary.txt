-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom" features a live recording with the "San Antonio 4" – Anna Vasquez, Cassandra Rivera, Elizabeth Ramirez, and Kristi Mayhew – and their attorney Mike Ware. The episode details their lives before, during, and after their wrongful conviction, highlighting the devastating impact of the injustice and their eventual exoneration in 2018. The conversation explores the systemic issues contributing to wrongful convictions, including flaws in forensic science, prejudice, and the pressure to close cases quickly.  The women share their personal experiences of faith, hope, and love that sustained them through their ordeal, and advocate for reforms within the justice system. The episode concludes with a call to support the Innocence Project of Texas and encourages listeners to be informed and skeptical jurors.
