-   **Narrative of Original Crime**: Melissa Kalyzinski was accused of causing fatal head injuries to 16-month-old Benjamin King while he was in her care at a daycare center. The prosecution argued she inflicted blunt force trauma leading to a skull fracture and intracranial bleeding.
-   **Key Evidence at Trial (Prosecution)**:
    -   Melissa's confession (obtained after 14-hour interrogation).
    -   Second autopsy findings by Dr. Choy, ruling the death a homicide due to blunt force head trauma and skull fracture.

However, the defense was hampered by poor quality x-ray images and the state's rebuttal expert.
-   **Key Evidence for Exoneration/Change in Legal Status**:
    -   Recantation of confession by Melissa immediately after interrogation.
    -   Discovery of clear TIFF images of Ben's skull, showing no skull fracture.

    -   Psychiatric evaluation highlighting Melissa's PTSD and suggestibility contributing to false confession.
