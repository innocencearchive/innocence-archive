### Basic Podcast Information

- **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction, part one of a series on Barry Beach, details his wrongful conviction for the murder of Kim Nice based on a nonsensical false confession. Despite being cleared as a suspect initially by physical evidence, Beach was sentenced to 100 years.  The podcast recounts Beach's decades-long fight for freedom from prison, including his self-education in law and relentless pursuit of evidence.  Investigator Richard Hepburn from Centurion Ministries joins to explain the reinvestigation, highlighting a crucial Brady violation involving a suppressed witness statement and physical evidence.  The episode reveals the pervasive local knowledge of other potential suspects, a group of women, and the systemic failures within the Montana justice system that perpetuated Beach's wrongful imprisonment.  Despite being released through executive clemency, Barry Beach remains convicted and continues to seek full exoneration.
