### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom delves into the egregious case of Andy Royer, wrongfully convicted in Elkhart, Indiana, for the murder of Helen Saylor. The episode exposes systemic issues within the Elkhart Police Department and prosecutor's office, highlighting a pattern of misconduct leading to wrongful convictions. Andy Royer, a disabled man, was coerced into a false confession after aggressive interrogation, while a vulnerable witness was pressured to fabricate testimony against Royer and his co-defendant, Lana Kanan.  False fingerprint analysis further cemented their wrongful conviction. The podcast details the relentless efforts of the Notre Dame Exoneration Justice Clinic, particularly attorney Elliot Slosar, to overturn Royer's conviction, revealing shocking admissions of misconduct from involved detectives and the pervasive systemic failures within Elkhart's justice system. Ultimately, Royer was exonerated after serving years in prison, underscoring the urgent need for systemic reform and accountability.

-   **Systemic Issues Identified**:
    -   Police misconduct in Elkhart PD, including coercive interrogations, fabrication of evidence, and perjury.
    -   Prosecutorial misconduct, including using fabricated evidence and failing to disclose exculpatory information.
    -   Lack of proper training and oversight within the Elkhart County Sheriff's Department (fingerprint analysis).
    -   Ineffective assistance of counsel in the original trials.
    -   Judicial bias and systemic protection of law enforcement within Elkhart's legal system.
    -   The "blue wall of silence" hindering accountability within law enforcement.
    -   Perverse incentives within the prison system related to inmate labor (13th Amendment loophole).

-   **Policy Recommendations/Calls to Action**:
    -   Mandatory video recording of all police interactions, including interrogations, witness interviews, and identification procedures.
    -   Independent review of convictions from Elkhart County due to systemic misconduct concerns.
    -   Criminal investigation and prosecution of Detective Conway, Dennis Chapman, and potentially Prosecutor Vicki Becker for their roles in wrongful convictions.
    -   Calls for reform in prosecutorial accountability and police training to prevent coercive tactics and evidence fabrication.
    -   Support for organizations like the Notre Dame Exoneration Justice Clinic and The Last Mile program.
    -   Urge voters to hold accountable prosecutors like Vicki Becker who contribute to wrongful convictions.
