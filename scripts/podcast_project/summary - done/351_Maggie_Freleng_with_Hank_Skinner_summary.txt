### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeland" revisits the case of Hank Skinner, who was convicted and sentenced to death for the 1993 murders of his girlfriend and her two sons in Pampa, Texas. Despite maintaining his innocence and presenting evidence of extreme intoxication at the time of the crime, Skinner spent nearly 30 years on death row. The podcast details the crime, the flawed investigation, the trial, and the key testimonies that led to his conviction. It highlights the lack of forensic evidence linking Skinner to the murders and points to potential alternative suspects, particularly Twyla Busby's uncle, Robert Denal. The episode also features interviews with Skinner's wife, Sandrine, and his lawyer, Rob Owen, emphasizing Skinner's continued fight for exoneration and the systemic issues within the justice system.  Hank Skinner died in February 2024 while still maintaining his innocence.
