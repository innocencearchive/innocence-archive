### Executive Summary

This episode of Wrongful Conviction details the case of Manfred Younger (aka Baby J), convicted in 2011 for the 2007 murder of Tiera Presley and attempted murder of survivor Adrienne Jackson in Camden, New Jersey. Despite Jackson initially being unable to identify his attackers and evidence suggesting Younger was incarcerated during a time Jackson later claimed to have seen him, Younger was implicated primarily through suggestive identification procedures. The conviction heavily relied on a single-photo identification by Jackson, prompted by investigator Diane Wilson, and the testimonies of two incentivized jailhouse informants, Jamie Channer and Jamal Gibbs. These informants provided factually inconsistent accounts and later evidence revealed they received undisclosed benefits for their testimony. Detective Martin Devlin, previously linked to questionable tactics in Philadelphia, supervised the investigation, highlighting a pattern of misconduct. The episode underscores systemic problems in Camden, including reliance on unreliable informants and suggestive IDs, and details ongoing efforts by attorney Justin Bonas to prove Younger's innocence using newly discovered evidence and witness statements.
