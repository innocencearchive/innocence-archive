### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction tells the harrowing story of Glenn Simmons, who was wrongfully convicted of a 1974 robbery homicide in Edmond, Oklahoma, and spent over 48 years in prison. Despite being in Louisiana at the time of the crime and significantly differing in description from the eyewitness account, Glenn was convicted based on a questionable eyewitness identification.  Crucially, a hidden police report surfaced decades later, revealing the eyewitness actually identified different suspects, Leonard and Delbert Patterson, who matched her initial description and were known criminals.  The podcast details the failures of his defense attorney, the suppression of exculpatory evidence, and the long arduous journey to Glenn's exoneration in December 2023.  The episode underscores systemic issues within the criminal justice system, including eyewitness misidentification, police misconduct, and the devastating impact of wrongful convictions, highlighting Glenn's resilience and his future advocacy for reintegration support for exonerees.
