### Basic Podcast Information

- **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction, False Confessions, tells the story of Chris Tapp, who at 20 years old was coerced into falsely confessing to the rape and murder of Angie Dodge in Idaho Falls in 1996.  Despite DNA evidence at the scene not matching Tapp, he was convicted based on a confession obtained after a grueling 25-hour interrogation.  Angie Dodge's mother, Carol Dodge, initially believed in Tapp's guilt but eventually became convinced of his innocence after reviewing interrogation tapes and researching false confessions.  Carol Dodge became a tireless advocate for Tapp's exoneration, pushing for further investigation and the use of genetic genealogy.  Her persistence, combined with legal efforts and new DNA technology, ultimately led to Tapp's release after 20 years and the identification of the actual perpetrator, Brian Dripps, through genetic genealogy. The episode highlights the devastating impact of false confessions and the remarkable role of a victim's family member in seeking justice and truth.
