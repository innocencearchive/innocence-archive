### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom discusses the case of Eddie Lee Howard, who was wrongfully convicted of murder in 1994 and sentenced to death based on discredited bite mark evidence presented by Michael West.  The episode features guests Chris Fabricant, Tucker Carrington, and John Grisham, who detail the flawed investigation, the reliance on junk science, and the systemic failures that led to Howard's wrongful imprisonment for decades.  The podcast highlights the role of corrupt medical examiner Dr. Hayne and fraudulent forensic dentist Michael West in securing convictions.  It emphasizes the lack of physical evidence and the manipulation of forensic science to build a case against Howard.  Despite multiple appeals and the exposure of West's fraudulent practices, Howard remained incarcerated until his exoneration in 2020, thanks to the persistent efforts of the Mississippi Innocence Project and others. The episode concludes with reflections on the profound impact of wrongful incarceration and the ongoing fight for forensic science reform.
