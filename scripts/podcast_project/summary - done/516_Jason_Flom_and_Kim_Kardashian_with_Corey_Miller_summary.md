### Executive Summary

This episode of Wrongful Conviction details the case of Corey Miller, the hip-hop artist known as C-Murder, who was convicted for the 2002 murder of 16-year-old Steve Thomas at a Louisiana nightclub. Hosted by Jason Flom with guests Corey Miller, Kim Kardashian, and appellate attorney Jane Hogan, the episode explores the controversial conviction based on a 10-2 non-unanimous jury verdict. It highlights significant issues including inconsistent eyewitness accounts, alleged police coercion by Detective Kloger leading key witnesses to implicate Miller, and the subsequent recantations of those witnesses. Despite DNA evidence excluding Miller and new analyses challenging the prosecution's narrative, Miller remains incarcerated after over 20 years, with post-conviction relief efforts repeatedly denied without evidentiary hearings. The episode calls for action, including signing petitions and advocating for the retroactive application of Louisiana's unanimous jury law.
