### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom" features Dewey Bozella, who was wrongfully convicted for the murder of Emma Crapser and spent over 26 years in prison. The episode details Dewey's traumatic childhood, his boxing journey, and the events leading to his wrongful conviction in 1983, despite a lack of evidence and the presence of evidence pointing to other perpetrators.  It highlights the prosecutorial misconduct, reliance on coerced and incentivized witness testimony, and racial bias that contributed to his conviction.  Despite two trials and offers of plea deals, Dewey maintained his innocence.  The podcast further explores his incredible transformation in prison, becoming a boxing champion and earning multiple degrees.  Ultimately, new evidence and the admission of the arresting officer led to his exoneration in 2009, after which he achieved his dream of becoming a professional boxer at age 52 and received the Arthur Ashe Courage Award. Dewey's story is a powerful testament to resilience, forgiveness, and the fight for justice against a flawed system.
