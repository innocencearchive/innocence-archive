### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction details the case of Raymond Allen Warren, wrongfully convicted in 1995 for murder based on unreliable gunshot residue evidence and coerced testimony from two young friends.  At 16, Raymond was arrested after a positive GSR test on his non-dominant hand, despite having been fixing a scooter with brake pads, a known source of false positives.  His friends, Antonio and Chante, were coerced by police into falsely implicating him.  Despite both friends recanting their testimonies years later and the scientific community debunking GSR testing, Raymond remains incarcerated due to procedural hurdles and time limitations on filing motions for new evidence. The episode highlights systemic issues in the justice system, particularly the devastating impact of junk science and the difficulties faced by wrongfully convicted individuals in achieving exoneration, even with compelling evidence of innocence.
