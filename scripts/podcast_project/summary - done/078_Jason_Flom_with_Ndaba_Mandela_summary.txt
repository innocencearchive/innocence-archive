-   **Systemic Issues Identified**:

    -   Harsh sentencing laws, particularly in the US (mandatory minimums, disproportionate sentences for non-violent crimes).
    -   Flaws in the US bail system, leading to pre-trial detention for those who cannot afford bail.
    -   Potential for corruption and poor conditions in prisons (South Africa example).
    -   Cycle of poverty and crime.
    -   Lack of focus on rehabilitation in some prison systems (US).

-   **Policy Recommendations/Calls to Action**:

    -   Reforming sentencing laws (eliminating mandatory minimums).
    -   Bail reform.
    -   Focus on rehabilitation in prisons.
    -   Addressing poverty as a crime prevention strategy.
    -   Importance of education and mentorship for youth.
    -   Supporting organizations like the Innocence Project.
