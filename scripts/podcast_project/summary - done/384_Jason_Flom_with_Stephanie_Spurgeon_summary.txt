### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Systemic Issues Identified**:
    -   Flaws in the criminal justice system's reliance on "shaken baby syndrome" and "abusive head trauma" diagnoses.
    -   Over-reliance on certain medical experts ("child abuse pediatricians") with potential biases and misapplication of science.
    -   Ineffective assistance of counsel in wrongful conviction cases.
    -   Difficulty in challenging established medical narratives in court, especially when presented by seemingly credible experts.
    -   Lack of understanding and consideration of alternative medical explanations for infant injuries and deaths.
    -   Potential for confirmation bias in investigations, leading to premature conclusions of abuse.
-   **Policy Recommendations/Calls to Action**:
    -   Call for reform in medical training to address the limitations and misapplications of SBS/AHT diagnoses.
    -   Urge law enforcement and medical professionals to be more diligent and less emotionally charged in investigations of child injuries, avoiding premature assumptions of abuse.
    -   Encourage public involvement in local elections, particularly for positions like state attorney, to promote prosecutorial accountability and reform.
    -   Support organizations like Innocence Projects to help those wrongfully convicted and advocate for systemic change.
