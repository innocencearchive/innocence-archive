### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction" features the case of Nelson Serrano, an Ecuadorian immigrant businessman wrongfully convicted of a quadruple murder in Florida in 1997. Despite an airtight alibi placing him in Atlanta at the time of the crime, and a lack of physical evidence, Serrano was illegally kidnapped from Ecuador and brought to Florida for trial.  Prosecutors presented a convoluted and scientifically implausible theory of guilt, relying on altered evidence and coerced testimony.  Serrano was convicted and sentenced to death in a non-unanimous jury decision. The podcast highlights the systemic failures in the justice system, including prosecutorial misconduct, inadequate defense, and the flaws of the death penalty in Florida, where a significant percentage of death row inmates have been exonerated. The episode calls for action to support Serrano's case and reform the criminal justice system to prevent future wrongful convictions.
