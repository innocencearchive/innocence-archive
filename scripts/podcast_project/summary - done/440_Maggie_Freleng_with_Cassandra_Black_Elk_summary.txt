### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling tells the story of Cassandra Black Elk, a young Cheyenne River Sioux Tribe mother wrongfully convicted of felony child neglect in North Dakota after her three-week-old baby, Starlight, died unexpectedly.  Police interrogated Cassie shortly after Starlight's death, pressuring her into a confession based on unfounded suspicions of shaken baby syndrome. Despite maintaining her innocence and lacking any medical evidence, Cassie's public defender urged her to take a plea deal to avoid a longer sentence. Cassie pled guilty and served 11 months in prison before finally obtaining the autopsy report which revealed Starlight died of SIDS, with no signs of neglect or abuse.  The Great North Innocence Project, along with the F5 Project, intervened, leading to Cassie's release after an evidentiary hearing. The state initially attempted to continue prosecution based on alcohol consumption, highlighting potential racial bias, before finally dismissing all charges, allowing Cassie to reunite with her daughters and begin rebuilding her life.
