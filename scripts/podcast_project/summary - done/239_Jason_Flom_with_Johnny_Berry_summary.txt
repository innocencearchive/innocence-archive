### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Johnny Berry, who was wrongfully convicted as a juvenile in Philadelphia for a 1994 murder he did not commit.  Berry recounts his life before and after his arrest, detailing the flawed investigation, false testimony from a co-defendant, and ineffective defense that led to his life sentence.  Despite a recantation from the co-defendant and evidence of his innocence, Berry spent nearly 25 years in prison due to prosecutorial misconduct and systemic issues.  The episode highlights the impact of Supreme Court rulings on juvenile life sentences which eventually led to Berry's release and subsequent exoneration thanks to a conviction integrity unit.  Berry emphasizes the need for parole eligibility for life sentences in Pennsylvania and discusses the lack of compensation for exonerees, while also sharing his positive life changes post-exoneration.
