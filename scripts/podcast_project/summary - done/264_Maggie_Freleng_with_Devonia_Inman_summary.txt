### Basic Podcast Information

- **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling" tells the story of Devanya Inman, wrongfully convicted of the 1998 murder of Donna Brown in Adele, Georgia. Despite having an alibi, flawed witness testimonies and a jailhouse informant led to his conviction and a life sentence. DNA evidence found years later on a mask from the crime scene exonerated Inman and pointed to Hercules Brown, a Taco Bell employee, as the actual perpetrator. However, Inman faced years of legal battles before his release in 2021, after serving 23 years. The podcast highlights the devastating impact of wrongful conviction on Inman's life and the systemic failures within the justice system, including reliance on unreliable witnesses and resistance to exonerating evidence. It also touches on the challenges exonerees face upon release and the need for better support for reintegration. The episode ends with a call to support Devanya Inman and local innocence organizations.
