The episode further explores his remarkable post-exoneration life, including his marriage to fellow exoneree Sonny Jacobs and their establishment of the Sonny Sanctuary to aid other exonerees, emphasizing themes of resilience, forgiveness, and the global nature of wrongful convictions.  Peter's story underscores systemic failures within the justice system and the profound personal impact of wrongful imprisonment.
