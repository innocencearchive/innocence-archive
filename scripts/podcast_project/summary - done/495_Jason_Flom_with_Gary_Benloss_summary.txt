### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction details the case of Gary Benloss, wrongfully convicted in 2003 for a 2002 murder in Brooklyn. Despite multiple eyewitnesses describing a shooter vastly different in appearance (shorter, darker skin, short hair) from Benloss (taller, lighter skin, large afro), and inconsistencies in the prosecution's case, Benloss was convicted based largely on the testimony of a potentially incentivized witness. The podcast highlights issues such as flawed eyewitness identification, police and prosecutorial misconduct, and ineffective defense counsel.  It underscores the devastating impact of wrongful convictions on individuals and their families, exemplified by Benloss's son's untimely death shortly after expressing hope for his father's release.  The episode serves as a call to action, urging listeners to support Gary Benloss's ongoing fight for exoneration and to engage in broader criminal justice reform. The episode also briefly mentions Gary's recent release on compassionate parole while he continues to fight to clear his name.
