### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features the story of Ken Wayne Empko, wrongfully convicted of rape in Michigan in 1994.  The episode details the flawed investigation, including a manipulated lineup and false testimony from a jailhouse informant coerced by the prosecutor and detective. Despite exculpatory serology evidence, Ken was convicted and sentenced to 40-60 years.  After nearly a decade in prison, the Cooley Innocence Project, led by attorney Gail Pamakoff, took on his case and secured DNA testing of the original rape kit, proving his innocence.  Ken was exonerated and released in 2003. The episode highlights the devastating impact of wrongful convictions and Ken's subsequent advocacy for criminal justice reform, including his work to pass a wrongful compensation bill in Michigan and his efforts to address systemic issues like false testimony and qualified immunity.
