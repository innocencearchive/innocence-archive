### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction tells the harrowing story of Chris Smith, wrongfully convicted of armed robbery in 2007 based on circumstantial evidence despite overwhelming evidence of his innocence.  The podcast details how police fixated on Chris due to a prior conviction and his girlfriend's car being used in the crime, ignoring the fact that the eyewitness description of the robber did not match Chris. Crucially, DNA evidence from the robber's costume and fingerprints pointed to Charles Allen, who even confessed on tape, but this was disregarded. Despite these clear indicators of innocence and a federal court order, Chris remained imprisoned for 12 years, facing judicial and prosecutorial resistance at every turn.  The episode highlights systemic issues in the justice system, including tunnel vision, judicial bias, and the immense difficulty of overturning wrongful convictions, even with strong evidence of innocence.  Ultimately, Chris was released but not fully exonerated at the time of this podcast recording, enduring prolonged legal battles and the deep psychological impact of wrongful imprisonment.
