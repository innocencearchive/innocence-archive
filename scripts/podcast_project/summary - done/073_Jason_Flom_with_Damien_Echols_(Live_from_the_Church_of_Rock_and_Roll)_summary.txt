### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom" features an interview with Damien Echols, one of the West Memphis Three. Echols recounts his childhood poverty, his interest in ceremonial magic, and how he became a suspect in the horrific murders of three young boys in West Memphis, Arkansas in 1993.  Despite a lack of evidence and coerced confession from a co-defendant, Echols and two others were convicted based largely on their appearance and interest in heavy metal music during the "Satanic Panic."  Echols details the injustice of the trial, his brutal treatment in prison including solitary confinement and physical abuse, and the long fight for his eventual release through an Alford plea after 18 years of wrongful imprisonment.  He emphasizes the importance of public support, the role of documentaries in raising awareness, and the devastating personal impact of wrongful conviction and long-term solitary confinement. The episode also touches on systemic issues within the justice system, including the pressure on law enforcement in high-profile cases, the political nature of legal roles, and the flaws of the death penalty.
