### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features the story of Andre Brown, who was wrongfully convicted of attempted murder in the Bronx in 1999.  Despite having a severe leg injury that made it physically impossible for him to commit the crime as described, and facing flimsy eyewitness testimony and an ineffective, corrupt lawyer, Andre was sentenced to 40 years in prison. The episode details the flawed investigation, the trial, and the subsequent efforts to overturn his conviction, highlighting systemic issues like eyewitness misidentification and inadequate legal representation.  Andre, along with his attorney Oscar Michelin, and host Maggie Freleng, recount the harrowing journey through the justice system, his decades of incarceration, and the ongoing fight for full exoneration as the Bronx DA appeals the vacature of his conviction. The discussion underscores the urgent need for criminal justice reform and emphasizes the devastating impact of wrongful convictions on individuals and families.
