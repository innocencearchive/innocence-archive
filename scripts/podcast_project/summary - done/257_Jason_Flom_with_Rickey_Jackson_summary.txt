### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Ricky Jackson, who was wrongfully convicted along with Wiley and Ronnie Bridgman for the murder of Harold Franks in 1975.  The conviction was based solely on the coerced testimony of 12-year-old Eddie Vernon, despite other compelling leads pointing to different suspects, including a car matching the escape vehicle and suspects identified by informants and even a suspect's own mother.  Police ignored exculpatory evidence and witness accounts from Vernon's classmates and bus driver, focusing instead on Vernon's fabricated story. Jackson, along with the Bridgman brothers, spent decades in prison, including time on death row, before finally being exonerated in 2014 after Vernon recanted his testimony.  The episode highlights the devastating impact of police tunnel vision, coerced testimony, and systemic issues within the criminal justice system, while also emphasizing Jackson's resilience and forgiveness.  The story underscores the profound personal cost of wrongful convictions and the urgent need for criminal justice reform.
