### Basic Podcast Information

- **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom" features Brian Ferguson, wrongfully convicted of murder and imprisoned for 11 years, and Mark Howard, a professor and mentor. Brian recounts his ordeal as a college student falsely accused based on hearsay, despite witness descriptions contradicting his appearance. He describes the surreal trial, the devastating conviction, and the harsh realities of prison. The episode exposes systemic failures, including ineffective counsel and pressure to close cases. Despite immense injustice, Brian found purpose in aiding fellow inmates and was eventually exonerated after 11 years with pro bono legal help, including from Eric Holder.  Brian's story is one of resilience, leading to his current role in Washington D.C.'s Mayor's Office of Returning Citizen Affairs, dedicated to assisting returning citizens. The conversation emphasizes hope, reform, and the importance of second chances.
