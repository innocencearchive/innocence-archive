### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features the story of Amanda Brumfield, wrongfully convicted of manslaughter in the death of her goddaughter Olivia.  Olivia died from a skull fracture after falling from a playpen while in Amanda's care. Despite it being an accident, the medical examiner incorrectly deemed the injuries inconsistent with a fall, leading to Amanda's charge and conviction.  Key factors contributing to the wrongful conviction included the flawed "shaken baby syndrome" theory, the barring of defense expert testimony, and national media attention due to Amanda's famous father, Billy Bob Thornton.  The Innocence Project of Florida and the Center for Integrity and Forensic Sciences (CIFS) took on her case, highlighting the flaws in the medical evidence and presenting studies on playpen fall dangers.  After years of fighting, Amanda was offered a plea deal for immediate release, choosing to prioritize her family over full exoneration. Amanda and Kate Judson from CIFS discuss the systemic issues and the ongoing misdiagnosis of child abuse, emphasizing the need for critical re-evaluation of forensic science in such cases.
