### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction, False Confessions, tells the story of Ray McCann, a reserve police officer in Constantine, Michigan, wrongfully accused of the 2007 murder of 11-year-old Jodi Perrick. Despite no physical evidence linking him to the crime, police fixated on Ray after he discovered Jodi's body.  Over five years, police interrogated Ray relentlessly, lied about evidence, and attacked his personal life to coerce a confession.  Unable to secure a murder charge, they charged him with perjury based on minor inconsistencies in his statements. Ray pled no contest to one perjury charge to end the ordeal and served 20 months in prison.  Later, Daniel Furlong confessed to Jodi's murder, and Ray was eventually exonerated of perjury after his legal team exposed police lies about surveillance video evidence. The episode highlights the dangers of tunnel vision in police investigations and the devastating impact of wrongful convictions.
