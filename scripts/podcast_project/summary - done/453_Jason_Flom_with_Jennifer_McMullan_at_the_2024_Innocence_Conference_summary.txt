### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features the story of Jennifer McMullen, who was wrongfully convicted of murder in 2002 and sentenced to 27 years in prison.  At 19, Jennifer and her friends were near the scene of a robbery-homicide.  After a lengthy and coercive 14-hour interrogation, influenced by medication, Jennifer falsely confessed.  Crucially, an alternate suspect group confessed to the crime, and their details aligned with withheld crime facts, yet this evidence was ignored by police and prosecutors.  Despite DNA evidence excluding Jennifer and her co-defendants, she was offered a plea deal for time served in 2021, which she accepted to be released after nearly 20 years.  Jennifer and the Illinois Innocence Project are now seeking clemency from the governor for full exoneration. The episode highlights systemic issues of false confessions, ineffective assistance of counsel, and prosecutorial misconduct, urging listeners to support Jennifer's clemency and innocence projects.
