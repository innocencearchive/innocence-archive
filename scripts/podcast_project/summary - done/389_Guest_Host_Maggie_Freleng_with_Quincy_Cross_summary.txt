### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction, hosted by Maggie Freeling, details the case of Quincy Cross, wrongfully convicted for the murder of Jessica Curran in Mayfield, Kentucky in 2000.  The investigation was marred by incompetence, mishandled evidence, and the emergence of a citizen investigator who steered suspicion towards Cross.  Key witnesses, Victoria Caldwell and Venetia Stubblefield, provided inconsistent and outlandish testimonies, potentially incentivized by lenient sentences and payments.  Despite a lack of physical evidence linking Cross to the crime and a questionable diary presented as evidence, he was convicted and sentenced to life without parole.  The Kentucky Innocence Project is now revisiting his case, uncovering new evidence of witness coercion and the potential for forensic retesting, offering a glimmer of hope for Cross's exoneration, with even the victim's father believing in his innocence.
