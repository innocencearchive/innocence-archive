-   **Executive Summary**: This Valentine's Day episode of Wrongful Conviction features Cedric Courtney and his wife Tina, sharing their unique love story that began when Tina was a prison guard and Cedric was wrongfully incarcerated for armed robbery. Cedric recounts his wrongful conviction in 1995 based on mistaken eyewitness identification and falsified lab evidence regarding hair analysis. Despite a strong alibi and the absurdity of red hair evidence against a black man, he was convicted and served 15 years.  The episode highlights systemic issues like ineffective legal counsel, unreliable forensic testimony, and the challenges in accessing evidence for exoneration.  Ultimately, DNA evidence found through the persistence of a law student proved his innocence, leading to his exoneration and a new life with Tina, who believed in him and supported him throughout. Their story is a testament to resilience, love found in unexpected places, and the fight for justice.
