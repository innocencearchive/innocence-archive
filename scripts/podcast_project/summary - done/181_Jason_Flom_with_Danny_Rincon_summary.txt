### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom features the story of Danny Rincon, wrongfully convicted for the 1991 Quad Murders in the Bronx.  Rincon, along with his lawyers from the Exoneration Initiative, Glenn Garber and Farrah Rossner, details the flawed investigation and trial that led to his 158-years-to-life sentence.  The episode highlights the intense pressure on police to solve the case, resulting in the use of unreliable, incentivized witnesses from a rival gang.  Despite having alibi witnesses, including the mother and brother of a victim, and later confessions from actual perpetrators, Rincon remains incarcerated. The podcast emphasizes systemic issues within the justice system, ineffective counsel, and judicial bias, urging listeners to support Rincon's exoneration and the Innocence Project.
