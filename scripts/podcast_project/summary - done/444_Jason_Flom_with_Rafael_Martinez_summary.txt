### Basic Podcast Information

- **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction details the case of Rafael Martinez, who was wrongly convicted of drug conspiracy, attempted murder, and murder, and sentenced to 213 years to life. The prosecution's case relied heavily on cooperating witnesses with hidden deals and fabricated narratives, while ignoring forensic evidence and eyewitness accounts pointing to another suspect, Franklin Cuevas.  Crucially, Rafael was convicted of murdering Jose Jimenez, who is revealed to be alive, having been imprisoned and later deported for another crime.  The episode highlights systemic issues including prosecutorial and judicial misconduct, ineffective defense counsel, and the devastating impact of the War on Drugs. Despite recantations, suppressed evidence, and the fact that the alleged murder victim is alive, Rafael Martinez remains incarcerated, illustrating a profound failure of the justice system to correct a clear wrongful conviction.
