-   **Systemic Issues Identified**:
    -   Over-reliance on eyewitness testimony in the justice system.
    -   The malleability and unreliability of human memory.
    -   Suggestive police procedures in eyewitness identification (photo and live lineups).
    -   Lack of understanding of memory's fallibility by juries and the public.
    -   Potential for unconscious bias and influence from law enforcement during identification processes.
-   **Policy Recommendations/Calls to Action**:
    -   Implement double-blind lineup procedures where neither the witness nor the administrator knows the suspect.
    -   Provide unbiased instructions to witnesses before lineups, emphasizing that the perpetrator may or may not be present and that exonerating the innocent is as important as identifying the guilty.
    -   Use fillers in lineups that match the witness's description of the perpetrator, not just resemble the suspect.
    -   Advocate for greater judicial and public awareness of the fallibility of eyewitness testimony and the science of memory.
