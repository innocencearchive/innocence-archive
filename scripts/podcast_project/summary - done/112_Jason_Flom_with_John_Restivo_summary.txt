### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom" features John Restivo, who was wrongfully convicted along with two other men for the rape and murder of a 16-year-old girl in Long Island in 1984.  The episode details the coerced false confession of John Cogut, the planting of fabricated evidence by police, and the lack of credible evidence against Restivo and Dennis Halsted. Despite having alibis and early DNA tests that were inconclusive but pointed away from them, they were convicted and spent decades in prison.  The episode highlights the systemic issues of guilt-presumptive interrogation, police misconduct in fabricating evidence, and the initial resistance to DNA evidence by the justice system. Through the persistent efforts of legal teams and organizations like the Innocence Project and Centurion Ministries, DNA testing of preserved evidence in 2003 finally exonerated all three men after they collectively served over 50 years for a crime they did not commit.  The conversation also touches upon Restivo's resilience, his work in prison, and his post-exoneration life advocating for justice.
