### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling tells the harrowing story of Evaristo Salas, wrongfully convicted of murder at the age of 16 and imprisoned for over 27 years.  Growing up in a chaotic environment and joining a gang for protection, Evaristo became a target of local police.  Despite maintaining his innocence, fabricated informant testimony and a questionable eyewitness identification, influenced by hypnosis, led to his conviction.  After decades of struggle and persistent efforts, new evidence of police misconduct and recanted testimony surfaced, leading to his exoneration in 2023.  The episode highlights systemic issues of racial bias, police misconduct, and the devastating impact of wrongful convictions on individuals and families.  Since his release, Evaristo is dedicated to giving back to his community and advocating for justice reform.
