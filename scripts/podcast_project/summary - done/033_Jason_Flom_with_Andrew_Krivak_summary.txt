-   **Systemic Issues Identified**:
    -   Police misconduct:  Fabricating evidence (jewelry), coercing witnesses (Denise Rose), aggressive and abusive interrogation tactics, ignoring alternative suspects (Howard Gomber), manipulating polygraph tests.
    -   False confessions:  Pressure tactics leading to signing pre-written confessions, lack of understanding of false confessions by juries and the public at the time of trial.
    -   Ineffective legal defense:  Trial lawyer failing to present promised evidence.
    -   Tunnel vision in investigations:  Focusing on initial suspects and ignoring contradictory evidence or other leads.
    -   Incentivized witness testimony:  Denise Rose potentially motivated to testify to avoid jail time.
    -   Lack of accountability for wrongful convictions:  Resistance from authorities to admit error and release Andrew Krivak despite co-defendant's exoneration.
