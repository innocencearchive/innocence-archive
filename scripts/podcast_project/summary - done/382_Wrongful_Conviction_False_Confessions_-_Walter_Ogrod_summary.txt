### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction: False Confessions" tells the story of Walter O'Grod, wrongfully convicted for the 1988 murder of Barbara Jean Horn in Philadelphia.  Despite lacking evidence, police coerced a false confession from O'Grod, a man with learning disabilities, after a grueling 14-hour interrogation.  He was initially acquitted in his first trial due to a hung jury, but subsequently convicted in a second trial based on jailhouse snitch testimony and sentenced to death.  After decades of appeals, the Philadelphia Conviction Integrity Unit under District Attorney Larry Krasner reinvestigated the case.  New evidence, including DNA exclusion and the recantation of snitch testimony, proved O'Grod's innocence.  He was exonerated in 2020 after serving 28 years, highlighting systemic issues of police misconduct, false confessions, and the importance of prosecutorial reform.
