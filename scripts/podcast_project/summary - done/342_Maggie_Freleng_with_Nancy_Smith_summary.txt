### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling" tells the tragic story of Nancy Smith, a single mother wrongfully convicted of child molestation in 1994 and sentenced to 30 to 90 years in prison. The accusations stemmed from a Head Start mother, Margie Grandin, who fabricated claims against Nancy, alleging she took children to a man named Joseph Allen for abuse. Despite initial police investigations finding no evidence and children's inconsistent testimonies, media frenzy and pressure led to charges and conviction.  Key exculpatory evidence, including alibi witnesses and attendance records, was not presented by the defense attorney.  After serving 15 years and with the help of the Ohio Innocence Project, Nancy's conviction was eventually vacated in 2022 after a new prosecutor reopened the case and uncovered evidence of coaching and manipulation of children, finally leading to her exoneration and a public apology from the state.
