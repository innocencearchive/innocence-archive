### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling" tells the story of Patty Pruitt, wrongfully convicted of murdering her husband Bill in 1984.  The podcast details the flawed police investigation, highlighting how investigators quickly focused on Patty due to her past marital issues and ignored crucial evidence, including a potential rape Patty experienced the night of the murder and a neighbor's report of a suspicious vehicle. The prosecution used character assassination, focusing on Patty's past relationships to paint her as a bad wife and mother, which influenced the jury.  Key evidence was overlooked, and Patty's defense was weak, failing to present critical information. Despite later admissions of investigative flaws by the prosecutor himself and the potential for DNA evidence to support Patty's rape claim, she remains incarcerated, having served nearly 36 years, maintaining her innocence and becoming a mentor to other incarcerated women.
