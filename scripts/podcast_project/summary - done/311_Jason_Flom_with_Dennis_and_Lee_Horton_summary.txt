### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features the harrowing story of Dennis and Lee Horton, two brothers wrongfully convicted of murder and armed robbery in Philadelphia in 1993. Despite flawed witness identifications, lack of physical evidence linking them to the crime, and the actual perpetrator, Robert Leif, being present, the brothers were sentenced to life without parole.  The episode details the systemic failures, including inadequate legal representation and procedural hurdles, that kept them imprisoned for nearly 27 years.  While incarcerated, Dennis and Lee dedicated themselves to self-improvement and helping others, becoming certified peer support specialists.  Their eventual release came through commutation, largely due to the advocacy of Lieutenant Governor John Fetterman.  The Horton brothers' story is a stark example of injustice, resilience, and the urgent need for criminal justice reform, highlighting the devastating impact of wrongful convictions and the long fight for freedom.
