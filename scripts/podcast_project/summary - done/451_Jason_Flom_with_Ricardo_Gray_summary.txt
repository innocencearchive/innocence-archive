### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction details the case of Ricardo Gray, wrongfully convicted in 1999 for a murder he did not commit in East Cleveland, Ohio.  The episode highlights the failures within the criminal justice system, focusing on fabricated witness testimony and potentially hidden exculpatory evidence. Ricardo was convicted based on testimony from two individuals who later recanted, stating they were pressured by detectives.  Despite multiple pieces of evidence pointing to his innocence, including original police reports and witness recantations, Ricardo remains incarcerated due to procedural hurdles and a judge known for denying post-conviction relief.  The podcast emphasizes systemic issues, judicial injustice, and the immense personal impact of wrongful convictions, appealing to listeners for help in raising awareness and seeking justice for Ricardo Gray. The episode underscores the urgent need for criminal justice reform to prevent and rectify wrongful convictions.
