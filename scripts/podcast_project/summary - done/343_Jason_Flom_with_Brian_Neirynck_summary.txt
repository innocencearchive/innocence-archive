### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features the story of Brian Neringke, who was wrongfully convicted of child molestation in Indiana. The accusations arose during a bitter custody dispute with his son's mother, Roberta Smedley.  Despite initial investigations revealing inconsistencies and the child stating his father did nothing wrong, Roberta persisted, and the child was subjected to suggestive play therapy that heavily influenced his testimony. Brian's public defender provided ineffective counsel, failing to investigate crucial evidence like therapy notes and videos.  After years of wrongful imprisonment and tireless efforts, Brian was eventually exonerated thanks to the work of post-conviction attorney Cynthia Carter and the discovery of the play therapy records that proved the child was groomed to fabricate accusations. The case highlights the devastating impact of false allegations in custody battles and the critical failures within the justice system, including ineffective legal representation and the misuse of child therapy.
