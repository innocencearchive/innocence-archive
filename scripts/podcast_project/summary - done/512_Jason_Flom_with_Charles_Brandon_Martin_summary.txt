### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction details the case of Charles Brandon Martin, convicted for the 2008 shooting of Jodie Lynn Torek in Maryland, which resulted in the loss of her pregnancy. The state alleged Martin, one potential father, arranged the attack to avoid child support, claiming he used a makeshift Gatorade bottle silencer. His conviction heavily relied on testimony from Sherry Carter about alleged internet searches for silencer instructions on a supposedly destroyed computer, and Michael Bradley, who implicated Martin after changing his initial story which corroborated Martin's alibi. Post-conviction, it was revealed the state withheld crucial Brady evidence: forensic analysis proving no such searches occurred on Martin's computer and Bradley's initial contradictory statement. Despite alternative suspects like Maggie McFadden and flawed evidence presentation, Martin was convicted; his conviction was vacated in 2016, but the state continues to delay the granted new trial.
