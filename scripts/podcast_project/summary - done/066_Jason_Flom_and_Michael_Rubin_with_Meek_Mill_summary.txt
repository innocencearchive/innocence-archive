### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom" features Meek Mill and Michael Rubin discussing Meek's wrongful conviction case and the broader systemic issues within the American criminal justice system. Meek recounts his experiences growing up in poverty and being caught in a system that seemed designed to incarcerate young Black men.  Rubin, initially unaware of these disparities, shares his awakening to the two Americas after witnessing Meek's legal battles. The conversation highlights unreliable eyewitness identification, excessive probation terms, the cycle of re-incarceration for technical violations, and the disproportionate impact on communities of color.  They emphasize the need for criminal justice reform, voting, and rethinking punishment to fit the crime, advocating for rehabilitation and addressing systemic flaws instead of perpetuating cycles of poverty and incarceration. The guests call for empathy, common sense solutions, and encourage listeners to get involved in reform efforts.
