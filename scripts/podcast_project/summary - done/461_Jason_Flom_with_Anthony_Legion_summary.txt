### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction details the case of Anthony Legion, wrongfully convicted of first-degree murder in Detroit in 2001.  Legion, along with Marvin Cotton and Devontae Parks, was accused of killing Juman McIntyre in a drug house related incident. The case was built on unreliable eyewitness testimony from Kenny Lockhart, who initially claimed to see nothing, and fabricated jailhouse informant testimony from Ellis Frazier.  Key exculpatory evidence, including Parks' alibi and Lockhart's misidentification of Parks, was suppressed.  Police misconduct and prosecutorial gamesmanship are highlighted as major contributing factors to the wrongful conviction.  After nearly 19 years in prison, Legion's conviction was finally overturned in 2020 due to the efforts of the Conviction Integrity Unit and new evidence of police misconduct and witness recantations. Legion is now working in law and advocating for others who are wrongfully convicted.
