### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction is guest-hosted by Chris Fabrikant, who interviews Gilbert Poole and Marla Mitchell-Sician about Poole's wrongful conviction for murder in Michigan. Poole was convicted in 1989 based on fabricated composite sketches, false testimony from an ex-girlfriend, and junk science bite mark evidence.  Despite blood evidence excluding him, and ineffective defense counsel, Poole served 32 years in prison. The episode details the failures of the justice system, including manipulated evidence, reliance on unreliable forensic science, and inadequate legal representation.  Poole was exonerated in 2021 thanks to the Innocence Project and a newly formed conviction integrity unit.  The episode highlights the devastating impact of wrongful convictions and the need for systemic reform and support for exonerees.
