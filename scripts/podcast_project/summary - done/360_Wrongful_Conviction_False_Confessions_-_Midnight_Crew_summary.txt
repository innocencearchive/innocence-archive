### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction: False Confessions delves into the horrific Chicago Police torture scandal involving John Burge and the "Midnight Crew," focusing on the wrongful conviction of Ronald Kitchen.  Kitchen was tortured into a false confession for a quintuple murder he did not commit, spending 21 years in prison, including time on death row. The podcast details the systemic nature of the torture, the racial bias involved, and the decades-long cover-up.  It highlights the investigative journalism that exposed the scandal and the eventual exonerations of some victims. The episode underscores the devastating impact of torture-induced false confessions and the ongoing fight for justice and accountability, even after Burge's death. The hosts emphasize the importance of remembering this history to prevent future abuses and advocate for systemic change in the criminal justice system, highlighting Ronald Kitchen's work with students to educate them about these issues.
