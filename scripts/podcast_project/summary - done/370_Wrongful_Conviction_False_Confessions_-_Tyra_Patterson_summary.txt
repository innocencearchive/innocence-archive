### Basic Podcast Information

- **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction: False Confessions tells the story of Tyra Patterson, wrongfully convicted of murder in Ohio. At 19, Tyra was present at a robbery where a murder occurred, but she did not commit the murder.  Police coerced a false confession from Tyra for robbery, which, due to the felony murder rule, led to her murder conviction.  Her defense attorneys were ineffective, and key evidence that could have supported her innocence was not presented at trial. Tyra was sentenced to 43 years to life, longer than the actual shooter. After serving 21 years, her sentence was commuted, and she was granted parole.  Since her release, Tyra has become a community outreach strategist and advocate for criminal justice reform, working to help others and fight for exoneration.
