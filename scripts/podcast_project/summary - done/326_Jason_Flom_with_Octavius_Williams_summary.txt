### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction tells the story of Octavius Williams, a promising young basketball player wrongfully convicted of attempted murder in 2011.  Despite a flawed eyewitness identification, lack of physical evidence, and multiple witnesses identifying his brother Ricky as the shooter, Octavius was convicted and sentenced to prison.  Adding to the injustice, Ricky confessed to the crime shortly after the trial, and later again in a signed affidavit and to a prosecutor.  Even with the unanimous recommendation for exoneration from the Conviction Integrity Unit, the prosecutor refused full exoneration, offering only judicial release after Octavius served 10-11 years.  The case highlights systemic issues within the Cuyahoga County justice system, including ineffective investigations, reliance on unreliable eyewitness testimony, and the immense difficulty in overturning wrongful convictions even with compelling evidence of innocence. Octavius is now out of prison but still fights for full exoneration to clear his name and rebuild his life.
