### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling tells the tragic story of Amanda Bussy, wrongfully convicted of second-degree murder.  Amanda endured horrific childhood abuse, including sexual abuse by her father and his associates.  She ran away with Larry DeClue, who was also abusive and later implicated in the murder of Diane Coleman.  Amanda's younger brother Buddy, facing charges for molesting Larry's niece, falsely confessed to police that Amanda was involved in Coleman's murder to lessen his own charges.  Despite weak evidence and Buddy's recantation on the stand, Amanda was convicted and sentenced to 25 years. The Willow Project is now working to exonerate her, and she is scheduled for parole in early 2025 after serving almost 20 years for a crime she maintains she did not commit.
