### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction, hosted by Connor Hall, details the case of Brian Pippett, an Indigenous man wrongfully convicted of the 1998 murder of Evelyn Mallon in Minnesota.  Despite a lack of physical evidence and credible alibis, Pippett and his relatives were targeted based on flimsy rumors and coerced testimonies. The episode highlights the deeply flawed investigation, reliance on a recanted testimony and a jailhouse snitch, and the prosecution's illogical basement entry theory.  Brian Pippett was convicted despite significant inconsistencies and lack of corroborating evidence.  The Minnesota Conviction Review Unit (CRU) has since conducted a thorough investigation finding substantial evidence of wrongful conviction, but local law enforcement is resisting these findings.  Pippett remains incarcerated after 25 years, hoping for exoneration and highlighting systemic issues within the justice system, including potential prejudice against Native Americans.
