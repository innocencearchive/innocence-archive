### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Calvin Boari, who was wrongfully convicted of a double homicide in 1995 and spent over two decades in prison, and Steve Fishman, a journalist whose work aided in Boari's eventual release.  The podcast details Boari's upbringing, his involvement in the drug trade, and the events leading to his wrongful conviction, highlighting prosecutorial misconduct, incentivized witnesses, and trial by ambush tactics. Despite maintaining his innocence and even receiving a confession from another individual who later recanted, Boari persevered.  Key to his eventual exoneration were new eyewitness testimonies that corroborated his innocence.  The episode explores the systemic failures within the justice system, the personal impact of wrongful conviction, and Boari's resilience and entrepreneurial spirit post-release, where he now runs a van service for prison visits and advocates for justice reform.
