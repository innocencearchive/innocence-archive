### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom details the harrowing case of Anthony Sims, wrongfully convicted of murder in 1998.  Despite overwhelming evidence pointing to Julius Graves as the actual perpetrator, including motive and eyewitness testimony from Rachel, Sims was convicted based on a fabricated narrative by Graves and his associates.  Key exculpatory evidence, including Rachel's statement and inconsistencies in witness testimonies, was withheld by the prosecution, a Brady violation.  Sims served 24 years of a 25-to-life sentence while maintaining his innocence.  His current legal team, Jonathan Hiles and Thomas Hoffman, are fighting for his exoneration, highlighting systemic issues within the justice system and calling for public support to pressure the Brooklyn District Attorney to review the case and release Anthony Sims.
