### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Gloria Killian, who was wrongfully convicted of a double murder and served 17 years in prison. The episode details the flawed investigation and trial that led to her conviction, highlighting the role of an incentivized witness, Gary Massey, and prosecutorial misconduct.  Gloria, a law student at the time of her arrest, recounts the surreal and devastating experience of being accused and convicted of a crime she did not commit.  The podcast also features Nina Morrison from the Innocence Project, who provides legal context and discusses systemic issues within the justice system, such as the use of jailhouse informants and lack of accountability for prosecutorial misconduct.  Gloria emphasizes the importance of jury awareness and the human impact of wrongful convictions, sharing her personal survival story in prison and her subsequent advocacy for incarcerated women through her organization, the Action Committee for Women in Prison.  The episode underscores the fragility of justice and the urgent need for reform to prevent future wrongful convictions.
