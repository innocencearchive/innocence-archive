### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction is guest-hosted by Gilbert King and features Robert Dubois, who was wrongfully convicted of capital murder in Florida in 1985 based on discredited bite mark evidence and the testimony of a jailhouse informant.  Robert spent 37 years incarcerated, including three years on death row, before being exonerated in 2020 due to DNA evidence and the work of the Innocence Project. The episode details the flawed investigation, the trial, the impact of junk science, and the challenges Robert faced both during his wrongful imprisonment and after his release, including the lack of compensation due to a prior minor offense from his youth. The discussion highlights systemic issues in the criminal justice system, including tunnel vision, reliance on unreliable forensic evidence, and the misuse of incentivized witnesses.  Susan Friedman from the Innocence Project also joins to discuss the legal aspects of Robert's case and calls for reform in Florida's compensation statute.
