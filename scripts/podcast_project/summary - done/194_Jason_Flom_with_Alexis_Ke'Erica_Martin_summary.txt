### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features the harrowing story of Alexis Chiarica Martin, a survivor of child sex trafficking who was wrongfully convicted of felony murder.  At 14, Alexis was groomed and trafficked.  During a robbery orchestrated as an escape attempt, her trafficker was killed, leading to Alexis's arrest at 15. Despite Ohio's Safe Harbor Law designed to protect trafficking victims, Alexis was tried as an adult and sentenced to 21 years to life. The podcast details the failures of the legal system to recognize her victimization, her eventual fight for clemency, and her release after seven years.  Alexis, with her lawyer Sasha Naaman, shares her journey of resilience, highlighting systemic issues and advocating for justice and support for survivors. She now focuses on helping at-risk youth and other survivors, aiming to prevent others from experiencing similar tragedies.
