### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom revisits the case of Nelson Cruz, who was wrongfully convicted of murder in 1998 and spent 25 years in prison before being released on parole.  The episode details the egregious misconduct by NYPD detectives Scarcella and Camille of the 75th precinct, who manipulated witnesses and ignored evidence pointing to the actual shooter, Eduardo Rodriguez. Despite a police officer witnessing Rodriguez with the gun and testifying in court that Nelson Cruz was not the shooter, and despite new exculpatory evidence presented during appeals, Cruz remained imprisoned for decades. The episode highlights the systemic failures within the justice system, including unreliable eyewitness testimony, police corruption, and the devastating impact of wrongful convictions on individuals and their families.  It also covers the unusual circumstances of Judge Simpson's declining mental health during a crucial appeal hearing, further delaying justice for Nelson Cruz, and culminates with Nelson's release on parole and his ongoing fight for full exoneration.
