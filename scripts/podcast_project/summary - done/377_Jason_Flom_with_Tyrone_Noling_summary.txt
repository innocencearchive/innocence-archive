### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction revisits the case of Tyrone Knowling, who has been on death row in Ohio for over 30 years for the murder of Bernhardt and Cora Hartick in 1990.  The episode highlights the twisted and nonsensical nature of his wrongful conviction, emphasizing coerced false testimonies from his friends under threat of the death penalty. Despite ballistics tests initially clearing Tyrone and his friends, and viable alternative suspects emerging, aggressive interrogation tactics by detective Ron Craig led to false confessions.  Key evidence pointing towards other suspects, including Dennis van Steenburg, Louis Lehman, and Dan Wilson, was seemingly ignored or mishandled. All co-defendants have since recanted their testimonies, and DNA evidence further undermines the conviction, yet Tyrone remains on death row, fighting for access to crucial case files to prove his innocence.

    -   **Louis Lehman**: Insurance salesman who owed money to Mr. Harding and owned a .25 caliber pistol of a similar type to the murder weapon. He was uncooperative with police.
    -   **Dan Wilson**: Foster brother of Nathan Chesley who initially implicated a foster brother. Dan Wilson matched the blood type on a cigarette butt found at the scene and was later arrested for another murder, suggesting potential serial killer tendencies and proximity to the Harding residence.
