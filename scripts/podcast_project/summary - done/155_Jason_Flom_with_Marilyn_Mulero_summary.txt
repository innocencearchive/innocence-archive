### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom features the story of Marilyn Malera, wrongfully convicted in 1992 for a double homicide she did not commit and sentenced to death after a coerced guilty plea.  The episode details the egregious failures of the justice system in her case, including corrupt detectives, false witness testimony, ineffective counsel, and a coerced confession after 20 hours of interrogation.  Justin Brooks, founder of the California Innocence Project, recounts his 25-year fight to exonerate Marilyn, highlighting the systemic issues that led to her wrongful conviction. Marilyn herself shares her harrowing experience on death row and her eventual release in 2020 after a clemency petition.  The episode underscores the devastating impact of wrongful convictions and the importance of innocence projects in rectifying these injustices, with Marilyn now dedicated to helping other wrongfully convicted individuals.
