### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features the harrowing story of Clinton Young, who was wrongfully convicted and sentenced to death for murders he did not commit in Texas in 2001. The podcast details Young's difficult upbringing, his presence during the crimes committed by David Page, and how incentivized testimonies and prosecutorial misconduct led to his wrongful conviction. Despite evidence pointing to his innocence, including ballistics and later confessions from David Page, Young spent years on death row.  The episode highlights the failures of his initial defense, the destruction and withholding of evidence by the prosecution, and the shocking revelation of prosecutorial misconduct involving a prosecutor moonlighting as a judge's clerk.  After years of appeals and new evidence, Young's conviction was overturned in 2021, and he was released on bond in 2022, awaiting a potential retrial, marking a significant, albeit ongoing, battle for justice.
