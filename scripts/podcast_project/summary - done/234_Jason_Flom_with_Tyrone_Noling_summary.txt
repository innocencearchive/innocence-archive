### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction delves into the egregious case of Tyrone Noling, who has spent over 30 years on death row in Ohio for a double murder he did not commit.  The podcast details how, in 1990, Noling and his friends, involved in minor robberies, became suspects in the Harding murders despite initial evidence excluding them.  Coerced testimonies from Noling's friends, obtained through aggressive interrogation and threats of the death penalty, formed the basis of his conviction.  The episode highlights multiple overlooked leads, including other potential suspects with stronger connections to the crime. Despite recantations from all prosecution witnesses and significant evidence of innocence uncovered by the Ohio Innocence Project, Noling remains on death row, underscoring profound systemic failures within the justice system and the ongoing fight for his exoneration.
