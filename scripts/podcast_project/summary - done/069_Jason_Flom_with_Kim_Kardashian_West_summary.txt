### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom" features Kim Kardashian as a special guest, discussing her involvement in criminal justice reform, sparked by seeing Alice Johnson's case on Twitter. Kardashian recounts her journey to advocate for Johnson's clemency, highlighting systemic issues like harsh mandatory minimum sentences and the disproportionate impact on low-level offenders, particularly women. The conversation expands to broader topics such as mass incarceration in the US, the flaws in the justice system including prosecutorial power and inadequate defense for the poor, and the importance of clemency. Both Flom and Kardashian emphasize the need for systemic change, public awareness, and action to address wrongful convictions and create a more humane justice system. They encourage listeners to get involved with organizations like Families Against Mandatory Minimums and the Innocence Project, and to advocate for reform at both state and federal levels.
