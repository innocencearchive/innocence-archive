### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom" features Rafael Madrigal, who was wrongfully convicted of first-degree murder and sentenced to 25 years to life.  Despite having an airtight alibi and multiple flaws in the prosecution's case, including mistaken eyewitness identification based on a decade-old photo and an incompetent defense attorney, Rafael spent nearly 10 years incarcerated. The episode details the failures of the justice system, from the initial flawed identification process to the ineffective legal representation and the subsequent struggle for compensation after exoneration.  Rafael emphasizes the devastating impact of wrongful conviction on individuals and their families, highlighting the urgent need for systemic reform and improved support for exonerees upon release. He expresses gratitude for organizations like the California Innocence Project and attorneys like Eric Moltop who helped secure his freedom. The episode concludes with a call to action for listeners to support organizations fighting wrongful convictions and to advocate for justice system reform.
