### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This podcast episode, part one of a two-part series, features the story of Nick Yaris, who was wrongfully convicted of rape and murder.  The host, Jason Flom, interviews Nick about his traumatic childhood and the circumstances leading to his arrest for a traffic violation.  While in jail, facing multiple charges, Nick attempted to barter false information about the Linda May Craig murder case, which backfired and led to him being falsely accused. Despite flawed evidence including serology and questionable eyewitness testimony, and relying on a jailhouse informant, Nick was convicted and sentenced to death.  The episode details the initial crime of Linda May Craig's murder and sets the stage for Nick's wrongful conviction trial and subsequent experiences on death row, ending with his escape and eventual decision to turn himself back in.
