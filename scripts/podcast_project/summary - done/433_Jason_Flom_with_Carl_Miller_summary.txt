### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of the Wrongful Conviction podcast tells the story of Carl Miller, wrongfully convicted for the 1979 murder of Rabbi David Akunab in Crown Heights, Brooklyn. The episode features Carl Miller and his attorneys, James Hanning and Carissa Cocken, discussing the racial and religious tensions in Crown Heights at the time of the crime, and the flawed investigation and trial that led to Carl's conviction.  Key issues highlighted include unreliable eyewitness testimony from Daryl Brown, inconsistencies in Brown's statements, ineffective assistance of counsel, potential Brady violations regarding alternate suspects and witness information, and the influence of community pressure on the investigation. Carl Miller spent 31 years in prison despite maintaining his innocence and is now working with his legal team to fully exonerate him, especially with new evidence emerging related to other potential perpetrators.
