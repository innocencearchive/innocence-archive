### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom details the case of Terrell Barrows, wrongfully convicted for a shooting in Providence, Rhode Island. Despite Stefan Bodden's on-scene confession to the crime, Terrell was convicted based on a questionable dying declaration, incentivized eyewitness testimony, and a confusing presentation of DNA evidence.  The podcast highlights critical flaws in the investigation and trial, including the ignoring of Bodden's confession, failure to properly investigate GSR evidence initially, and reliance on unreliable witness testimony.  New evidence, uncovered by Georgetown University students and Terrell's new legal team, including a re-examination of GSR evidence and new witness affidavits, led to his release on bail and the vacating of his conviction. The episode advocates for Terrell's full exoneration and discusses the systemic issues that contribute to wrongful convictions.
