### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Darian Harris, who was wrongfully convicted of murder and spent nearly a decade in prison.  The episode details the flawed police investigation, highlighting unreliable eyewitness testimony, police coercion, and prosecutorial misconduct.  Key witnesses, including one who was legally blind, provided inconsistent and questionable identifications.  Despite recantations and evidence of the lead witness's blindness being known by the prosecution, Darian was convicted and sentenced to 76 years.  Through the efforts of the Exoneration Project and new legal representation, Darian's conviction was eventually vacated in December 2023. The episode also touches on the systemic issues within the justice system, the harsh realities of prison life, and Darian's ongoing journey to rebuild his life post-exoneration and his commitment to advocacy and helping others.
