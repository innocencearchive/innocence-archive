### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom" tells the harrowing story of Keith Allen Harward, a Navy sailor wrongfully convicted of murder and rape in 1983.  Despite lacking matching the victim's description and initial dental exclusions, flawed bite mark evidence and suggestive hypnosis techniques led to his conviction and 33 years of imprisonment. The podcast details the "junk science" of bite mark analysis and the devastating impact of misidentification and potential prosecutorial misconduct in his case.  Through the efforts of the Innocence Project and pro bono lawyers, DNA evidence ultimately exonerated Keith, identifying the true perpetrator, Jerry Crotty, a fellow sailor who had since died in prison for other crimes.  Keith's resilience and spiritual strength are highlighted, as is the re-victimization of the rape survivor upon learning the truth after decades. The episode underscores the critical need for justice system reform and support for organizations like the Innocence Project.
