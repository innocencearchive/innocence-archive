### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features an interview with Jimmy Dennis, who was wrongfully convicted of murder and spent 25.5 years on death row in Pennsylvania before being exonerated. Dennis recounts the details of his case, highlighting the lack of evidence connecting him to the crime, flawed eyewitness identification, and withheld exculpatory evidence, including a welfare receipt that proved his alibi. He describes the devastating impact of wrongful conviction on his life, family, and mental health, including the dehumanizing experience of death row.  Despite the immense injustice, Dennis shares his journey of maintaining hope, faith, and ultimately finding freedom and purpose through music and advocacy for others. The episode underscores systemic issues within the criminal justice system, particularly in Philadelphia during the early 1990s, and emphasizes the importance of jury duty and vigilance against wrongful convictions.
