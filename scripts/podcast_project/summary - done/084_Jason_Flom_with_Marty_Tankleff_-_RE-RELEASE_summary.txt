-   **Systemic Issues Identified**:
    -   False confessions are a significant factor in wrongful convictions.
    -   Police deception during interrogations is legal and contributes to false confessions.
    -   Tunnel vision in police investigations.
    -   The immense weight jurors place on confession evidence, even over contradictory evidence like DNA.
    -   Lack of recording of interrogations.
    -   Inadequate legal protection for vulnerable individuals during interrogation, particularly minors.
-   **Policy Recommendations/Calls to Action**:
    -   Advocacy for recording all police interrogations.
    -   Need for courts to revisit the legality and ethical implications of police deception during interrogations.
    -   Supporting organizations like The Innocence Project to prevent future wrongful convictions.
