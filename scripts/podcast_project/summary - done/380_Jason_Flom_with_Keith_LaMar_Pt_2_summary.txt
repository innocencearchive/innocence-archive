### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction, part two of Keith Lamar's story, delves into the deeply flawed trial and conviction of Keith Lamar for murders during the Lucasville prison riot in 1993.  Despite maintaining his innocence and refusing a plea deal, Lamar was convicted based on the testimonies of incentivized inmate witnesses whose stories drastically changed over time. The prosecution, led by Mark Peepmeyer and Seth Teager, withheld crucial exculpatory evidence and employed questionable tactics, including presenting statement summaries without names to the defense.  The podcast highlights the systemic issues of coerced testimonies, prosecutorial misconduct, and racial bias in the justice system that contributed to Lamar's wrongful conviction and death sentence.  It emphasizes the ongoing fight for his exoneration and the urgent need for judicial review of the suppressed evidence.  The episode ends with a call to action for listeners to support Keith Lamar's case and advocate for justice.
