### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of the Wrongful Conviction podcast, hosted by Jason Pham, delves into the case of Ken Middleton, who has spent over 30 years in prison for the death of his wife, Kathy.  Despite evidence suggesting a tragic accident and serious flaws in the investigation and trial, including potential evidence tampering, ineffective counsel, and prosecutorial conflicts of interest, Middleton remains incarcerated. The podcast highlights the mishandling of the crime scene, altered gunshot residue evidence, and the failure of Middleton's defense attorney to present crucial evidence.  Even after his conviction was overturned in 2004 due to ineffective counsel, a jurisdictional technicality kept him in legal limbo. A new Missouri law offers hope, but the current prosecutor has yet to act, leaving Middleton and his son, Cliff, in a continued fight for justice and exoneration. The episode calls for public support to pressure the prosecutor to review the case and apply the new statute to finally grant Middleton a new trial and freedom.

### Wrongfully Convicted Individuals Featured

-   **Name**: Ken Middleton
-   **Case Location**: Blue Springs, Missouri, USA (Jackson County)
-   **Conviction Year**: 1991
-   **Exoneration Year**: 2004 (conviction overturned, but not fully exonerated due to jurisdictional issues, still fighting for exoneration as of podcast release in 2022)
-   **Crime Convicted Of**: Murder of his wife, Kathy Middleton
-   **Sentence**: Life without parole plus 200 years
-   **Time Served**: Over 30 years (as of March 2022)
-   **Contributing Factors**:
    - Ineffective assistance of counsel (Bob Duncan)
    - Possible evidence tampering (gunshot residue test alteration)
    - False expert testimony (blood spatter expert with limited training)
    - Prosecutorial misconduct and conflict of interest (civil suit by victim's family linked to prosecutor's father's law firm)
    - Botched investigation (mishandled crime scene, re-staging, missing photos, improper evidence collection)
    - Jurisdictional technicality after conviction overturned
-   **Legal Status**: Conviction overturned in 2004, but not fully exonerated. Still fighting for a new trial and exoneration as of 2022.
-   **Organizations Involved**: Not explicitly mentioned in transcript, but implied support from wrongful conviction advocacy groups and media (podcast itself). Website: free-kenmiddleton.com
-   **Innocence Lawyer Details**:
    - **Bob Duncan**: Original trial attorney, deemed ineffective. Failed to investigate, examine evidence, interview witnesses, and make an opening statement.
    - **Gerald Handley**: Appellate attorney for 2915 appeal, also ineffective, procedurally defaulted issues, did not meet with client, filed inadequate motion.
    - **Jonathan Lawrence**: New attorney in 2000s, successfully reopened case and secured overturning of conviction in 2004.
-   **Narrative of Original Crime**: Ken Middleton was accused of murdering his wife, Kathy, in their home on February 12, 1990. The prosecution alleged Ken held Kathy against a wall and shot her in the face. Ken maintained it was a tragic accident, stating Kathy was upset about news of a past affair, picked up a gun he had laid down to clean, and accidentally shot herself in the head while going to call the alleged mistress.
-   **Key Evidence at Trial (Prosecution)**:
    - Expert testimony (blood spatter) claiming Kathy was held against the wall and shot at close range by Ken.
    - Implied motive of marital discord and possibly secret assets (later shown to be false testimony).
    - Claimed boot print on the wall, purported to be Ken's (later shown to be pre-existing and physically improbable).
    - Suggestion Ken attempted to wash gunshot residue from his hands.
-   **Trial Defender Notes**:
    - Bob Duncan was ineffective and unprepared.
    - Failed to investigate crime scene evidence, including the gun and gunshot residue tests.
    - Did not present expert witnesses to counter prosecution's claims.
    - Reserved opening statement but forgot to give it.
    - Failed to highlight inconsistencies and potential evidence tampering.
    - Did not impeach false testimony effectively.
-   **Key Evidence for Exoneration/Change in Legal Status**:
    - **Expert testimony (Bob Tressel, forensic investigator)**:  Demonstrated the state's scenario was physically impossible based on ballistics and crime scene analysis. Showed the shooter would have had to be under the table to achieve the trajectory and wound. No blood or GSR on Ken, despite close range shooting.
    - **Expert testimony (Chuck Gay, crime scene investigation)**: Highlighted improper crime scene handling, restaging, and evidence mishandling by police.
    - **Gunshot Residue Document Examination**: Revealed alteration of the crime lab document, suggesting a cover-up regarding the left-hand GSR test on Kathy.
    - **Testimony of Former Governor Joseph P. Tisdale**: Condemned the constitutional violations and ineffective counsel, stating he would pardon Ken Middleton.
    - **Judge Messina's 2004 Ruling**: Overturned conviction based on eight points of ineffective assistance of counsel.
-   **Actual Perpetrator Details**:  Not applicable, case is presented as a tragic accident, not a murder by another perpetrator.
