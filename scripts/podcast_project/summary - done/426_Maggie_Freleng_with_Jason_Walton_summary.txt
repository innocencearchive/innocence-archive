### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling" tells the story of Jason Walton, wrongfully convicted of the 2005 murder of William Cox and attempted murder of Edward Williams in Los Angeles. Despite a solid alibi backed by surveillance footage and cell phone records placing him miles away at the time of the shooting, Jason was convicted based on inconsistent eyewitness testimony and gang affiliation bias. The podcast highlights the flawed police investigation, the prosecution's cherry-picking of evidence, and the failure of the justice system to acknowledge Jason's alibi and the inconsistencies in witness accounts.  The Innocence Center is now working on Jason's case, uncovering crucial suppressed witness testimony that could prove his innocence and reveal the likely actual perpetrators. Jason, maintaining his innocence, has spent years in prison and hopes to be exonerated and become an attorney to help others facing wrongful convictions.

-   **Narrative of Original Crime**: A shooting occurred at a pop-up street fair in South Central Los Angeles in November 2005. William Cox was killed, and Edward Williams was injured. Police investigated the shooting as gang-related violence between Roland 30s and Roland 40s gangs.
-   **Key Evidence at Trial (Prosecution)**:
    - Eyewitness identification from Edward Williams, the surviving victim, identifying Jason as the shooter.
    - Eyewitness identification from Richard Gray, a carnival worker, who initially said he only saw the shooter fleeing but later identified Jason in a photo lineup.
    - Gang expert testimony linking Jason to the Mansfield Crips, allies of Roland 30s, who were rivals with Roland 40s (Edward Williams' gang).
    - Prosecution theory that Jason had a prior altercation with Edward Williams and sought revenge at the carnival.
-   **Trial Defender Notes**:
    - Alibi defense: Jason was at Roscoe's Chicken and Waffles at the time of the shooting, supported by surveillance footage and witness testimony (manager and Nico, gang intervention specialist).
    - Argument that eyewitness identifications were unreliable and inconsistent.
    - Argument that Jason's height didn't match witness description of shooter's height.
    - Presented time trials demonstrating the unlikelihood of Jason traveling from Roscoe's to the crime scene and back within the timeframe.
-   **Key Evidence for Exoneration/Change in Legal Status**:
    - Surveillance camera footage from Roscoe's Chicken and Waffles placing Jason there during the time of the shooting.
    - Cell phone records corroborating Jason's location near Roscoe's.
    - Testimony of Christopher Green, a carnival worker who actually witnessed the shooting and stated the shooter wore a hoodie obscuring their face and saw a gun, contradicting Edward Williams' account and Richard Gray's visibility. Green's statement was not presented at the original trial.
    - Edward Williams' initial statements implicating rival gang members from Roland 30s.
    - Inconsistencies in Richard Gray's testimony and his inability to have seen the shooting from his location due to obstructions (porta potties).
-   **Actual Perpetrator Details**: Not definitively identified in the podcast.
