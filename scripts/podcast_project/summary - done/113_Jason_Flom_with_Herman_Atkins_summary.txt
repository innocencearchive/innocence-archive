### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Herman Atkins, who was wrongfully convicted of rape, oral copulation, robbery, and kidnap in 1986 and sentenced to 47 years plus eight months. The episode details the flawed case against him, highlighting mistaken eyewitness identification, misleading serology, police and prosecutorial misconduct, and the role of a jailhouse informant.  Atkins recounts his 14-year ordeal in prison, his eventual exoneration through DNA testing facilitated by the Innocence Project in 2000, and his journey towards forgiveness, including his reconciliation with his father who initially doubted him.  He discusses the systemic issues within the justice system and advocates for reforms, particularly accountability for prosecutors and improved support for exonerees.  Atkins emphasizes the importance of awareness and encourages listeners to educate others about wrongful convictions and the need for judicial reform.
