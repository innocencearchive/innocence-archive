### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom features Robert Jones, a man wrongfully convicted in New Orleans, and Emily Maw, the director of the Innocence Project of New Orleans (IPNO).  Robert recounts his childhood, the events leading to his wrongful arrest in 1992 for a series of crimes committed by another man named Lester Jones, and his 23-year ordeal in prison. Emily Maw details the systemic failures in Robert's case, including police tunnel vision, prosecutorial misconduct, and ineffective defense counsel.  Despite overwhelming evidence of his innocence and the actual perpetrator being identified and convicted, Robert remained imprisoned until IPNO took up his case. The episode highlights the devastating impact of wrongful convictions and the arduous fight for justice and exoneration, celebrating Robert's eventual freedom and his inspiring resilience and advocacy for systemic change.
