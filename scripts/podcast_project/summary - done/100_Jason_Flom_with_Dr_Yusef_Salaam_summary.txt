### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Jason Flom interviewing Dr. Yusef Salaam, one of the Exonerated Five.  Dr. Salaam recounts his horrific experience of being wrongfully convicted in the Central Park jogger case as a teenager in the 1980s. He describes the intense pressure and coercion during police interrogation, leading to false confessions from some of the group.  The episode highlights the racial bias and media frenzy surrounding the case, emphasizing how the justice system failed these young men.  Despite the immense injustice and years spent in prison, Dr. Salaam shares his journey of finding purpose and becoming an advocate for criminal justice reform.  The conversation underscores the devastating impact of wrongful convictions on individuals and families, and the systemic issues within the criminal justice system that contribute to such tragedies.  Dr. Salaam's powerful story serves as a stark reminder of the importance of fighting for justice and reform.
