### Basic Podcast Information

- **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction tells the tragic story of Jeremy Puckett, wrongfully convicted in California for a murder he did not commit.  The case hinged on the unreliable testimony of Israel Sept, a jailhouse informant who received a lenient plea deal in exchange for implicating Jeremy.  Despite Sept's recantation and significant evidence pointing to another suspect, James Reeves (JMO), Jeremy was convicted and served 19 years in prison.  The Northern California Innocence Project (NCIP) took on his case, uncovering suppressed evidence, including a massive file related to the murder of Angela Dvorsky, which revealed JMO's potential involvement and a pattern of set-up robberies.  Through persistent legal battles and the discovery of crucial evidence, Jeremy was finally exonerated and declared factually innocent, highlighting the systemic issues of snitch testimony, prosecutorial misconduct, and the immense personal toll of wrongful conviction.
