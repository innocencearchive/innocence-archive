### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction recounts the harrowing story of Stephon Morant, wrongfully convicted alongside his friend Scott Lewis for a double homicide in New Haven, Connecticut in 1990.  The narrative details how corrupt Detective Vincent Rauchy, at the behest of drug kingpin Frank Parisi, coerced Morant into a false confession and incentivized a mentally ill witness, Ovil Ruiz, to fabricate testimony implicating Morant and Lewis. Despite recanting his false statement quickly, Morant was convicted and sentenced to 70 years.  The podcast highlights the systemic failures, including ineffective defense counsel and prosecutorial misconduct, that perpetuated this injustice for over two decades.  Ultimately, through the efforts of law students, an FBI investigation, and the intervention of Detective Sweeney, who came forward with crucial information, Morant and Lewis's case was re-examined. Stephon Morant was eventually granted an absolute pardon, marking the end of a long fight for freedom and justice. The episode underscores themes of resilience, faith, and the ongoing struggle for exonerees to rebuild their lives after wrongful imprisonment.
