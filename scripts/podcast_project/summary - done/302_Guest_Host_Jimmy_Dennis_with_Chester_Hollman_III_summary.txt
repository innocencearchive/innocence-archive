### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction is guest-hosted by exoneree Jimmy Dennis, featuring Chester Holman III, another exoneree, and his attorney Alan Tauber.  Chester recounts his wrongful conviction for a 1991 Philadelphia murder, a case of mistaken identity exacerbated by a remarkable coincidence involving similar vehicles and license plates.  The episode details the flawed police investigation marked by tunnel vision, coercive interrogation of a key witness (Deirdre Jones), and reliance on a questionable eyewitness (Andre Dawkins) who later recanted.  Prosecutorial misconduct by Roger King, including withholding crucial evidence, further solidified the wrongful conviction.  Chester served 28 years before being exonerated in 2019 thanks to the efforts of his attorney and the newly elected District Attorney Larry Krasner's Conviction Integrity Unit. The podcast underscores systemic issues within the Philadelphia justice system and the profound personal impact of wrongful convictions.
