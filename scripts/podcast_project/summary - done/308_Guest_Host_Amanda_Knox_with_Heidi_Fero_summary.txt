### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction, guest-hosted by exoneree Amanda Knox, features the story of Heidi Farrow, wrongfully convicted of first-degree assault of a child due to a Shaken Baby Syndrome (SBS) diagnosis. In 2002, while babysitting 15-month-old Bryn, Heidi sought medical help when Bryn became unresponsive. Despite evidence suggesting Bryn's four-year-old brother, Cade, exhibited violent behavior towards Bryn and potential pre-existing injuries, Heidi was convicted based on the SBS diagnosis and sentenced to 15 years, later reduced to 10.  The podcast details the trial's reliance on now-questionable medical testimony, Heidi's experiences in prison, and the long fight for her legal relief, which was complicated and emotionally taxing even after release. Heidi, alongside Laura Zyrowski from the Washington Innocence Project, highlights the flaws in the SBS diagnosis as forensic science and the immense personal toll of wrongful convictions, advocating for systemic change and support for exonerees.
