### Basic Podcast Information

- **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction, guest-hosted by Greg Glott and Clayton English from "The War on Drugs" podcast, tells the story of Louis Garcia, wrongfully convicted in San Antonio, Texas, due to a flawed drug raid based on an unreliable confidential informant. Louis, an electrician, was helping a neighbor when police raided her home and found drugs planted by a person using the guise of needing to use the restroom. Despite maintaining his innocence and initial legal optimism, Louis was pressured to plead guilty and served eight years in prison.  His case was eventually overturned thanks to the work of the Conviction Integrity Unit and lawyer Dana Jones, who highlighted the systemic issues with confidential informants and police practices in Bear County.  The episode underscores the devastating impact of wrongful convictions and the importance of conviction integrity units.

    - Over-reliance on unreliable confidential informants.

    - Pressure on defendants to take plea deals, especially when incarcerated.
    - Potential for police misconduct (planted evidence, camera inconsistencies).
    - Issues within Bear County Narcotics Division (historical problems).
    - Lack of compensation for wrongful conviction in Texas if not declared "actually innocent".
