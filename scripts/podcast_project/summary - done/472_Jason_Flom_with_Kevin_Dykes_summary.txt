### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom tells the harrowing story of Kevin Dykes, who was wrongfully convicted in 1986 based on the unreliable testimony of jailhouse snitches.  Despite being initially a witness and even a victim in related events, Kevin was accused of murder and attempted murder, crimes actually committed by two drug dealers, Slim and Hondo.  His conviction rested solely on the accounts of three snitches, all of whom had incentives to lie for leniency in their own cases.  The episode details the systemic failures that led to his wrongful conviction and the decades-long fight for his freedom.  It highlights the devastating impact of jailhouse snitch testimony and the slow progress towards justice, even with recantations and legal reforms.  Ultimately, after 38 years, Kevin was released on parole in 2024, offering a glimmer of hope while underscoring the urgent need for systemic change to prevent similar injustices.
