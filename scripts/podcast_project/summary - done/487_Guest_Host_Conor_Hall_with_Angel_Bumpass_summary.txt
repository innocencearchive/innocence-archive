### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features the story of Angel Bumpus, who was wrongfully convicted of felony murder and aggravated robbery in Chattanooga, Tennessee, for a crime committed when she was 13 years old.  The sole piece of evidence linking her to the 2009 murder of Franklin Bonner was a partial fingerprint found on duct tape at the crime scene, which matched her prints nine years later after being entered into the system for a traffic ticket. Despite a lack of other evidence, an implausible narrative, and ineffective trial counsel, Angel was convicted and sentenced to 60 years in prison.  After nearly four years of incarceration, and with the help of new attorney Bill Massey, her conviction was overturned due to insufficient evidence and ineffective assistance of counsel.  The charges were ultimately dismissed in August 2023, leading to her exoneration and release.  The episode highlights the dangers of fingerprint evidence, ineffective legal representation, and the flaws in the juvenile justice system.
