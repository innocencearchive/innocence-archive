- **Systemic Issues Identified**:
    - Legal allowance of police deception during interrogations.
    - The immense weight juries place on confession evidence, often overriding other forms of evidence.
    - Tunnel vision and confirmation bias in police investigations, leading to premature case closure after obtaining a confession.
    - Lack of mandatory recording of police interrogations.
    - Insufficient safeguards for vulnerable individuals (youth, those in shock/trauma) during police interrogations.
- **Policy Recommendations/Calls to Action**:
    - Mandatory recording of all police interrogations.
    - Re-evaluation of the legality and ethical implications of police deception during interrogations.
    - Increased public awareness and education about the phenomenon of false confessions.
    - Support for organizations like the Innocence Project to help exonerate the wrongfully convicted.
