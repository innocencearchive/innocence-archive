### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction" features Mark Denny, who was wrongfully convicted and served 30 years in prison for a brutal rape and robbery at a Burger King in Brooklyn in 1987.  Denny, just 16 at the time of the crime, was implicated by his cousin out of spite after being arrested with him on an unrelated charge. Despite the victim initially identifying three other individuals and maintaining there were only three assailants, detectives used suggestive lineup techniques to pressure her into identifying Denny as a fourth assailant.  Key evidence against Denny was a false statement from his cousin, which was later recanted.  The podcast details the flawed investigation, the trauma Denny endured in prison, and his eventual exoneration in 2017 thanks to the Brooklyn Conviction Review Unit and the Innocence Project. Denny reflects on the systemic issues within the justice system and the importance of truth.
