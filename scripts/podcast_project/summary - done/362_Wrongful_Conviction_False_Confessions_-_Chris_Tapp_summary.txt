### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction: False Confessions tells the story of Chris Tapp, who at 20 years old, was coerced into confessing to the 1996 rape and murder of Angie Dodge after a grueling 25-hour interrogation. Despite lacking DNA evidence and even confessing to details that were factually incorrect about the crime scene, Tapp was convicted and served 20 years.  Angie Dodge's mother, Carol Dodge, initially sought the death penalty for Tapp, but later became convinced of his innocence after reviewing interrogation tapes and researching false confessions.  Carol Dodge became a fierce advocate for Tapp's exoneration and relentlessly pushed for further DNA testing.  Her persistence led to the use of genetic genealogy, which ultimately identified Brian Dripps as the actual perpetrator. Tapp was exonerated in 2019, and Dripps was later arrested. The episode highlights the dangers of coercive interrogation tactics, the flaws in the justice system, and the extraordinary impact of Carol Dodge's unwavering pursuit of truth and justice.
