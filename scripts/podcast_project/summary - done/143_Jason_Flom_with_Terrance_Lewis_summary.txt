### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom tells the harrowing story of Terence Lewis, wrongfully convicted in 1999 for a murder he did not commit and sentenced to life without parole.  The case hinged on the unreliable eyewitness testimony of a crack cocaine user and an anonymous tip, while police ignored evidence pointing to another suspect, Hakeem Sade Muhammad, known as "Stink".  Despite multiple appeals and even a magistrate judge acknowledging his likely innocence, Lewis spent over 21 years in prison.  His exoneration in 2021 came after the Philadelphia Conviction Integrity Unit uncovered a crucial Brady violation: police had known the real "Stink's" identity from the beginning.  Now a free man, Lewis has founded the Liberation Foundation to advocate for other wrongfully convicted individuals and received a $6.25 million settlement for his ordeal. His story highlights systemic issues within the justice system, including police misconduct, unreliable eyewitness testimony, and the devastating impact of wrongful convictions.
