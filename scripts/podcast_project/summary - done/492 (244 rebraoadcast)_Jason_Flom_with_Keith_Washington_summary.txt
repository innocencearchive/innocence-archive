### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction delves into the deeply troubling case of Kevin Cooper, who was convicted and sentenced to death for the 1983 Ryan family murders in Chino Hills, California.  Despite initial witness descriptions pointing to three white or Hispanic men, and significant evidence of police misconduct, evidence tampering, and potential alternate suspects like Lee Furrow, Kevin Cooper, a Black man who had escaped from a nearby prison, became the primary suspect. The podcast highlights the egregious mishandling of the crime scene, destruction of evidence, and manipulation of forensic findings that led to Cooper's wrongful conviction.  It features interviews with Kevin Cooper from San Quentin prison and his attorney Norman Heil, detailing the systemic failures and racial biases within the justice system that have kept Cooper incarcerated for decades, and the ongoing fight for his exoneration, including Governor Newsom's ordered innocence investigation.
