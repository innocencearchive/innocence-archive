### Basic Podcast Information

- **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction: False Confessions" continues the story of Tommy Ward and Carl Fontenot, wrongfully convicted for the murder of Denise Haraway in Ada, Oklahoma. Their convictions were based on confessions obtained after lengthy police interrogations, confessions later proven to be factually incorrect and derived from a dream Tommy Ward described.  A key piece of prosecution evidence was a detail about the victim's blouse, seemingly unknown to police until the confessions. However, newly discovered evidence, 860 pages withheld by prosecution, revealed police knew about the blouse description prior to interrogations and contained alibi evidence for Tommy Ward.  This revelation led to Carl Fontenot's release in 2019 after 34 years, and in December 2020, Tommy Ward's conviction was also overturned, though he remained in custody pending a prosecutor's appeal at the time of this recording. The episode underscores the devastating consequences of false confessions and prosecutorial misconduct within the justice system.
