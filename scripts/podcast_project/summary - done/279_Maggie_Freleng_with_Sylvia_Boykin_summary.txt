### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling tells the story of Sylvia Boykin, wrongfully convicted of first-degree murder in 1994.  Sylvia was involved in a drug debt collection that turned deadly, though she did not shoot anyone.  Coerced into signing a statement by police, and with inadequate legal representation, she was convicted based largely on the testimony of an individual with a vested interest.  The podcast highlights Sylvia's traumatic past, substance abuse issues, and how systemic gender bias in drug markets contributed to her wrongful conviction.  Dr. Jill McCorkle from the Philadelphia Justice Project for Women and Girls advocates for Sylvia's commutation, emphasizing her vulnerability and the miscarriage of justice. Despite her life sentence, Sylvia has become a certified peer specialist in prison and hopes to help others upon release. A petition for her commutation is mentioned, indicating ongoing efforts to secure her freedom.
