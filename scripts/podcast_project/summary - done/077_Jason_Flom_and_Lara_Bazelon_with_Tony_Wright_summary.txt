-   **Systemic Issues Identified**:
    -   Police misconduct leading to wrongful convictions.
    -   Eyewitness misidentification as a significant cause of wrongful convictions.
    -   Lack of adequate compensation for exonerees in many states, creating a "second punishment."
    -   Prosecutorial misconduct and lack of accountability.
    -   Ineffective defense counsel.
    -   Unconscious bias in the justice system, including racial bias in jury decisions.
    -   Disparities in how drug-related issues are addressed based on the demographics of those affected (e.g., crack epidemic vs. opioid crisis).

-   **Policy Recommendations/Calls to Action**:
    -   Mandate compensation for all exonerees in every state to ensure justice and support for re-entry.
    -   Strongly enforce state bar rules to hold prosecutors and defense lawyers accountable for misconduct and ineffective assistance.
    -   Provide comprehensive support and therapy for victims of wrongful conviction, including both exonerees and original crime victims and their families.
    -   Emphasize the importance of jury duty and educate potential jurors on unconscious bias and the presumption of innocence.
    -   Support organizations like the Innocence Project to help prevent future wrongful convictions.
    -   Promote restorative justice practices as a means of healing and reconciliation in the aftermath of wrongful convictions.
