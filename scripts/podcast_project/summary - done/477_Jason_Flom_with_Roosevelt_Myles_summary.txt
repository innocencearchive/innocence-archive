### Basic Podcast Information

- **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction tells the harrowing story of Roosevelt Miles, wrongfully convicted in Chicago for a murder he did not commit.  Miles details his challenging childhood and early brushes with the law before being ensnared by corrupt Area 5 gang unit detectives, including the notorious Anthony Wojczyk.  Coerced witness testimony and fabricated evidence led to his 1996 conviction and a 60-year sentence, despite his innocence.  The podcast highlights the systemic issues within the Chicago justice system, including police misconduct and ineffective legal representation. After serving 28 years and persistent legal battles aided by the Bond Jean Law Group, Miles was finally exonerated in 2024.  The episode underscores the immense personal toll of wrongful convictions and the ongoing fight for justice and systemic reform in Chicago.
