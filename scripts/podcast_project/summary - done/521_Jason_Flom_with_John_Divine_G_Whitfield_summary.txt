### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This special edition of Wrongful Conviction features Jason Flom interviewing Divine G, a man wrongfully convicted of homicide, and his attorney Jeffrey Deskovic, also an exoneree.  The episode details Divine's life, the flawed case against him based on a unreliable informant, and the systemic issues that led to his wrongful conviction, particularly inadequate discovery laws.  Divine recounts his 25 years in prison, during which he founded the impactful Rehabilitation Through the Arts (RTA) program. The podcast highlights the ongoing struggle for Divine's exoneration despite compelling evidence of innocence, and calls to action to support his case and advocate for fair discovery laws, emphasizing the urgent need for justice system reform.  The discussion also touches upon the low recidivism rate of RTA and the power of art in rehabilitation.  Listeners are urged to sign a petition for Divine's exoneration and to contact lawmakers to support discovery law reforms.
