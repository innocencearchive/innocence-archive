### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction: False Confessions details the case of Daniel Villegas, wrongfully convicted of a double murder in El Paso, Texas, in 1993.  At 16, Daniel falsely confessed after coercive interrogation tactics by a detective known for obtaining coerced statements.  Despite inconsistencies in his confession and lack of physical evidence, Daniel was convicted in a second trial marked by ineffective legal representation.  John Mabela, a local businessman, championed Daniel's cause, funding legal efforts and public awareness campaigns that eventually led to a retrial.  Daniel's confession was suppressed in the new trial, and in 2018, after spending nearly two decades in prison, Daniel Villegas was fully exonerated. The episode underscores the dangers of false confessions, the flaws in the justice system, and the profound impact of dedicated advocacy in righting wrongful convictions.
