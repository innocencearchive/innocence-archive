### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features an interview with David Ayala, who was wrongfully convicted and served 42 years in Illinois, reportedly the longest wrongful conviction in the state's history.  The episode details the crime he was accused of—a gang-related shooting in Petroski Park in 1981—and how police targeted Ayala and his cousin Jimmy Soto, based on gang affiliation and personal vendetta.  Key factors contributing to his wrongful conviction included coerced witness testimony, particularly from a co-defendant who received a lenient sentence for his cooperation, and the pervasive influence of gang bias in the judicial process. The podcast highlights the systemic issues within the Chicago Police Department and the Cook County legal system that led to this miscarriage of justice.  Jennifer Bonjean, Ayala's lawyer, also joins the episode to discuss the legal battle for his exoneration and the immense challenges he faced, including years in solitary confinement.  Ayala shares his emotional journey, the horrors of Supermax prison, and his profound sense of gratitude for his newfound freedom after his exoneration in December 2023.
