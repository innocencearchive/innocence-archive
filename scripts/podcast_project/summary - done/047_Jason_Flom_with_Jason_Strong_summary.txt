### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Jason Strong, who was wrongfully convicted of murder and spent 15 and a half years in prison in Illinois. Jason shares his harrowing experience of being arrested, tried, and convicted based on false testimony and police misconduct, despite his innocence. He details the incompetence of his public defenders and his initial despair in prison.  Jason recounts his decision to fight for his freedom, becoming his own lawyer and eventually gaining the support of the Northwestern Innocence Project.  The episode highlights the systemic issues contributing to wrongful convictions, including police misconduct, unreliable forensic evidence, and inadequate legal representation.  Ultimately, Jason was exonerated after new evidence and reinvestigation revealed the likely actual perpetrators. He emphasizes the devastating impact of wrongful convictions and calls for greater awareness and accountability within the justice system.
