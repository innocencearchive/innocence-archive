### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction: False Confessions" delves into the disturbing case of Tommy Ward and Carl Fontano, who were convicted for the murder of Denise Haraway based on coerced confessions.  The hosts, Lauren Nye-writer and Steve Drizen, explain how police in Ada, Oklahoma, manipulated Tommy Ward's "bad dream" into a murder confession, and similarly coerced a confession from Carl Fontano, who was intellectually disabled and traumatized. Despite inconsistencies in their confessions and lack of corroborating evidence, except for a detail about the victim's blouse, both men were sentenced to death.  The episode highlights the dangers of false confessions, particularly dream confessions, and sets the stage for the next episode to reveal further developments and the ongoing fight for Tommy and Carl's exoneration after 35 years of imprisonment.  The case gained wider attention through John Grisham's book and a Netflix series, emphasizing the profound injustice suffered by Tommy and Carl.
