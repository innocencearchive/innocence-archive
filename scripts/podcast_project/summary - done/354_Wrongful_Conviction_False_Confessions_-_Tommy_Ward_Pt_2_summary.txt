### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction: False Confessions" continues the story of Tommy Ward and Carl Fontenot, wrongfully convicted in Ada, Oklahoma, for the murder of Denise Haraway based on false confessions derived from Tommy's dream. Despite the discovery of Denise's body revealing inconsistencies with their confessions (location, cause of death), they were convicted again based on a detail about the victim's blouse that was seemingly corroborated by their confessions.  However, newly discovered evidence, withheld by the prosecution, revealed that police knew about the blouse description before the interrogations.  This suppressed evidence, along with Carl Fontenot's recantation and inconsistencies in witness testimonies, ultimately led to Carl's release after 34 years. Tommy Ward remains incarcerated, awaiting a decision in his case, highlighting the egregious miscarriage of justice and the devastating impact of false confessions.
