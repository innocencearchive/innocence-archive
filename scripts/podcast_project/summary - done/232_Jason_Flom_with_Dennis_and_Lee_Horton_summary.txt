### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction" features the harrowing story of brothers Lee and Dennis Horton, who were wrongfully convicted of armed robbery and murder in Philadelphia in 1993 and served nearly 27 years in prison.  Despite flawed witness identifications, lack of physical evidence, and a confession from the actual perpetrator, the brothers were convicted and sentenced to life without parole. The podcast highlights the systemic issues within the Philadelphia justice system in the 1990s, including inadequate legal representation and procedural hurdles that prevented exculpatory evidence from being considered.  Throughout their imprisonment, Lee and Dennis maintained their innocence and dedicated themselves to self-improvement and helping fellow inmates.  Their eventual release came through commutation, significantly aided by the advocacy of Lieutenant Governor John Fetterman.  The brothers emphasize the importance of criminal justice reform and public accountability to prevent similar injustices and support those still wrongfully incarcerated.
