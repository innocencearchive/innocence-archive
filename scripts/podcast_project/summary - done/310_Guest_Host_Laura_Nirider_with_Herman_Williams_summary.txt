### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction, guest-hosted by Lauren Nye-Rider, tells the harrowing story of Hermann Williams, who was wrongfully convicted of the murder of his ex-wife, Penny, and served 29 years in prison. Despite maintaining his innocence and having a strong alibi, Williams was convicted based on a fabricated confession attributed to Detective Lou Tessman and manipulated time-of-death testimony. The podcast highlights the systemic failures in Lake County, Illinois, including police tunnel vision, prosecutorial misconduct by Mike Marmel, and ineffective defense counsel.  DNA evidence and a re-evaluation of the forensic pathology eventually led to Williams' exoneration in 2022.  The episode underscores the devastating impact of wrongful convictions and calls for systemic reforms and accountability for misconduct.
