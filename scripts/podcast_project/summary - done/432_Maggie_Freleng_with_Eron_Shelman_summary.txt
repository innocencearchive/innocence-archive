### Basic Podcast Information

- **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling tells the story of Iran Shellman, who was wrongfully convicted of first-degree murder in 1992 for the death of Antonio Knight. Despite a confession from Andre Rice, who admitted to shooting Knight and stated Shellman was uninvolved, Shellman was convicted and sentenced to life without parole. The podcast details Shellman's difficult upbringing, the events leading to Knight's death, the flawed trial with separate juries for co-defendants, and the ineffective assistance of his counsel.  After serving nearly 31 years, Shellman's conviction was vacated in January 2023 due to new evidence and ineffective counsel claims. He was released but faces an appeal from the prosecution, highlighting the ongoing battle for his full exoneration and the systemic issues within the criminal justice system.
