### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features the story of Greg Brown, who was wrongfully convicted of arson and murder in connection with a 1995 fire that tragically killed three firefighters in Pittsburgh.  Despite evidence suggesting the fire was accidental, fueled by a gas-powered lawnmower, and initial lab results being negative for accelerants,  Brown and his mother, Darlene Buckner, were targeted.  The investigation was marred by junk science in arson investigation, and crucially, by paid witnesses who provided incentivized testimony against Brown. He served 20 years in prison before his conviction was overturned due to the exposure of the paid witness scheme.  However, even after release and facing continued legal challenges, Brown ultimately accepted an Alford plea to conclude the case, highlighting the profound systemic failures within the justice system and the long-lasting impact on the wrongfully convicted.
