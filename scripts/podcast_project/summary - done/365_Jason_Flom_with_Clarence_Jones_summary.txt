### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction tells the harrowing story of Clarence Jones III, who was wrongfully convicted of murdering his infant son, Colin, in 1999.  Clarence and his attorney, Lauren Kelleher, detail how the now-discredited "shaken baby syndrome" (SBS) theory and flawed medical testimony led to his conviction.  Despite no evidence of violent shaking and a clear medical history of Colin's pre-existing conditions, including a bleeding disorder and sepsis, the medical and legal systems rushed to judgment.  Clarence spent 18 years in prison before being exonerated in 2021 thanks to the Mid-Atlantic Innocence Project. The episode highlights the devastating impact of junk science on the justice system and the urgent need for reform, emphasizing the personal tragedy and systemic failures that Clarence endured.
