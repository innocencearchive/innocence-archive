### Basic Podcast Information

- **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom details the horrific case of Roddy Long, wrongfully convicted in 1976 for rape and burglary.  The episode highlights the lack of evidence against Long, relying solely on a flawed cross-racial eyewitness identification by the victim, despite her initial inability to identify him in court.  Crucially, forensic evidence collected at the crime scene, which exonerated Long, was deliberately withheld by the police from both the prosecution and defense.  The episode exposes systemic issues of racial bias in the justice system, particularly in 1970s North Carolina, and the egregious misconduct by law enforcement in fabricating evidence and manipulating the jury selection process.  After serving 44 years, Long's conviction was finally overturned in 2020 due to the suppressed evidence and persistent legal efforts. The episode features an interview with Roddy Long himself and Jamie Lau, an attorney from Duke University's wrongful convictions clinic, emphasizing the long and arduous fight for justice and Long's resilience throughout his ordeal.
