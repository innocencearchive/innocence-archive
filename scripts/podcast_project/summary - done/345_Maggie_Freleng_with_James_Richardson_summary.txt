### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling" tells the story of James Richardson, a former professional basketball player wrongfully convicted of murdering two white men in Greenville, North Carolina in 2009. Despite inconsistent witness descriptions and alibi evidence, Richardson was convicted based largely on the testimony of a late-discovered eyewitness and a questionable surveillance video. The podcast highlights potential racial bias, as Richardson is Black and the victims were white, and explores systemic issues such as juror intimidation and prosecutorial misconduct.  Post-conviction efforts by attorneys Heather Radillade and Dawn Blagrove are detailed, including recanted testimony, evidence of a manipulated video, and juror concerns about racial bias.  Richardson maintains his innocence and his legal team continues to fight for his exoneration.
