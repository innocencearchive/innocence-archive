### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction: False Confessions details the wrongful conviction of Hamad Hayat, a U.S. citizen accused of terrorism after 9/11.  Fueled by post-9/11 panic, the FBI relied on a paid informant with a history of lies who targeted Hayat.  Despite a lack of credible evidence, Hayat was interrogated for hours, leading to a coerced and nonsensical false confession about attending a terrorist training camp and ninja turtle pole-vaulters.  His trial lawyer provided inadequate defense, and he was convicted and sentenced to 24 years.  Years later, new lawyers and alibi witnesses from Pakistan proved his innocence.  After serving 14 years, his conviction was overturned due to ineffective counsel, and he was exonerated, highlighting the dangers of fear-driven investigations and false confessions in the justice system.
