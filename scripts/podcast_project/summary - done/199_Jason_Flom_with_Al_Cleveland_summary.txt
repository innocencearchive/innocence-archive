### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom tells the harrowing story of Al Cleveland, who spent 25 years in prison for a double murder he did not commit in Lorraine, Ohio.  The case hinged on the unreliable testimony of William Avery Jr., the teenage son of a corrupt police informant, who falsely implicated Al and three other men for a $2,000 reward. Despite Avery Jr.'s repeated recantations and Al's verifiable alibi in New York, the prosecution proceeded, highlighting systemic failures in the Ohio legal system. Al describes the devastating impact of his wrongful conviction, his fight for survival and education in prison, and the unwavering support of his wife and the Ohio Innocence Project, which ultimately led to his parole.  The episode underscores the injustice of wrongful convictions, the devastating effects of police corruption and reliance on unreliable informants, and the ongoing fight for full exoneration and justice for Al and his remaining co-defendants.
