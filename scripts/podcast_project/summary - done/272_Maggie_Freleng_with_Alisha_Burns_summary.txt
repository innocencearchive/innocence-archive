### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling" tells the story of Alicia Burns, who at 15 years old, was a foster child and victim of sex trafficking.  She was manipulated by her trafficker, Stephen Kismeric, into participating in a robbery that resulted in the death of Pedro Villarreal. Despite her being a victim of trafficking and coercion, Alicia was charged as a co-defendant in the murder.  Due to manipulation and a lack of understanding of the legal system, she pleaded guilty to second-degree murder and served 16 years in prison. The podcast highlights systemic failures in the foster care system, the criminalization of trafficking victims, and the long-lasting impact of trauma and manipulation.  Alicia's case is now being re-examined, focusing on evidence suggesting another person may have been involved after Alicia and Kismeric left the scene and the fact that Alicia was a trafficking victim.  Alicia is now working to rebuild her life and advocate for other victims.
