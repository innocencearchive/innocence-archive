### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling" tells the story of Brian Parnell, wrongfully convicted of the 1997 murder of Gus Boulius.  Despite having alibi witnesses and no connection to the victim, Brian was arrested four years after the crime based on partial fingerprint evidence.  Detective Beam fixated on Brian after the FBI identified him as a potential match from a national database.  The prosecution lacked motive, witnesses, and a murder weapon, relying solely on the fingerprint.  Brian's defense team failed to present alibi witnesses or evidence of his physical limitations at the time of the murder.  He was convicted in a trial lasting under two days and sentenced to life without parole, highlighting systemic issues with fingerprint evidence and ineffective legal representation.
