### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Larry Smith Jr., who was wrongfully convicted of murder in Detroit in 1994 and served nearly 27 years in prison. The case against Smith was built on flawed eyewitness accounts, planted evidence (shell casings and a boot print), and false testimony from a jailhouse informant with a history of lying.  The police disregarded evidence that contradicted their narrative, including the victim being shot with a different caliber weapon than the planted casings.  Smith's defense was inadequate, failing to uncover crucial information like the girlfriend's original statement and the informant's history.  He was eventually exonerated due to the efforts of a conviction integrity unit that recognized systemic issues with jailhouse informants in Wayne County and the pervasive corruption in his case.  Smith now works at a rec center in Detroit, and the episode highlights the devastating impact of wrongful convictions and the urgent need for criminal justice reform, including addressing issues with snitch testimony and police misconduct.
