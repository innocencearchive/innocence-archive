### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This promotional segment features Rabia Jaudri and Jason Flom, hosts of the podcasts "Undisclosed" and "Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom," discussing crucial legal rights when interacting with law enforcement.  They highlight the right to remain silent and advise individuals to clearly state this right when stopped by police.  The segment also addresses "stop and identify" statutes in 22 states, the right to refuse searches without consent, and the right to record police activity from a safe distance (while being aware of state laws).  The hosts emphasize the importance of knowing, understanding, and exercising these rights to ensure safety and avoid legal complications during police encounters. The segment serves as an advertisement to encourage listeners to tune into their respective podcasts for more in-depth content related to legal rights and wrongful convictions.
