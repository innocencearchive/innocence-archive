### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling tells the story of James D'Gorski, who was wrongfully convicted for the 1993 Brown's Chicken massacre in Palatine, Illinois. Despite the horrific nature of the crime where seven people were murdered, the podcast reveals a lack of physical evidence linking D'Gorski to the scene.  The prosecution's case heavily relied on the testimony of two women, one an ex-girlfriend with questionable motives and the other a former friend whose reliability is also questioned.  D'Gorski maintains his innocence, claiming his confession was coerced after relentless interrogation and false promises.  The episode highlights systemic issues including potential police misconduct, the impact of public pressure on investigations, and the challenges of overturning wrongful convictions, especially in high-profile cases.  The podcast emphasizes the devastating personal impact of wrongful conviction on D'Gorski and his family, and his current legal fight for exoneration.
