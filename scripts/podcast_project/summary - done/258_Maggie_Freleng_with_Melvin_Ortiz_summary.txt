### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling" tells the story of Melvin Ortiz, who was wrongfully convicted of second-degree murder in the 1997 robbery and killing of George Clouser. Despite having a strong alibi supported by 19 witnesses, and no physical evidence linking him to the crime, Ortiz was convicted based on the testimony of a man named John Calte Jerome and his girlfriend, Tina Valentine.  The podcast highlights the lack of effective defense, the influence of a politically connected witness, and the subsequent recantation of testimony that could have exonerated Ortiz.  It explores the devastating impact of wrongful conviction on Ortiz and his family, including his relationship with Victoria Blanco, who remained a steadfast supporter. The episode underscores systemic issues within the justice system, including time-barred evidence rules and prosecutorial misconduct, while advocating for a re-evaluation of Ortiz's case and similar wrongful convictions.
