### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Ricky Jackson, who was wrongfully convicted along with Wiley Bridgman and Ronnie Bridgman for a 1975 murder in Cleveland, Ohio.  Their conviction rested solely on the coerced testimony of a 12-year-old boy, Eddie Vernon, while evidence pointing to other suspects was ignored by police. Ricky, Wiley, and Ronnie were initially sentenced to death, later commuted to life.  The podcast details the crime, the flawed investigation, the trial, and Ricky's decades-long fight for exoneration.  Ricky describes the devastating impact of wrongful conviction, the systemic issues within the justice system, and his eventual exoneration in 2014 after Eddie Vernon recanted his testimony.  The episode highlights the importance of abolishing the death penalty and the enduring trauma of wrongful conviction.
