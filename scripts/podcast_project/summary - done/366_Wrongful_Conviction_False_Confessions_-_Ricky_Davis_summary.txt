### Basic Podcast Information

- **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction: False Confessions" recounts the wrongful conviction of Ricky Davis for the 1985 murder of Jane Hilton.  Despite initial evidence suggesting his and his girlfriend Connie Dahl's innocence, the case was reopened 14 years later.  Detectives used manipulative interrogation tactics on Connie, leading to a coerced false confession that implicated Ricky.  Based on this false testimony, Ricky was convicted in 2005 and served 12 years. The Northern California Innocence Project took his case, and DNA testing in 2019 proved his innocence and identified the actual killer, Michael Green, through genetic genealogy. Ricky Davis was exonerated in 2020, highlighting the devastating consequences of false confessions and the importance of DNA evidence in rectifying wrongful convictions. The story also underscores systemic issues in interrogation practices and the long-lasting impact on the wrongfully accused and their families.
