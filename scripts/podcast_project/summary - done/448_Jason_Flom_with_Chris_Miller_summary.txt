### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction details the harrowing story of Chris Miller, wrongfully convicted in Cuyahoga County, Ohio, for rape, robbery, and other charges, leading to a 60-year sentence. Despite a lack of physical evidence and DNA exclusions, Miller was convicted based on a questionable eyewitness identification influenced by suggestive police tactics and a stolen cell phone found in his possession post-crime.  The episode exposes police misconduct by Detective Schmidt, who manipulated evidence and coerced a false confession from a co-defendant. Years later, DNA evidence definitively linked Richard Marlin Stadmire and Charles Boyd to the crime, leading to Miller's exoneration after serving years in prison. The podcast highlights systemic issues within the justice system, emphasizing the devastating impact of wrongful convictions and the urgent need for reform. The episode concludes with a call to action, urging listeners to recognize the prevalence of wrongful convictions and advocate for change.
