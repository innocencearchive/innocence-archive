### Basic Podcast Information

- **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features the story of Dante West, who was wrongfully convicted in Kansas for conspiracy to distribute marijuana.  Dante and his friends were pulled over while traveling, and despite the drugs being found in a different car and the only evidence being the testimony of an informant claiming Dante planned to sell him marijuana, Dante was convicted and sentenced to nearly eight years in prison. The podcast highlights the harsh drug laws in Kansas, the potential for over-policing and profiling, and issues with ineffective legal counsel.  Dante fought his conviction from prison, eventually leading to his exoneration after his lawyer uncovered a Brady violation related to the informant's undisclosed deal with law enforcement.  The episode emphasizes the devastating impact of wrongful convictions on individuals and families, and the need for criminal justice reform, particularly regarding cannabis laws. Dante is now a successful entrepreneur in the legal cannabis industry and an advocate for criminal justice reform.
