### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Kion Katibi, who recounts his harrowing experience of being wrongfully convicted of a stabbing in Pleasantville, New York, despite having an alibi of being at the police station at the time of the incident.  The podcast details the flawed police investigation, including suggestive interrogation techniques, withheld exculpatory evidence like a video tape and bloody clothing, and coerced witness testimonies.  Kion describes his trial, wrongful conviction, and the years spent in maximum-security prison.  Ultimately, his exoneration came after his brother confessed to the crime.  Kion reflects on the systemic injustices he faced and how this experience motivated him to become a criminal defense attorney, now fighting for others who may be wrongfully convicted, including Darwin Roque, whose case is highlighted at the end of the episode.
