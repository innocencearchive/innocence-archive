### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling tells the story of Eddie Ramirez, who was wrongfully convicted of second-degree murder in Philadelphia in 1998 and spent 27 and a half years in prison. The episode details Eddie's life before and during incarceration, emphasizing his innocence and the unwavering support of his family.  It uncovers the flawed investigation, relying on coerced witness testimonies from teenagers and suppressed DNA evidence that could have exonerated him earlier.  The podcast highlights the systemic issues within the justice system, including police pressure on vulnerable witnesses ("homicide hotel") and prosecutorial misconduct in withholding exculpatory evidence.  Finally, after years of appeals and the work of the Philadelphia Innocence Project, Eddie's conviction was vacated in 2023, and he was released, reclaiming his life and aiming to help others. The narrative underscores the devastating impact of wrongful convictions and the long fight for justice.
