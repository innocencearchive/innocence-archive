### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom" features the story of Kevin Dykes, wrongfully convicted in 1986 in Compton, California.  Dykes was initially a witness in a murder and attempted murder case involving two men, Slim and Hondo, after he reported their crimes to the police. Subsequently, he was arrested on a cocaine possession charge and placed in a holding tank with state witnesses, where three jailhouse snitches claimed he confessed to the crimes committed by Slim and Hondo. Despite lacking physical evidence and victims testifying he was not the perpetrator, Dykes was convicted based solely on snitch testimony, and sentenced to life.  Even after two snitches recanted and a new law against uncorroborated snitch testimony was enacted, Dykes remains incarcerated, highlighting systemic issues within the criminal justice system and the difficulty of overturning wrongful convictions. His attorney and the podcast host express hope for his release through parole or intervention from a progressive District Attorney or the Governor.
