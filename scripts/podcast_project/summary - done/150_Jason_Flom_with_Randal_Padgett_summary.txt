### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom details the harrowing story of Randall Padgett, a poultry farmer wrongfully convicted of murdering his wife, Cathy, in 1990. Despite a lack of solid evidence and compelling alibi from his children, Padgett was convicted and sentenced to death based largely on DNA evidence of his semen found in his deceased wife, amidst an affair he was having.  The episode highlights prosecutorial misconduct, including withholding exculpatory blood evidence from the defense in the first trial.  Attorney Richard S. Jaffe took on Padgett's case for the retrial, uncovering crucial evidence pointing to Judy Smith, Padgett's former coworker and mistress, as a potential alternative suspect.  Judy Smith's bizarre behavior and admissions on the stand, combined with the previously withheld blood evidence, led to Padgett's exoneration after six years on death row.  The story underscores systemic issues in the justice system, including tunnel vision in investigations and the potential for juries to be swayed by circumstantial evidence despite exculpatory facts.
