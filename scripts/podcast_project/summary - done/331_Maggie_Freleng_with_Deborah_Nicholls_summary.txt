### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling" tells the tragic story of Deb Nichols, who was convicted of murdering her three children in a house fire in 2003.  Despite being out of the house at the time of the fire, Deb was convicted based on the prosecution's theory that she conspired with her husband Tim to commit arson for insurance money to fuel a supposed meth addiction. The prosecution's case relied heavily on now-discredited fire science expert testimony and a jailhouse informant.  The episode highlights the flaws in the investigation, the misinterpretation of Deb's grieving process, and the subsequent efforts by the Corrie Wise Innocence Project to overturn her conviction based on new fire science and the informant's unreliability. Deb maintains her innocence and seeks to clear her name and honor her children.
