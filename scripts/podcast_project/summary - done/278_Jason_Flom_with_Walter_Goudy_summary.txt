### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction tells the harrowing story of Walter Goudy, wrongfully convicted of murder and attempted murder in Indiana.  Despite having 16 alibi witnesses, Goudy was misidentified and prosecuted largely due to the zealous actions of Detective Rodney Cummings, who later became the prosecutor.  Key exculpatory evidence, including witness identifications of another suspect, Kaidi Harvel, and a confession from Goudy's half-brother, Romeo Lee, was withheld from the defense.  Goudy was sentenced to 110 years and spent 16 and a half years in prison fighting to prove his innocence.  His conviction was eventually overturned by the Seventh Circuit Court of Appeals due to Brady violations and ineffective assistance of counsel, highlighting severe systemic failures within the Indiana justice system. The episode underscores the devastating personal impact of wrongful convictions and the long fight for exoneration.

-   **Systemic Issues Identified**:
    - **Brady Violations**: Withholding of exculpatory evidence by the prosecution.
    - **Ineffective Assistance of Counsel**: Original trial attorney's failure to present key evidence.
    - **Suggestive Lineup Procedures**: Initial one-man show-up and subsequent lineups compromised by witness familiarity with fillers.
    - **Prosecutorial Misconduct/Zeal**: Rodney Cummings's personal vendetta and actions as both detective and prosecutor.
    - **Indiana "Work Product" Rule**:  Legal loophole allowing police reports with exculpatory evidence to be withheld.
    - **Judicial System Failures**: Repeated failures by judges and appellate courts at various levels to correct the wrongful conviction.
    - **Lack of Accountability**:  Rodney Cummings continues to serve as prosecutor despite his role in the wrongful conviction.
    - **Limited Compensation**:  Indiana's initial lack of compensation statute and subsequent statute that requires waiver of civil litigation rights upon accepting compensation.

-   **Policy Recommendations/Calls to Action**:
    - Reform of Indiana's "work product" rule to ensure access to police reports and exculpatory evidence for the defense.
    - Strengthening Brady compliance and accountability for prosecutors and law enforcement.
    - Review and reform of eyewitness identification procedures to minimize suggestiveness.
    - Reconsideration of Indiana's compensation statute to remove the waiver of civil litigation rights.
    - Call for citizens in Madison County to consider alternative candidates for prosecutor to replace Rodney Cummings.
