### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features the harrowing story of James Cloppelberg, wrongfully convicted of arson and murder in a 1984 fire that killed six people. Despite initial findings of accidental fire and lack of prior arson history, Cloppelberg was coerced into a false confession through brutal torture by Chicago PD officers under Lieutenant John Burge.  Key testimony against him came from a dubious witness and a fire captain using debunked arson science.  His trial was marred by ineffective defense, a biased judge, and prosecutorial misconduct including perjury and withholding exculpatory evidence. Cloppelberg spent 25 years in prison before being exonerated in 2012 thanks to the Exoneration Project.  The episode highlights systemic issues of police torture, junk science in arson investigations, and the challenges exonerees face upon release, even after receiving compensation.
