### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction Junk Science" delves into the flawed forensic practice of bite mark analysis.  Hosts Kate Judson and Josh Dubin, along with guest Chris Fabrikan from the Innocence Project, expose bite mark evidence as junk science, tracing its roots back to the Salem witch trials. The episode highlights how bite mark evidence, despite its lack of scientific validity and reliability, has been used to secure wrongful convictions, citing cases like *People versus Marks* and the Ted Bundy trial as pivotal in its acceptance.  It emphasizes the subjective interpretation involved in bite mark analysis, the lack of uniqueness in teeth marks on skin, and the alarming rate of misidentification by experts. The discussion underscores systemic issues in the justice system that prioritize convictions over sound science, and concludes with calls to action for jurors, legal professionals, and the public to critically evaluate forensic evidence and support organizations like the Innocence Project to prevent future wrongful convictions and rectify past injustices.
