### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction, False Confessions, tells the story of Daniel Villegas, wrongfully convicted of a double murder in El Paso, Texas, based on a coerced false confession at age 16.  Despite inconsistencies in his confession and a lack of physical evidence, he was convicted and served nearly two decades in prison.  The episode highlights the crucial role of John Mimbella, a local businessman who, upon learning of Daniel's case through family connections, dedicated himself to proving Daniel's innocence. Mimbella's relentless efforts, including hiring lawyers and investigators, organizing community support, and funding the legal battle, were instrumental in securing Daniel a new trial.  Ultimately, with the help of legal experts and the support of the wrongful conviction community, Daniel's confession was deemed inadmissible, and he was exonerated in 2018, showcasing the power of perseverance and external advocacy in the fight for justice.
