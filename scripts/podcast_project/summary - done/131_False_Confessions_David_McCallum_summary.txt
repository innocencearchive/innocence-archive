### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction: False Confessions tells the story of David McCallum and Willie Stuckey, two teenagers wrongfully convicted of murder in 1986 based on coerced confessions.  Despite inconsistencies in their confessions and lack of other evidence, they were convicted and sentenced to 25 years to life.  The episode details David's decades-long fight for exoneration, aided by famous exoneree Reuben Hurricane Carter and lawyer Steve Drizin.  New DNA evidence and the efforts of reform-minded Brooklyn District Attorney Ken Thompson ultimately led to David's exoneration in 2014 and a posthumous exoneration for Willie Stuckey. The episode highlights the devastating impact of false confessions, the importance of conviction review units, and the power of perseverance and dedicated advocacy in the fight for justice.  The story emphasizes the grace and humanity shown by DA Thompson's office during David's release, contrasting with typical exoneration processes.
