### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom" tells the story of Troy Coleman, who has been imprisoned for 31 years for a murder he did not commit in Philadelphia in 1989. Despite not being in Philadelphia at the time of the crime, Coleman was convicted based on shaky eyewitness identification from Arthur Sanders and coerced testimony from Darren Keith Johnson, both influenced by corrupt police tactics and pressure.  Key evidence includes Sanders' initial misidentification, Johnson's later recantation due to police coercion and threats, and a 2019 confession from Byron Johnson admitting his involvement and exonerating Coleman.  The podcast highlights systemic issues in the Philadelphia justice system during the 1980s and 90s, including police brutality and corruption. Despite multiple pieces of exculpatory evidence and recantations, Coleman remains incarcerated, emphasizing the arduous fight for justice for the wrongfully convicted and the ongoing efforts to bring his case to the attention of the Philadelphia Conviction Integrity Unit.  The episode calls for support for innocence projects and highlights Troy Coleman's website for further information on his case.
