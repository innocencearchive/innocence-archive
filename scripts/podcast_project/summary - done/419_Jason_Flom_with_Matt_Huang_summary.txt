### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction recounts the harrowing experience of Matt Huang and his family in Qatar.  Matt, an American civil engineer, moved to Qatar for a job opportunity with his wife and three adopted children.  Tragedy struck when their daughter Gloria passed away, leading to Matt and his wife being wrongfully accused of murder and organ harvesting based on racist assumptions by Qatari police.  Despite clear evidence of their innocence, including adoption papers and family photos, they faced a Kafkaesque legal battle in a system plagued by language barriers, lack of due process, and political influence.  They were initially charged with first-degree murder with the death penalty, then convicted of manslaughter and sentenced to three years before ultimately being acquitted on appeal after 23 months of legal ordeal. The case highlights systemic issues within the Qatari justice system and the challenges faced by foreigners navigating it, even with US government intervention.
