### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom tells the story of Pierre Rushing, wrongfully convicted of murder and serving a 50-year to life sentence.  The episode details the events of the crime, the flawed eyewitness identification by Robert Green, and the lack of physical evidence linking Pierre to the crime.  Despite a solid alibi and testimony from Lauren Richardson, Pierre was convicted based on questionable eyewitness testimony.  Post-conviction attorney Marvin Liu highlights crucial evidence like phone records and untested DNA that could exonerate Pierre, but were not presented or were suppressed. The episode exposes systemic issues including reliance on jailhouse informants, ineffective counsel, and the resistance to re-examine evidence in potential wrongful conviction cases. Pierre and Marvin appeal to the public to sign a petition for justice and to pressure authorities to test the DNA evidence.
