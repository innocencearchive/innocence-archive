### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom" features Anthony de Pippo, who spent 20 years in prison for the 1994 rape and murder of 12-year-old Josette Wright before being exonerated after a third trial.  De Pippo recounts his wrongful conviction, highlighting the flawed investigation, incentivized false witness testimonies, and the withholding of evidence pointing to Howard Gombert as the actual perpetrator.  The podcast details the unethical conduct of his initial attorney and the coercive tactics used by detectives to fabricate a case against De Pippo and his co-defendant, Andrew Krivak, who remains incarcerated. De Pippo shares his harrowing prison experience and his eventual acquittal, emphasizing the systemic failures within the criminal justice system and his ongoing fight for Krivak's freedom and broader criminal justice reform. He calls for support for organizations like the Innocence Project and the Jeffrey Deskovic Foundation.
