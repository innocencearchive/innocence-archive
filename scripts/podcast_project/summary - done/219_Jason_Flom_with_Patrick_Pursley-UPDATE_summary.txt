### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom features the story of Patrick Persley, wrongfully convicted of murder in Rockford, Illinois, and his 25-year fight for exoneration.  Persley details the flawed investigation including incentivized witness testimony, a coerced false confession from his girlfriend, and misleading ballistics evidence.  Despite overwhelming odds and systemic barriers, Persley educated himself in law, and remarkably, initiated a change in Illinois law to allow for post-conviction ballistics testing, ultimately leading to his freedom.  The episode highlights the profound personal impact of wrongful conviction, the flaws within the justice system, and Persley's inspiring resilience and advocacy for reform and compassion. Even after exoneration and release, Persley continues to fight for a certificate of actual innocence to access state compensation and pursue civil rights violations claims, illustrating the long and arduous path to justice for the wrongfully convicted.
