### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features the harrowing story of Ron Jacobson, wrongfully convicted of abduction and sexual assault in 1990 and exonerated in 2021. Despite a lack of physical evidence and initial statements from the victim identifying a stranger, police manipulated the victim into identifying Jacobson, her ex-boyfriend.  Jacobson's trial was marred by ineffective defense counsel, and he was sentenced to life in prison.  DNA evidence in 2017 proved his innocence, but the District Attorney fought against his exoneration, even using rape shield laws to block the DNA evidence.  After a change in District Attorney, charges were finally dropped in 2021. The episode highlights issues of police coercion, ineffective counsel, prosecutorial misconduct, and the immense personal toll of wrongful convictions.
