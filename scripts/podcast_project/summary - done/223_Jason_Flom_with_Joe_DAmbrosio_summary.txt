### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom tells the harrowing story of Jo De Ambrosio's wrongful conviction for the murder of Anthony Clann in Cleveland, Ohio, in 1988.  The narrative was built upon false eyewitness testimony from Edward Espinosa, a man with a motive and a history of violence, and supported by Paul Stoney Lewis, a drug dealer and rapist who had a strong reason to implicate others.  Despite a lack of physical evidence and a shifting timeline of events presented by the prosecution, Jo De Ambrosio and his co-defendant Michael Keenan were initially sentenced to death row.  Key to De Ambrosio's exoneration years later was the relentless work of Neil Kukute, a nurse, lawyer, and priest, who uncovered crucial exculpatory evidence, including alibi witnesses and evidence pointing to Stoney Lewis as a more likely suspect. This evidence, suppressed at the original trial, ultimately led to De Ambrosio's release and exoneration after years on death row. The episode highlights systemic issues within the justice system, including prosecutorial misconduct, ineffective defense counsel, and the dangers of relying on incentivized and unreliable witnesses.
