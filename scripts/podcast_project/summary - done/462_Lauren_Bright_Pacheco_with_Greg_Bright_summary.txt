### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Gregory Bright, who was wrongfully convicted of second-degree murder in 1975 and spent 27 and a half years in Louisiana State Penitentiary at Angola.  The episode details his arrest, the flawed trial based on the testimony of a drug addict for reward money, and the subsequent decades of incarceration.  Despite illiteracy upon entering prison, Bright taught himself to read and the law, eventually leading to the Innocence Project of New Orleans stepping in and securing his exoneration in 2003.  The conversation explores the profound impact of wrongful conviction, his resilience, and his collaboration with writer Lara Naughton to create a one-man play, "Never Fight a Shark in Water," based on his experiences.  The episode highlights systemic issues within the justice system and the enduring human spirit in the face of injustice.
