-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Elmer Daniels, who was wrongfully convicted of rape in 1980 and served 38 years in prison before being exonerated in 2018.  Guest host John Huffington, also a former exoneree, interviews Elmer and his attorney Emika Igway. The episode details the flawed investigation, including unreliable eyewitness identification, false hair analysis testimony by FBI agent Michael Malone, and ineffective defense counsel.  Elmer's alibi and a psychologist's assessment of his innocence were disregarded.  The podcast highlights systemic issues within the justice system, including the lack of compensation for wrongful conviction in Delaware and the long-lasting impact of wrongful imprisonment on exonerees and their fight for justice and re-entry into society.  Elmer and Emika discuss their ongoing efforts to advocate for legislative change in Delaware and Elmer's desire to help others facing similar injustices.
