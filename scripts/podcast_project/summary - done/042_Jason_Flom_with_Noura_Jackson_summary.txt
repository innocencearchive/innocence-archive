### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Nora Jackson, who was wrongfully convicted of her mother's murder and served 11 years in prison.  The episode details the horrific ordeal Nora endured, starting with the unsolved murder of her father and then the brutal murder of her mother, for which she was wrongly accused. It highlights the egregious prosecutorial misconduct by Amy Wyrick and Stephen Jones who withheld evidence and built a case based on flawed theories and irrelevant details like Nora wearing long sleeves.  Despite DNA evidence excluding her, Nora was convicted and sentenced to over 20 years. The Tennessee Supreme Court eventually overturned her conviction due to prosecutorial misconduct, but Nora still faced immense pressure to take a plea deal to secure her freedom, ultimately accepting a plea of voluntary manslaughter to avoid further unjust imprisonment.  The episode emphasizes the systemic failures within the justice system, the devastating impact on the wrongfully convicted, and the ongoing fight for justice and reform. Nora's resilience and advocacy for other exonerees are also highlighted.
