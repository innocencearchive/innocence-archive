### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Gary Drinkard, who was wrongfully convicted and sentenced to death for a murder he did not commit in Decatur, Alabama in 1993. Despite having a strong alibi and being physically incapable of committing the crime due to a back injury and location 35 miles away, Gary was convicted based on false testimony from his half-sister and her boyfriend, who sought leniency in their own legal troubles. A detective falsely testified about a confession on a wire recording.  Ineffective initial legal representation failed to present crucial evidence of Gary's innocence.  After years on death row and a retrial secured by Brian Stevenson and Randy Suskin, attorney Richard Jaffe joined the defense team, ultimately leading to Gary's exoneration. The episode highlights the systemic issues within the justice system, including prosecutorial misconduct, reliance on incentivized witnesses, and ineffective defense, underscoring the need to abolish the death penalty. Gary now works with Witness to Innocence to advocate against wrongful convictions and the death penalty.
