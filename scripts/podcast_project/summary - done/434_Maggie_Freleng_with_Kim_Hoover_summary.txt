### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling tells the story of Kim Hoover, wrongfully convicted in 2003 for the murder of a baby in her daycare and imprisoned for 19 years. The case hinged on a Shaken Baby Syndrome diagnosis, based on now outdated medical understanding.  Key medical witnesses for the prosecution testified to SBS based on symptoms observed.  However, critical medical records were not presented at trial by her defense.  The Wrongful Conviction Project at the Ohio Public Defender's Office took on Kim's case, uncovering evidence of a pre-existing brain injury in the child through autopsy tissue slides and expert re-evaluation. This new evidence led to the original coroner and a radiologist changing their opinions. Ultimately, the prosecutor's office agreed to dismiss all charges, and Kim was released in 2021, highlighting the flaws of the justice system and advancements in forensic science that can overturn wrongful convictions.
