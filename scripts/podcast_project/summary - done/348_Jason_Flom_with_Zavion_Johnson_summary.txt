### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features the story of Zavion Johnson, who was wrongfully convicted of child abuse resulting in death, related to shaken baby syndrome, after his four-month-old daughter Nadia died following a fall in the shower.  The episode details the faulty medical assumptions prevalent at the time of his 2002 trial, where expert witnesses testified that Nadia's injuries could only be from shaking.  Zavion maintained his innocence throughout his 17 years of incarceration.  The Northern California Innocence Project took on his case, and with the help of updated medical understanding and re-evaluation of the original medical evidence by experts, including a reversal from the original medical examiner, his conviction was vacated in 2017 and charges were dropped in 2018.  The episode highlights the dangers of relying on outdated or untested medical theories in the justice system and the importance of continuous scientific review.
