### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features the story of Ronnie Miller, wrongfully convicted in Illinois of sexually assaulting his young daughter amidst a contentious custody battle.  The accusation surfaced after Ronnie threatened to sue for full custody due to concerns about his daughter's safety in her mother's home.  Despite the daughter recanting her accusation multiple times, and the lack of physical evidence, a nurse practitioner's testimony about suspicious medical history led to Ronnie's conviction and a 20-year sentence.  Ineffective counsel, failure to present exculpatory evidence including the mother's history of weaponizing family court and the daughter's recantations, and questionable expert testimony contributed to the wrongful conviction. Ronnie maintains his innocence, and his post-conviction appeals are ongoing, highlighting systemic issues within the justice system and the devastating impact on families.
