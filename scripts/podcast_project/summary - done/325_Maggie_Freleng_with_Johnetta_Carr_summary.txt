### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling" tells the story of Janeta Carr, wrongfully convicted in Kentucky for the murder of her boyfriend, Michael Adolfe, in 2006.  Despite a solid alibi and maintaining her innocence during a coercive 11-hour interrogation at age 16, Janeta was arrested based on a fabricated confession from a friend pressured by police.  A jailhouse informant later falsely claimed Janeta confessed to her. Facing the threat of the death penalty for her co-defendants, Janeta took an Alford plea, serving four years of a 20-year sentence.  Years later, the Kentucky Innocence Project took her case, uncovering withheld DNA evidence and alternative suspects.  Governor Bevin pardoned Janeta in 2019 based on actual innocence, but she remains ineligible for DNA testing and compensation due to Kentucky law. Janeta now advocates for criminal justice reform and supports organizations aiding the wrongfully convicted.
