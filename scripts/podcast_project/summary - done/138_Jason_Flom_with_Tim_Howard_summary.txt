### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom features the story of Tim Howard, wrongfully convicted in 1997 for the murders of Brian and Shannon Day and the attempted murder of their infant son. Despite a lack of physical evidence and obvious alternative suspects linked to Brian Day's drug debts and potential snitching, authorities focused on Tim Howard, Brian's close friend.  The prosecution presented a flimsy motive based on a fabricated "meth-induced rage" and misrepresented facts about Shannon Day's pregnancy.  Key evidence, including exculpatory DNA lab notes indicating potential contamination, was withheld from the defense (Brady violation).  Although Tim Howard's conviction was eventually overturned and he was paroled after 20 years, he was not fully exonerated and still carries the weight of this injustice. The episode also highlights the work of Proclaim Justice in advocating for the wrongfully convicted and the systemic issues contributing to such cases, including potential racial bias and prosecutorial misconduct.
