### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction delves into the harrowing case of the Groveland Four, four young black men falsely accused of raping a white woman in 1949 Groveland, Florida.  The podcast features an interview with Gilbert King, author of "Devil in the Grove," who details the systemic racism, corruption, and violence that led to the wrongful convictions.  The narrative exposes the brutality of Sheriff Willis McCall, the influence of the KKK, and the deeply flawed justice system of the Jim Crow South.  Despite confessions obtained through torture and fabricated evidence, the NAACP and Thurgood Marshall fought for appeals.  The story includes the murder of one defendant by the sheriff during a retrial transport, and the long struggle for exoneration that finally came decades later, highlighting the devastating impact of racial injustice and the fight for truth and accountability.
