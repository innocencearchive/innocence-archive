### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Ivory Dorsey, who spent over a decade imprisoned for a 1994 robbery and murder in Houston, Texas, a crime he did not commit.  The episode details the flawed investigation, including inconsistent eyewitness testimony from Clifford Tyler after 12 years, and the recanted testimony of Kelton Smith. Despite evidence of innocence, including phone records and the eventual confession of the actual perpetrator, Xavier Stevenson, in a 2012 affidavit, Dorsey was convicted due to ineffective counsel.  Princeton University's "Making an Exonary Class," led by Kennedy Mattis, played a crucial role in advocating for Dorsey's case, ultimately contributing to his parole in 2023.  Dorsey, while paroled, is still fighting for full exoneration and highlights the systemic injustices and challenges faced by wrongfully convicted individuals, including expensive family communication during incarceration and restricted parole conditions.  The episode underscores the devastating impact of wrongful convictions and the ongoing battle for justice and systemic reform.
