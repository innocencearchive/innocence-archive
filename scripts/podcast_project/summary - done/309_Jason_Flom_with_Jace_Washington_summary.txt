### Basic Podcast Information

- **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction delves into the case of Jason Washington, wrongfully convicted in St. Tammany Parish, Louisiana, for a 2007 robbery and murder.  The podcast highlights the deeply flawed criminal justice system in St. Tammany Parish, marked by prosecutorial misconduct, ineffective counsel, and racist practices. Jason, along with Grant Gathers, was convicted based on inconsistent and evolving testimonies, primarily from an accomplice, Edrick Cooper, despite lacking any credible evidence and possessing strong alibis.  The episode exposes the systemic issues of non-unanimous juries, coerced confessions, and the perverse incentives within the Louisiana justice system that contributed to Jason's wrongful conviction and continue to affect many others.  Despite recantations from key witnesses and clear inconsistencies in the prosecution's narrative, Jason remains incarcerated, fighting for exoneration. The podcast calls for action to support Jason and address the broader injustices within the criminal justice system.
