### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Blaze Labato, recently exonerated after serving over 16 years for a murder she did not commit. The podcast details the gruesome murder of Duran Bailey in 2001 and how police fixated on Labato based on a misinterpreted self-defense incident she reported.  Despite overwhelming evidence of a botched investigation, including untested DNA, ignored leads, and reliance on a jailhouse snitch, Labato was wrongly convicted in two trials.  The episode highlights systemic issues like tunnel vision in law enforcement, ineffective defense counsel in the second trial, and the devastating impact of wrongful convictions.  Ultimately, the Innocence Project intervened, presenting expert testimony on time of death which proved Labato's alibi, leading to her exoneration.  The podcast emphasizes the crucial role of citizen activism, exemplified by Labato’s supporter Michelle Ravel, and calls for systemic reform in the justice system.
