### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction, hosted by Gilbert King, details the wrongful conviction of Juan Melendez for the murder of Delbert Baker in 1983.  Melendez, a fruit picker with limited English, was convicted based on coerced and incentivized testimony from unreliable informants, despite an alibi and conflicting witness statements.  The prosecutor, Hardy Pickard, is highlighted for potential misconduct, including suppressing exculpatory evidence and protecting a dubious star witness.  Melendez spent 17 years on death row before being exonerated when tapes surfaced revealing a confession from another suspect. The episode underscores systemic issues within the justice system, including prosecutorial overreach, ineffective defense, and the devastating impact of wrongful convictions, particularly in death penalty cases. Juan Melendez and his attorney Linda McDermott discuss the case and advocate against the death penalty, emphasizing the risk of executing innocent individuals.
