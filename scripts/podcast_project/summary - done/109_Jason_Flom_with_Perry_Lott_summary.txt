### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Perry Lot, who spent 31 years in prison for a rape and robbery in Ada, Oklahoma, a town known for wrongful convictions.  His case highlights the dangers of eyewitness misidentification, especially cross-racial, and suggestive police procedures.  Despite having a solid alibi, Lot was convicted based on the victim's identification in a flawed lineup where police artificially created gold teeth for other participants to mimic Lot's.  DNA evidence eventually exonerated him, and the victim recanted her testimony, admitting she identified Lot on a hunch and not based on his voice.  However, due to the challenging legal environment in Ada, Lot accepted a probation deal instead of pursuing full exoneration.  The episode also discusses the systemic issues contributing to wrongful convictions and the work of the Innocence Project and advocates like Eric Cullen in fighting for justice.
