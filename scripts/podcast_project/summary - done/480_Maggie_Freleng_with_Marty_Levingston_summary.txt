### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling tells the story of Marty Leviston, who was wrongfully convicted of murder and spent 15 years in prison.  Marty recounts his troubled youth and the circumstances leading to his arrest for a crime he did not commit in 2007.  Despite key witnesses recanting their initial statements and lack of physical evidence, Marty was convicted in 2009 based largely on the testimony of a jailhouse informant.  After years of appeals and the intervention of the Ohio Innocence Project, Marty was offered a "dark plea" – pleading guilty to involuntary manslaughter in exchange for immediate release.  Marty accepted the plea in 2023, regaining his freedom but without formal exoneration. The episode highlights the issues of wrongful convictions, the pressure to solve crimes, the use of jailhouse informants, and the controversial practice of dark pleas, featuring commentary from Justice Michael Donnelly on the coercive nature of these plea deals.
