### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction tells the story of Jerome Curry, who was wrongfully convicted for multiple shootings in New York City in 1996, including the murder of Reginald Myers.  The episode highlights the coercive interrogation tactics used by Detective De Leon to obtain a false confession from Curry, despite contradictory physical evidence and alibi.  Key issues include fabricated witness statements, unreliable eyewitness identification by Reginald Frazier, and ineffective defense counsel.  The podcast emphasizes the systemic problems within the NYPD during the crack epidemic era, where clearing cases was prioritized over justice.  New evidence, including expert forensic analysis and a recantation from a co-defendant, strongly suggests Curry's innocence.  His attorney, Justin Bonus, is currently working on a 440 motion seeking to overturn Curry's conviction, focusing on the murder case of Reginald Myers.  Jerome Curry spent almost 30 years in prison for these wrongful convictions and continues to fight for exoneration.

    -   Jerome Curry's confession (later recanted as coerced)

    -   Eyewitness identification by Reginald Frazier.

    -   Inconsistencies and questionable credibility of Reginald Frazier (changing descriptions, potential incentives, rape charge).
-   **Actual Perpetrator Details**:
    -   Miguel Enriquez and Pedro Guzman admitted to being involved in the robbery of Reginald Myers. Enriquez indicated that "Marcoos" nicknamed "Boas" (Marker Laboa) was the one who fired the fatal shot.

    -   "Clear the books" mentality in policing during the crack epidemic era, leading to rushed and flawed investigations.
    -   Ineffective assistance of counsel and lack of resources for indigent defendants.
    -   Prosecutorial suppression of exculpatory evidence (failure to disclose Frazier's inconsistent descriptions).
    -   Flawed eyewitness identification procedures and suggestive lineups.
    -   Racial bias and assumptions in law enforcement and the justice system.
-   **Policy Recommendations/Calls to Action**:
    -   Urgent re-examination of Jerome Curry's case by the Manhattan DA's office.
    -   Investigation into Detective B.A. De Leon's past cases and practices due to pattern of misconduct.
    -   Review and reform of police interrogation techniques to prevent false confessions.
    -   Improvement of eyewitness identification procedures to ensure fairness and accuracy.
    -   Increased accountability for police and prosecutors in wrongful conviction cases.
