### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Thomas Haynesworth, who was wrongfully convicted of multiple sexual assaults in Richmond, Virginia, in 1984 based on misidentification by five victims. Despite the assaults continuing while he was incarcerated and evidence suggesting another perpetrator, Leon Davis, Haynesworth served 27 years in prison. The episode details the failures of eyewitness identification, particularly cross-racial identification, and the systemic issues within the justice system.  With the help of the Mid-Atlantic Innocence Project and DNA evidence from saved samples by a forensic analyst, Haynesworth was finally exonerated in 2011. The episode emphasizes the profound impact of wrongful convictions on individuals and the urgent need for criminal justice reform to prevent similar tragedies.
