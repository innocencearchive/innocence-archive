The episode highlights the systemic failures within the justice system and emphasizes the importance of humanity and empathy in addressing wrongful convictions and achieving meaningful reform.  Ronald now works with Just Leadership USA, an organization led by Glenn Martin, aiming to reduce incarceration and empower formerly incarcerated individuals.
