### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction details the case of Elwood Jones, wrongfully convicted and sentenced to death in Ohio for the 1994 murder of Rhoda Nathan. The podcast reveals numerous flaws in the prosecution's case, including fabricated evidence like a planted pendant and junk science regarding a hand infection.  It highlights withheld Brady material, including witness statements pointing to other suspects and evidence contradicting the state's narrative.  Despite compelling evidence of innocence and prosecutorial misconduct by Mark Peepmeyer and Seth Teager, Elwood Jones spent years on death row.  The episode culminates in the granting of a new trial in 2022 and Elwood's release, but emphasizes the ongoing fight for full exoneration and accountability for the prosecutors involved. The systemic issues of tunnel vision, confirmation bias, and lack of accountability within the Hamilton County justice system are strongly emphasized. Listeners are urged to contact the Hamilton County prosecutor to demand justice for Elwood Jones.
