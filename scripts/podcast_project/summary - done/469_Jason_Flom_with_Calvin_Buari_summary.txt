### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom features Calvin Boari and journalist Steve Fishman discussing Boari's wrongful conviction for a 1992 double homicide in the Bronx. Boari, a former drug dealer, was targeted by police and prosecutors who were determined to convict him, even with flimsy evidence and incentivized witnesses. The episode details the systemic issues that contributed to his wrongful conviction, including prosecutorial misconduct, trial by ambush tactics, and the use of jailhouse informants.  Boari spent over two decades in prison, maintaining his innocence and fighting for exoneration.  The conversation highlights his resilience, his eventual exoneration after new witnesses came forward, and his efforts to rebuild his life and advocate for justice reform upon release.  The episode underscores the devastating impact of wrongful convictions and the urgent need for systemic change within the criminal justice system.

-   **Systemic Issues Identified**:
    -   Prosecutorial Misconduct: Brady violations, withholding evidence, overzealous prosecution, and lack of accountability for misconduct.
    -   Trial by Ambush: Withholding witness information from the defense, creating an unfair disadvantage.
    -   Incentivized Witnesses/Jailhouse Informants: Use of unreliable testimony from individuals seeking personal gain, contributing to wrongful convictions.
    -   Prosecutorial Immunity: Lack of accountability for prosecutors, allowing misconduct to go unpunished.
    -   Racial Bias/Prejudice: Mention of "black magic" propaganda suggests racial prejudice may have played a role in the prosecution's zeal.

-   **Policy Recommendations/Calls to Action**:
    -   Eliminate or reform prosecutorial immunity to hold prosecutors accountable for misconduct.
    -   Mandate open case files with full disclosure to defense attorneys to prevent "trial by ambush".
    -   Reform the use of incentivized witnesses and jailhouse informants, implementing stricter rules and scrutiny.
    -   Justice Reform: General call for justice reform to prevent future wrongful convictions.
    -   Support organizations like the Innocence Project.
