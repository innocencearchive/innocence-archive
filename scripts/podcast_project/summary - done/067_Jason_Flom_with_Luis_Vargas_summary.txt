    -   Flawed Eyewitness Identification Procedures: Use of six-pack photo arrays and suggestive procedures.

    -   Ineffective Assistance of Counsel: Inadequate legal representation.
    -   Prison Hierarchy and Violence: Inmates controlling aspects of prison environment and targeting inmates based on crime type.
