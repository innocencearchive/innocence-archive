### Basic Podcast Information

The episode highlights the story of Stephanie Spurgeon, wrongfully convicted of manslaughter based on SBS diagnosis and later exonerated.  The podcast emphasizes the lack of scientific certainty in SBS diagnosis, the potential for bias among medical experts, and the devastating consequences of wrongful accusations on families. It calls for rigorous medical evaluations, criticizes the over-reliance on subjective expert opinions, and underscores the urgent need to prevent future wrongful convictions based on junk science in child abuse cases.

-   **Systemic Issues Identified**:
    -   Over-reliance on subjective medical expert testimony.
    -   "Tunnel vision" and confirmation bias in child abuse pediatrics, potentially leading to misdiagnosis.
    -   Lack of rigorous and comprehensive medical testing in all cases of infant brain injury.
    -   Pressure on jurors and legal professionals to "err on the side of the child," which can lead to wrongful convictions when science is uncertain.
    -   Role effects bias when experts are closely aligned with law enforcement.
