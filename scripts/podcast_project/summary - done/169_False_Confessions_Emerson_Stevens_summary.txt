### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction, False Confessions, tells the story of Emerson Stevens, a fisherman wrongfully convicted of murdering Mary Harding in Lancaster County, Virginia in 1985. Despite no confession, Emerson was convicted based on a false inculpatory statement coerced by Detective David Riley, flawed eyewitness testimony, and debunked hair microscopy evidence. The podcast details the weak prosecution case, highlighting inconsistencies like the body being found upstream from where prosecutors claimed it was dumped, and the manipulation of evidence by Detective Riley.  Beverly Monroe, another victim of Detective Riley's tactics, became a key advocate for Emerson, enlisting lawyers to help his case.  While Emerson was paroled after 31 years, he is still fighting for full exoneration and a pardon from the Governor of Virginia to clear his name completely.
