### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features the harrowing story of Eric Brown, wrongfully convicted in Louisiana at age 16 for a murder he did not commit.  The podcast highlights the systemic issues within the Louisiana justice system, including non-unanimous jury verdicts and the treatment of juveniles.  Eric recounts his arrest based on flimsy witness testimony and circumstantial evidence, his unfair trial with an ineffective defense attorney, and the subsequent 25 years he spent incarcerated in Angola prison. Despite facing immense challenges and the loss of his brother while imprisoned, Eric dedicated himself to legal study, eventually contributing to the legal fight against juvenile life sentences.  The episode details his journey through the legal system, the support he received from Brian Stevenson and the Innocence Project, and his eventual release on parole.  Eric emphasizes the plague of wrongful convictions and the need for systemic reform, concluding with a message of resilience and hope for his fellow exonerees.
