### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling" tells the story of Brandon Woodruff, who was wrongly convicted of murdering his parents in Texas in 2005.  Despite a lack of concrete evidence, Brandon was convicted based largely on speculation related to his sexuality and a weak circumstantial case built by investigators who overlooked crucial details and mishandled the crime scene. The podcast highlights the flawed investigation, the prosecution's reliance on prejudice, and the devastating impact of wrongful conviction on Brandon and his family, especially his grandmother Bonnie who steadfastly supports him.  It also introduces the work of the Innocence Project of Texas, led by Allison Clayton, who is now working on Brandon's case, seeking DNA testing and highlighting timeline inconsistencies that undermine the prosecution's theory. The episode underscores systemic issues within the justice system and calls for reform to prevent future wrongful convictions.
