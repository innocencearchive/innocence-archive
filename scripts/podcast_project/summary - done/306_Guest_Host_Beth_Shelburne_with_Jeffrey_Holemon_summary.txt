### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction, guest-hosted by investigative reporter Beth Shelburne, tells the story of Jeffrey Holman's wrongful conviction for rape in Tuscaloosa, Alabama in 1987.  Despite maintaining his innocence and the lack of physical evidence, Holman was convicted based on eyewitness identification and sentenced to life in prison.  The podcast details his difficult childhood, his admission to other crimes like burglary and check forgery which may have prejudiced the jury, and the deficiencies of his court-appointed lawyer. Holman spent 12 years in prison before DNA evidence from a rediscovered rape kit exonerated him in 1998.  The episode explores the emotional toll of wrongful conviction and Holman's eventual work in ministry to help others re-entering society after incarceration, highlighting his resilience and forgiveness.
