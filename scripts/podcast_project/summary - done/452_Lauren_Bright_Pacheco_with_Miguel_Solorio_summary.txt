### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Miguel Solorio and his wife Sylvia, detailing Miguel's wrongful conviction for a 1998 drive-by shooting and his nearly 25-year imprisonment.  Despite having an alibi supported by Sylvia, Miguel was convicted based on shaky eyewitness identification and flawed police investigation, particularly by Detective Macaldary.  Sylvia recounts her unwavering support, including becoming a private investigator to fight for Miguel's freedom. The podcast highlights the devastating impact of wrongful convictions on individuals and their loved ones, showcasing Sylvia's relentless dedication and the eventual exoneration of Miguel with the help of the Northern California Innocence Project and attorney Ellen Eggers. The story underscores systemic issues within the justice system and the importance of fighting for justice. Miguel and Sylvia's enduring love story provides a powerful narrative of hope and resilience in the face of injustice.
