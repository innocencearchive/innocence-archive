Harold recounts being falsely accused by his 15-year-old stepdaughter, spurred by her father, after a minor disciplinary incident. Despite inconsistencies in the accusations and suppressed evidence, Harold was convicted in what his lawyer described as a "horribly biased corrupt trial." Harold served approximately two years in a medium-security prison before accepting an alpha plea for release, allowing him to maintain innocence. He reflects on the psychological impact of incarceration, the failures of the prison system, and his commitment to prison reform and advocacy for the wrongfully convicted. Despite the ordeal, Harold expresses forgiveness and a desire to promote love and unity.
