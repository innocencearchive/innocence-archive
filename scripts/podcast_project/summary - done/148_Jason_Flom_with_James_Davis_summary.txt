Davis and his lawyer, Elizabeth Felber from the Legal Aid Society, recount the events leading to his conviction, highlighting the flawed police investigation, unreliable eyewitness identification, and the prosecution's reliance on a key witness who was a dangerous drug dealer. Despite compelling alibi evidence and inconsistencies in witness testimonies, Davis was convicted in a second trial after a hung jury in the first.  The podcast exposes systemic issues within the justice system, including tunnel vision in investigations, ineffective defense counsel, and prosecutorial misconduct through Brady violations.  It concludes with Davis's ongoing fight for exoneration and appeals to listeners for support.
