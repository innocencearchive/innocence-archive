### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: In this episode of Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom, host Jason Flom interviews Calvin Johnson, a man wrongfully convicted of rape and burglary in 1983. Calvin recounts his idyllic childhood, his brief foray into petty crime which led to a burglary conviction, and then his shocking arrest and conviction for a rape he did not commit.  Despite flawed evidence, including serology and eyewitness identification, and exculpatory evidence being presented, Calvin was sentenced to double life terms. He describes the dehumanizing prison conditions and his unwavering belief in his innocence.  Years later, DNA technology became available, offering a chance for exoneration.  The Innocence Project took his case, and after years of fighting, DNA testing proved his innocence, leading to his release in 1999. Calvin now serves on the board of the Innocence Project and shares his story to advocate for justice and reform.
