### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling tells the story of Marnie Yang, wrongfully convicted for the 2007 murder of Ronnie Ryder and her unborn child. Despite a lack of forensic evidence, Marnie was convicted based on circumstantial evidence including a coerced false confession, questionable address labels, and misidentified rental car footage. The prosecution painted Marnie as a jealous girlfriend of Ronnie's boyfriend, NFL star Sean Gale.  Marnie's children were also heavily interrogated and manipulated.  Post-conviction efforts by Jed Stone and his team have uncovered significant flaws in the original investigation and trial, pointing to Marnie's innocence and a pattern of wrongful convictions in Lake County, Illinois. Marnie has served 13 years of a life sentence and maintains her innocence, hoping for justice and reunification with her family.
