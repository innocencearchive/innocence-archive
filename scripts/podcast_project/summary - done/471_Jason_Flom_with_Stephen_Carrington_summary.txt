### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom features the story of Stefan Carrington, who was wrongfully convicted in 1995 for a lumberyard robbery and murder and served 23 years in prison. The conviction was based on a questionable eyewitness identification and the detective's "gut feeling," despite a solid alibi and no physical evidence linking Stefan to the crime.  His brother was involved, but Stefan became the target due to a prior criminal record and being on parole.  The episode details the flawed trial, ineffective counsel, and the eventual fight for exoneration.  After years of struggle, the King’s County Conviction Review Unit reviewed his case, leading to the vacating of his conviction and dismissal of all charges, finally proving Stefan's innocence.  This episode highlights the devastating impact of wrongful convictions and the long fight for justice.
