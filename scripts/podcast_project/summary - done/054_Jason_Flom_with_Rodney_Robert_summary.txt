### Basic Podcast Information

- **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom" features Rodney Roberts, who recounts his harrowing 17-year ordeal of wrongful imprisonment. Rodney details his initial wrongful conviction as a youth for conspiracy, followed by a second wrongful conviction for rape and kidnapping he did not commit.  Advised by his public defender and fearing a life sentence, he pled guilty to kidnapping, receiving a seven-year sentence, only to be civilly committed upon release for an additional decade based on the false premise of being a sexual predator.  The podcast explores the systemic failures, including ineffective counsel, prosecutorial misconduct, and the coercive nature of plea bargains that contributed to his unjust incarceration.  Rodney emphasizes the devastating impact of wrongful conviction, the importance of family support, and his eventual exoneration thanks to DNA evidence and dedicated legal help.  He now runs the Rodney Roberts Foundation to assist other exonerees.

    - Prosecutorial misconduct (misrepresenting evidence, withholding evidence, "losing" evidence).
    - Flaws in eyewitness identification.
    - Misuse of civil commitment laws to indefinitely detain individuals without criminal conviction.
    - Lack of support systems for exonerees upon release.
