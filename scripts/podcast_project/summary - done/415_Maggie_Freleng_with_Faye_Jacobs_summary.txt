### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling features the story of Laquanda Fay Jacobs, who was wrongfully convicted of the 1992 murder of Kevin Gaddy in Little Rock, Arkansas.  Despite a lack of forensic evidence and eyewitness descriptions not matching Fay, she was convicted at 16 and sentenced to life without parole. The podcast details the events leading to her arrest, the flawed trial, and the systemic issues contributing to her wrongful conviction, including potential racial bias and ineffective counsel. Fay was released in 2018 after accepting a time-served offer due to changes in juvenile sentencing laws, but remains a convicted felon.  She is currently seeking clemency to achieve full exoneration and advocates for others who are wrongfully incarcerated.  The episode also touches on the difficult choice exonerees face when offered release deals that prevent them from fully fighting for their innocence.
