### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction tells the harrowing story of Ray Krohn, the 100th death row exoneree in the US.  Convicted in Arizona based solely on bite mark analysis, a discredited forensic science, Krohn spent over ten years in prison, including time on death row, for a murder he did not commit.  Despite DNA evidence excluding him and identifying the actual perpetrator, Kenneth Phillips, Krohn was initially re-convicted in a second trial.  The podcast details the egregious prosecutorial misconduct, the failures of his initial defense, and the unwavering support of his family.  Ultimately, post-conviction DNA testing led to his exoneration and release.  Krohn now works with Witness to Innocence, advocating against the death penalty and for justice system reform.  His story highlights the dangers of junk science, the flaws in the justice system, and the resilience of the human spirit.

-   **Cross-Reference**: Appears in exoneree database (as the 100th death row exoneree).

-   **Key Evidence at Trial (Prosecution)**:
    -   Bite mark analysis: Expert Ray Rosen testified that Krohn's teeth uniquely matched bite marks on the victim's neck and breast, claiming it was definitive evidence.

-   **Trial Defender Notes**:
    -   First trial: Public defender was dismissive and unhelpful. Court-appointed lawyer lacked resources and experience, failed to adequately challenge bite mark evidence initially and was denied a continuance to prepare for last-minute evidence.

-   **Key Evidence for Exoneration/Change in Legal Status**:
    -   Salivary DNA testing: Post-conviction DNA testing on saliva from the bite marks excluded Ray Krohn and the victim and matched Kenneth Phillips.
    -   Other evidence at second sentencing hearing (presented by defense attorney Chris Plord): Footprints at the scene didn't match Krohn's shoe size, palm prints and hair found at the scene didn't match Krohn.
-   **Actual Perpetrator Details**:
    -   Name: Kenneth Phillips
