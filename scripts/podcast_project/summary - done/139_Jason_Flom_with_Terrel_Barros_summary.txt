### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Jason Flom discusses the case of Terrell Barros, who was wrongfully convicted for a shooting in Providence, Rhode Island in 2012. Despite Stefan Bodden confessing to the shooting at the scene and being caught with the gun, Terrell was charged and convicted of murder. Key issues in the case include a questionable dying declaration attributed to the deceased victim, a key eyewitness who drastically changed his testimony shortly before trial after being offered a deal, and DNA evidence that excluded Terrell from ever handling the weapon. The podcast emphasizes the systemic failures within the justice system and the profound, tragic impact on Terrell and his family. New evidence, including gunshot residue analysis and corroborating witness testimonies, is now being presented to push for a new trial and Terrell's exoneration. The episode features interviews with Terrell from prison and Nurgina Hendi, a Georgetown student investigating his case, underscoring the ongoing struggle for justice and the urgent need for public support.
