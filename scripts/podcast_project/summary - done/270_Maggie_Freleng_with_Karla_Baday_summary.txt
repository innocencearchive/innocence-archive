### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling" tells the story of Carla Bidet, a Honduran immigrant and lesbian wrongfully convicted of child sexual abuse in California. In 2002, Carla was accused by her ex-girlfriend, Maria Medina, and despite inconsistent testimonies and lack of credible evidence, she was convicted and sentenced to 45 years to life.  Prejudice against her sexual orientation and immigration status, combined with ineffective legal counsel, contributed to the wrongful conviction.  Carla served 17 years before the Northern California Innocence Project took her case.  They uncovered false medical evidence and ineffective assistance of counsel, leading to the reversal of her conviction.  To secure her release from prison and avoid retrial, Carla accepted a plea deal for false imprisonment, despite her innocence of all charges.  The episode highlights systemic issues of prejudice, inadequate defense, and the immense personal impact of wrongful conviction.
