### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling tells the story of Alan Andre Khazi, wrongfully convicted of murder in 1992 and sentenced to 50 years. Andre was arrested and coerced into signing a false confession after a seven-hour interrogation by the Austin Police Department. Key evidence against him was this confession and eyewitness accounts of his car near the crime scene. Despite no other evidence and the actual perpetrator remaining at large, Andre served over 31 years in prison. The Innocence Project of Texas took his case, uncovering a pattern of coercive interrogation tactics by the police department, particularly by detective Hector Polanco.  Andre was eventually released on parole in 2022, highlighting systemic issues within the justice system and the devastating impact of false confessions.
