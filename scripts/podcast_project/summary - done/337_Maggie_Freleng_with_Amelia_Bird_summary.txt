### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling tells the tragic story of Amelia Bird, who was wrongfully convicted in connection to the shooting of her parents and the death of her mother.  Amelia endured severe childhood abuse, leading to substance abuse and unhealthy relationships. At 16, after her mother was killed and her father injured, Amelia was pressured into a plea deal by her public defender, despite being a minor and not eligible for the death penalty.  She received two life sentences for a crime she maintains she did not commit.  The podcast highlights systemic failures, including ineffective counsel and the vulnerability of abused women in the justice system.  The Willow Project, a wrongful conviction project for women, is now working to advocate for Amelia's case, seeking clemency and parole.  Despite her wrongful conviction, Amelia has shown remarkable resilience, earning her GED in prison and becoming a vital part of the CHAMP Assistance Dog Program.
