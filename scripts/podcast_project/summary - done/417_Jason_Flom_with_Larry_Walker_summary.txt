### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features the story of Larry Walker, who was wrongfully convicted of murder in Philadelphia and served 38 years in prison before being released.  The host, Jason Flom, interviews Larry Walker and Paul Castolero, Legal Director of Centurion Ministries, the organization that helped exonerate Larry.  The episode details the flawed investigation and trial, including unreliable eyewitness identification, ineffective defense counsel, and prosecutorial misconduct, specifically the suppression of evidence regarding a key witness.  Despite a weak case acknowledged even by the trial prosecutor, Larry was convicted based on shaky witness testimony.  Centurion Ministries reinvestigated the case, uncovering suppressed evidence and highlighting systemic issues within the justice system that contribute to wrongful convictions.  Due to procedural hurdles and lack of full exoneration options, Larry ultimately had to take a plea to a reduced charge to gain his freedom, highlighting the ongoing challenges faced by wrongfully convicted individuals even when new evidence surfaces. The episode emphasizes the devastating impact of wrongful convictions on individuals and families, and the urgent need for systemic reform.
