### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction: Behind Bars features John Adrian Velasquez, an inmate at Sing Sing Correctional Facility, who maintains his innocence in the murder of a retired police officer.  Recorded inside Sing Sing, the episode details Velasquez's background, his wrongful conviction based on a flawed lineup and pressured witness testimony, and the lack of physical evidence in his case.  Despite a denied motion for a new trial, Velasquez has dedicated his time in prison to positive initiatives like education programs and reentry preparation.  He describes the devastating impact of his wrongful conviction on his family, including his children and mother.  The episode underscores systemic issues in the justice system, particularly concerning eyewitness identification and the immense pressure on individuals in marginalized communities, and calls for support for Velasquez's exoneration.  Listeners are encouraged to visit freejohnadriavolaskets.org to learn more and offer support.
