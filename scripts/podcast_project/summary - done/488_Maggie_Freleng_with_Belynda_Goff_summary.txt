### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of "Wrongful Conviction with Maggie Freeling" tells the tragic story of Belinda Gough, who was wrongfully convicted of the first-degree murder of her husband, Stephen Gough, in 1996.  The podcast details Belinda's early life, her marriage to Stephen, and the events leading up to his death.  Despite a lack of physical evidence, Belinda was convicted based on circumstantial evidence, reliance on gender stereotypes, and a flawed police investigation that prematurely focused on her as the suspect.  She served over 22 years in prison before the Innocence Project took her case, revealing fabricated testimony and overlooked evidence pointing to alternative suspects involved in Stephen's criminal activities.  Belinda was released in 2019 after a judge acknowledged she would likely be acquitted if tried today, but she was resentenced to time served, leaving her still a convicted felon despite her innocence.  The episode underscores the devastating impact of wrongful convictions on individuals and their families, and the systemic issues within the justice system that contribute to such tragedies.
