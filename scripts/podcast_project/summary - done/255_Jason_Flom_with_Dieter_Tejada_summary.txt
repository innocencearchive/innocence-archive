### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Dieter Tejada, who shares his story of being wrongfully convicted of assault.  Dieter recounts a physical altercation that started when he was threatened and attacked with a baseball bat by Nick Schwartz. Despite acting in self-defense, Dieter and his friends faced charges due to the influence of Schwartz's family and an ambitious prosecutor.  He was pressured into taking a plea deal to avoid a potentially severe sentence, even though he was innocent.  Dieter served nine months in prison and highlights the systemic issues within the justice system, including prosecutorial misconduct, ineffective counsel, and the pressure to accept plea deals.  The episode also touches on the long-term consequences of wrongful convictions and Dieter's journey towards becoming a lawyer and advocate for justice reform through the National Justice Impact Bar Association and the Justice Impact Alliance.  The conversation underscores how easily individuals, even from seemingly normal backgrounds, can be ensnared by the flaws of the legal system.
