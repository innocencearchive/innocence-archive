-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Derek Hamilton, who recounts his two wrongful convictions and eventual exoneration.  Hamilton describes his first wrongful conviction for murder based on coerced grand jury testimony, which was overturned but led to a coerced plea deal to secure release.  He was then wrongfully convicted a second time for murder, highlighting the misconduct of detective Louis Garcella, known for fabricating evidence and coercing witnesses.  During his second incarceration, Hamilton became a jailhouse lawyer, advocating for himself and others. He details forming an "Actual Innocence" team in prison that utilized media and legal strategies to raise awareness and fight for justice.  Hamilton's case eventually contributed to the scrutiny of Garcella's cases and the exoneration of others.  The episode underscores systemic issues in the criminal justice system, including eyewitness misidentification and prosecutorial misconduct, and advocates for reforms.
