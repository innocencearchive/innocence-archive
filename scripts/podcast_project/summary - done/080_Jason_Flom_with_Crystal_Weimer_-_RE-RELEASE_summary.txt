### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Crystal Weimer, an exoneree from Western Pennsylvania, and her lawyer Nilam Sangvi from the Pennsylvania Innocence Project. Crystal was wrongfully convicted of murder based on bite mark evidence, later deemed junk science, and false testimony from incentivized jailhouse informants.  The episode details the numerous factors contributing to her wrongful conviction, including police and prosecutorial misconduct, and a false confession from a codefendant. Crystal served 12 years in prison before being exonerated with the help of the Pennsylvania Innocence Project and Jones Day law firm.  The discussion highlights the systemic issues within the justice system, particularly the use of unreliable evidence and the pressures leading to wrongful convictions.  The episode also emphasizes the personal impact of wrongful conviction, focusing on Crystal's experience as a single mother separated from her children.  The importance of organizations like the Innocence Project and the need for criminal justice reform, such as mandatory videotaping of interrogations, are underscored.
