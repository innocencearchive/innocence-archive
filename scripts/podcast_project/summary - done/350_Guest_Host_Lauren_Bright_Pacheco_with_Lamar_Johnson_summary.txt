### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction, hosted by Lauren Bright-Pacheco, tells the harrowing story of Lamar Johnson's wrongful conviction for a 1994 murder in St. Louis.  Despite maintaining his innocence and having a solid alibi, Johnson was convicted based on unreliable eyewitness testimony, incentivized jailhouse informant testimony, and questionable police tactics by Detective Joseph Nickerson.  The actual perpetrators confessed to the crime in letters and affidavits, but this evidence was initially ignored by the courts.  After years of legal battles and the passage of new Missouri legislation prompted by his case ("the Johnson fix"), Johnson was finally exonerated in 2023 after serving 28 years in prison.  The episode highlights systemic failures in the justice system, including prosecutorial misconduct, ineffective defense, and the challenges faced by wrongfully convicted individuals even with evidence of innocence.  Johnson's case ultimately led to positive change in Missouri law, offering hope for future exonerations.
