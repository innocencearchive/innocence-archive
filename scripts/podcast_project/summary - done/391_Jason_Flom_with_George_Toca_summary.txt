### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction tells the harrowing story of George Toca, wrongfully convicted for the murder of his best friend, Eric Batiste, in 1984.  Despite lacking a description match from eyewitnesses and having an alibi, George was convicted based on a police officer's hunch and flawed eyewitness identification in a photo array.  He was sentenced to life without parole and spent over 30 years in Angola Penitentiary.  The episode highlights the systemic issues of unreliable eyewitness testimony, ineffective defense counsel, and a rush to judgment in the justice system.  The Innocence Project of New Orleans (IPNO) took on his case, facing numerous obstacles including lost evidence due to Hurricane Katrina and an uncooperative judge.  George was eventually released in 2015 through a plea deal, but it wasn't until 2022, with new legislation and a reform-minded prosecutor, that he was fully exonerated, finally achieving true freedom and justice.
