### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction, False Confessions, tells the story of Matt Livers and Nick Sampson, who were wrongfully accused of murdering Wayne and Sherman Stoke in Murdoch, Nebraska, on Easter Sunday 2006.  Matt Livers, despite having intellectual disabilities, was subjected to a coercive 11-hour interrogation where he falsely confessed to the crime after being threatened with the death penalty and lied to about polygraph results.  Although there was no physical evidence linking them to the crime and a planted blood sample was later discovered, Matt and Nick were initially charged.  Good police work tracing a ring found at the crime scene eventually led to the actual perpetrators, Greg Fester and Jessica Reed, who confessed and were convicted. Matt Livers was exonerated after spending months in jail, highlighting the dangers of false confessions and the importance of ethical police investigation. The case also exposes issues with "hero" police officers who cut corners and fabricate evidence.
