### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Bobbi Jean Johnson, wrongfully convicted in 1977 for the murder of Arthur Sampson, and Kat Forrester from the Innocence Project of New Orleans. Bobbi Jean recounts her horrific interrogation, where she was tortured into a false confession for a crime she did not commit.  Despite a co-defendant admitting to planting evidence, and inconsistencies in her confession compared to the crime scene, Bobbi Jean was convicted due to ineffective counsel who was asleep during the trial and made no opening or closing statements.  She served 41 years before being released in 2018 after the Innocence Project of New Orleans and Promise of Justice Initiative intervened.  Tragically, Bobbi Jean passed away less than 18 months after her release, highlighting the lasting damage of wrongful conviction. The episode underscores the systemic issues of police misconduct, false confessions, and inadequate legal representation within the criminal justice system.

    -   Plea deal offered by the DA's office to vacate the first-degree murder conviction in exchange for a guilty plea to manslaughter and armed robbery, resulting in time served and release.
