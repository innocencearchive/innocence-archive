### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Frank Benitez and his mother Betty, sharing their harrowing 34-year ordeal after Frank's wrongful conviction for a double murder in Chicago in 1989.  At 18, Frank was coerced into a false confession after being selected from a photo lineup based on a loose eyewitness description. Despite lacking physical evidence and having an alibi, Frank was sentenced to life without parole. His mother, Betty, tirelessly fought for his release, eventually finding help from the Exoneration Project at the University of Chicago Law School.  New witness statements and the exposure of the detectives' history of coercing false confessions led to Frank's release on bond in August 2023 and formal exoneration in December 2023.  The episode highlights the devastating impact of false confessions, systemic failures, and the enduring strength of family love in the face of injustice. Frank and Betty emphasize the importance of never giving up hope.
