### Basic Podcast Information

-   **Executive Summary**: This episode of Wrongful Conviction features Ricky Kidd, who was wrongfully convicted of a double homicide in Kansas City in 1997 and served 23 years in prison before being exonerated in 2019. The episode details the crime, the flawed police investigation that focused on Ricky despite a strong alibi, and the reliance on incentivized and unreliable witness testimony.  Key evidence pointing to other suspects, the Goodspeed family and Marcus Merrill, was ignored or hidden by the prosecution. Ricky recounts the devastating trial, his long years in maximum-security prison, and the relentless fight for his freedom with the help of the Midwest Innocence Project. Ultimately, the recantation of the false eyewitness, Richard Harris, led to Ricky's exoneration, highlighting egregious prosecutorial misconduct and systemic failures in the justice system.
